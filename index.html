    Data collection

    November 6 preprint · ToolDex: synchronized ego views and eight exocentric cameras capture creative tool use, with hand, tool, target-object and deformation annotations. Benchmarks cover tool reasoning, sparse-view 3D/4D reconstruction and robot policy learning. Interpretation: multi-view consistency and interaction labels are distinct deliverables, beyond raw video volume. This research release is not a paid supplier request. Paper

    November 5 catch-up · Meituan-Robotics-0: the authors combine 15,060 hours of curated trajectories across 12 embodiments with 147 million vision-language samples. Their Rollback method pairs replaced policy segments with human corrections, supplying positive and negative examples. Those numbers describe training inputs; they do not establish external purchasing or an open vendor brief. Paper

    November 6 · DobotTrack adds a quadruped person-tracking corpus with 126 episodes and 596,323 mask-annotated frames. Its 77.9% success result belongs to EVT-Bench; it is not a success rate measured across those real-world collection episodes. Paper

    Data annotation & validation

    November 6 · TouchAnnotator: synchronized ego+wrist training views and heterogeneous tactile supervision teach a student that needs only ego video and estimated pose at inference. Six datasets, three embodiments and 860 synchronized glove recordings support the study. Predicted tactile labels remain estimates, not measured pressure ground truth. Paper

    November 6 · HOC2R extracts hand–object contact trajectories from demonstrations. The robot test is small: four feasible trajectories and three successful tasks across six evaluation sequences. Treat this as promising pseudo-labeling evidence, not a production QA guarantee. Paper

    November 6 · Counterfactual grasp audit: eight predictors with high ordinary benchmark accuracy fall near chance on planner-verified obstacle counterfactuals. The result supports testing geometrically meaningful failure cases, rather than relying on aggregate accuracy alone. Paper

    Embedded AI systems

    November 6 · ActPrune uses the previous robot action to prune visual inputs before the encoder; authors report up to 1.61× speedup with limited success degradation in their simulation and four hardware tasks. This is algorithmic inference efficiency, not a new processor launch. Paper

    November 6 · VLA Handover switches from a small on-robot model to a larger server model when failure is predicted. Simulation reports 49–77% fewer server requests, at 2–4 percentage points below server-only success. It does not demonstrate deployed 6G robotics. Paper

    November 6 · CucumberVision at the Edge measures latency, accuracy and energy on Jetson Orin Nano and Raspberry Pi 4 using 50 replayed greenhouse captures of seven specimens. Whole-head INT8 quantization failed in that pipeline; lower wattage did not automatically mean lower energy per fruit. This is a small controlled benchmark, not a field-scale evaluation. Paper

    Radar sensing research also separates gains from additional viewpoints from joints inferred by a stronger estimator. November 6 mmWave study. NVIDIA/Qualcomm/AMD/Hailo discovery yielded no additional verified new chip announcement.

    Robotics & humanoids

    Today · MIT/NYU FLEX: natural-language corrections update a robot planner through adding features, generalizing them, then removing irrelevant ones. “Avoid the laptop” can generalize to avoiding electronics. The reported experiments are three simulated arm tasks, not household deployments; the update process takes about five seconds. November 9 institutional release

    November 6 · On Agentic Manipulation Policies: the AMP1 harness reports 104/108 successful real-world trials across 12 tasks and three robot instances, without task-specific training. This is a significant author-reported comparison, not proof that demonstration-trained systems are obsolete across all latency, safety or industrial requirements. Paper

    November 6 · SUGARv2 synthesizes task references for humanoid loco-manipulation. Its 91% mean success is a simulation result across five tasks; hardware transfer is reported separately. Paper

    World models, dated catch-up: NVIDIA’s November 5 release adds four 16B Cosmos 3 Nano checkpoints: Transfer-Auto, Sim-Auto, Sim-Transfer and Sim-Bimanual. They cover multi-view driving, camera/LiDAR streaming, simulator-controlled rendering and action-conditioned bimanual observations. Sim-Transfer leaves physical state with the simulator; generated realism is not itself a physics guarantee. Bimanual data requires consistent coordinate/action schemas and T+1 observations for T transitions. NVIDIA release

    Ai2’s November 6 ToyBlocks provides a deliberately constrained tabletop world, automatically generated demonstrations and controllable tests of generalization. Its usefulness is experimental control; it does not establish that synthetic data covers arbitrary real environments. Ai2 release
