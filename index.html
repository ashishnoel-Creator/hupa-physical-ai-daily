    Data collection

    • November 2 submission; newly surfaced in today’s listing · AIRoA-0. Authors report handheld-only training with YUBI, wrist-camera operation and 78% mean success across ten tasks versus 27% for their strongest post-trained comparison. Transfer still requires robots fitted with the matching gripper; this is not evidence that arbitrary smartphone footage substitutes for action-grounded capture. Dataset, weights and recipe are announced. Paper
    • November 2 · Soft-finger UMI evidence. A 99.7-hour collection over 53 objects tests gripper matching and camera setup. The paper reports a 13-point gain from adding a top view, even when it is present for only about half the data. This is a concrete capture-design experiment. Paper
    • November 3 · GUILD and PyRUA-DataFarm test two ways to improve collection economics. GUILD feeds estimated policy benefit back into the next collection request. DataFarm combines one calibration episode, simulation and generated multiview video, with independent judges before admitting episodes. Its reported average USD 107 per three-view hour is an experimental cost calculation across five tasks, not a commercial supplier rate or a universal replacement cost. GUILD · DataFarm
    • November 3 · Human-video transfer remains active. Action-Intent Transfer separates intent from scene noise; RoboTryOn translates human demonstrations into target-robot videos and infers actions. Both are research mechanisms, not purchase requests. AIT · RoboTryOn
