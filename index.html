    Data collection

    Human video is becoming a richer pretraining source. HuGe combines over 2,000 hours of human and robot experience, using simulation-derived pseudo-proprioception and visual adaptation. RynnVLA-Latent converts 30.1K hours of mixed human, robot and simulated video into latent-action supervision; that figure is not an order for human footage. EXE separates transferable manipulation structure from human-specific motion. These papers strengthen the case for diverse task coverage while showing that plain RGB still needs transformation before it supervises robot control. HuGe · RynnVLA-Latent · EXE

    Capture beyond RGB. DEX-PHD logs wrist video, proprioception and fingertip contact from an untethered dexterous handheld device. Its demonstration is open-loop humanoid replay, so learned-policy generalization remains unestablished. FLORE uses force-sensing handheld grippers and body trackers; its three-task evaluation reports 80% success versus 26.7% for position-only control on the same demonstrations. Hardware, calibration and synchronized contact signals are substantive additions to an ordinary headcam workflow. DEX-PHD · FLORE

    Automation changes the labour mix. AuRA uses robot interaction to acquire deformable digital twins, reporting lower active human labour across 13 objects. Zero-HLM uses smartphone scene capture and simulated demonstrations without real robot demonstrations. Neither removes the need to acquire and validate the physical scene. AuRA · Zero-HLM

    Data annotation & validation

    Outcome judges need their own audits. RoboJev’s open success judge reports strong held-out results, but its unseen-lab AUROC is slightly below the base model, and its frontier comparisons use different frame counts. A separate experiment finds that false execution logs can sway visual progress judgments. Keep video evidence, operator narration and machine logs distinguishable in QA. RoboJev · Execution-log study

    Success is insufficient. WorldFailBench tests where and why policies fail; RoboWorM checks kinematic and multiview consistency. SPECOLA measures specification margins and persistence, while EAST finds successful rollouts that violate safety requirements. The learning-utility study independently warns that realistic generated human behaviour need not be the best training partner. These are research findings, not universally validated acceptance standards. WorldFailBench · RoboWorM · SPECOLA · EAST · Utility study

    Embedded AI systems

    VLA-HWBench profiles the actual bottlenecks. Eighteen models on Jetson AGX Thor and AMD Ryzen AI Max+ PRO 395 show large latency differences driven by action generation and software. Parameter count alone is a poor latency proxy. TRIAGE chooses cached reuse versus pruning using token drift; TinyFlow removes a resampling bottleneck for static INT8 execution. Thermal-Headroom Routing is more preliminary: its throttling improvements are simulated, with hardware measurements supporting only part of the model. VLA-HWBench · TRIAGE · TinyFlow · Thermal study

    Data collection

    Original date Paper Why it matters / limit
    2026-10-24 EXE Factorizes human-specific motion from shared manipulation latents; tests transfer to grippers and dexterous hands. Evidence for reusable ego demonstrations, not native action labels.
    2026-10-25 HuGe Pretrains humanoid VLAs on over 2,000 hours of combined human and robot data; simulation-derived proprioception and visual adaptation support transfer.
    2026-10-25 RynnVLA-Latent Derives 3D-grounded latent supervision from 30.1K hours of human, robot and simulation video. This is mixed-source volume, not human capture procurement.

    2026-10-25 ARPG Pretrains action representations using human manipulation; a compact generative model then learns dexterous actions. Broader behaviour coverage improves tested downstream performance.

    2026-10-26 FLORE Force-sensing handheld grippers plus body trackers capture robot-free demonstrations; 80% versus 26.7% success on three contact-rich robot tasks.

    Annotation

    Original date Paper Why it matters / limit
    2026-10-24 RoboJev Open success judge trained on 52,598 episode records. Unseen-lab AUROC trails its base model; frontier comparisons use unequal frame inputs.
    2026-10-24 RoboReason Generates spatial and temporal ordering supervision from robot stereo/wrist views; separates reasoning evaluation from expensive physical rollouts.
    2026-10-24 WorldFailBench Tests failure reason, stage and boundary across seven world models. Matching final success alone does not validate simulated failure dynamics.
    2026-10-24 RoboWorM Uses kinematic and multiview invariants across 1,605 episodes; audits whether faster world models preserve physical consistency.
    2026-10-25 BEHAVIOR-Spatial Separates spatial question answering, grounding and action. Changed instructions within a familiar layout remain difficult despite apparent task competence.
    2026-10-25 PhysLadder Sixteen matched sim/real contact tasks, three trials each. Performance collapses on the most coordinated force-control tier; the small sample limits inference.
    2026-10-26 RobotAgentBench Fifty household layouts assess task discovery, reasoning, manipulation, recovery and robustness. Measures autonomy beyond completing a predefined action.
    2026-10-26 Behavioural quality versus learning utility Human-behaviour realism metrics do not consistently predict downstream robot learning in driving and handover experiments.
    2026-10-26 FERRET Human edits to an environment complement robot-motion corrections, exposing preferences that movement corrections alone cannot identify.
    2026-10-26 SPECOLA Temporal-logic monitors expose margins, violations and persistence hidden by success rates; adapters cover nine simulation environments.
    2026-10-25 Execution logs sway judges False progress logs shift VLM robot-video judgments; warning about unreliable logs removes much of the measured effect.
    2026-10-25 EAST Verified digital-twin fault injection finds task-completing rollouts with safety violations. Retained traces separate successful execution from safe execution.
    2026-10-24 RoboLongR History-dependent rewards preserve causal prerequisites; shuffled stages and terminal feedback help expose superficially plausible but invalid progress.
    2026-10-26 Language of Deformation Proposes semantic failure analysis for deformables, including buckling and tearing; proof-of-concept research rather than a production QA standard.
    2026-10-26 EmbodiedLab Thirty simulated science experiments require physical interaction to discover answers; manipulation of apparatus remains the largest failure source.
    2026-10-26 Preemptive instruction following Benchmarks interrupting and resuming navigation across single, repeated and nested requests; separate stage metrics reveal hidden recovery failures.
    2026-10-15 Generative Value Classifier Catch-up: return-conditioned action generation supplies richer critic supervision; evaluates offline and online reinforcement learning. First submitted October 15.

    2026-10-26 OpTTA Adapts world models with observed prediction error and an optimistic planning objective; better prediction alone need not improve control.

    Original date Paper Why it matters / limit

    Original date Paper Why it matters / limit

    2026-10-24 MOTIO Builds humanoid skills from human videos, scene assets and motion references with simulation feedback and human validation; demonstrates a physical G1 sequence.
