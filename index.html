    Data collection

    November 19 · Awaken-Human / Awaken-HI: the authors pair approximately 50,000 hours of stereo egocentric video with synchronized whole-body motion across more than 100 skills. Joint human-motion and retargeted robot-action supervision supports T800 adaptation; reported average success is 93.75% across eight individual tasks. This is bounded author-reported evidence, not a universal deployment rate; dataset downloads/licences were not independently tested. Paper

    November 19 · HumanCOIN: 12,500 hours / 38 scene classes comprise 10,000 hours of inferred-attention data, 2,000 hours with EMG and 500 hours with EEG; higher tiers inherit lower-tier signals. It also describes multi-view RGB, depth, point clouds, head/wrist pose and step labels, with timing/calibration records. Inferred gaze in the broad tier is distinct from directly measured gaze in the controlled tier. Interpretation: collection quality increasingly includes sensor provenance and synchronization, alongside video hours. Paper

    PUPPET separates task completion from a usable record: its 30-person remote-lab study reports 85.6% validation-ready VR trials versus 66.0% with 2D teleoperation. A successful manipulation can still yield an incomplete dataset. November19 paper

    Data annotation & validation

    November 18 catch-up · SimAudit: audits task instructions against simulated success checkers, finding 61 distinct defects across 49 tasks: 40 false successes and 21 rejected valid executions. The relevant lesson is to validate both incomplete attempts and alternative correct completions. Paper

    EgoCaptioner builds 50,000 training examples with coarse-to-fine event structure, detailed timed steps and separate factual/temporal rewards. Ego-ExBind shows retrieval improvements can arise from familiar verb–noun combinations without stronger compositional understanding. Neither a fluent caption nor a higher retrieval score alone establishes reliable action labels. EgoCaptioner · Ego-ExBind

    AgentRoboCritic describes 12,844 action annotations and a 400-action human-labelled evaluation set, exposing grasp, alignment and recovery failures that episode success conceals. Mind2Act separately tests reasoning, execution and their combination; strong isolated components still struggle together. Critic · Benchmark

    Embedded AI systems

    November 19 · FlashX proposes NAND-based experience retrieval to warm-start embodied policies. Reported end-to-end speedups are 1.29–3.95× versus SSD retrieval in evaluated workloads. This is architecture research, not an announced shipping chip. Paper

    CauSyncVLA addresses stale perceptual memory during asynchronous control; UniVEP reduces action-generation calls by changing prediction targets as noise falls. Their benchmark gains are not interchangeable with measured latency or reliability on a specified edge robot. CauSyncVLA · UniVEP
