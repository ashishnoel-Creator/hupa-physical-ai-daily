    Data collection

    November 5 · Serve opens three fleet products. Serve Link connects robots to off-board systems; Data Curator selects useful rare examples; a data catalogue packages terrain and infrastructure observations from its own sidewalk fleet. Bedrock uses the connectivity product across Wi-Fi, cellular and satellite. This is a seller launch, not an invitation to external collection vendors. Primary release

    November 5 · Capture methods now need more than a video-hour count. VREgo uses a consumer PICO headset for human demonstrations, but tests only two simulated tabletop tasks with one operator. Its strong stacking result depends on simulator object positions and rules developed on the same layouts used for evaluation. Spatial-UMI-Dream, first submitted November 4, instead uses 605 hours of RGB-D UMI and separates environmental from embodiment transfer. VREgo · Spatial-UMI-Dream

    November 5 · Useful dataset and augmentation additions: Social Humanoid derives a 260-hour human-humanoid interaction benchmark from five motion-capture datasets; VLA-Tug releases 3,180 annotated episodes for failure monitoring. MOMASPLAT augments mobile-manipulation demonstrations across base poses while modelling moving objects. OopsieRecover studies how a small set of semantic interventions transfers beyond the demonstrated failures. Social Humanoid · VLA-Tug · MOMASPLAT · OopsieRecover

    October 30 missed-signal catch-up · Movens-Zero. Authors describe 15M recovered human-motion sequences and 5M retargeted, execution-verified humanoid sequences spanning over 10,000 hours. These are pipeline-derived corpus figures, not newly commissioned capture hours or proof of universal video-use rights. Paper

    Data annotation & validation

    November 4 catch-up · Perceptron Egocentric 1.0. The asynchronous API provides subtask timelines, optional hand tracking and per-hand activity. List prices are $9/video-hour for timelines, +$12 for 2D or +$14 for 3D hands, and +$6 for activity labels; sub-minute videos have a one-minute minimum. The $29 combination uses 3D, not both tracking add-ons. Company benchmarks are limited: comparator hand results were not reproduced on identical segments, and its new EgoHome benchmark/code are promised for release. Robot grippers receive activity timelines, not human hand meshes. Release

    November 5 · MIT explains uncertainty-aware robot memory. UQ-DAAAM flags inconsistent object descriptions, selects better views and fuses captions. The institutional article is new; the underlying paper was first submitted June 6. MIT · Paper

    November 5 · Evaluation integrity is a recurring issue. La-JEPA identifies corrupted instructions in the released LIBERO-Plus evaluator; another study finds that exposing benchmark criteria can teach agents to satisfy a verifier without transferable task skill. RoboARCade records false success declarations on 24–39% of attempted subtasks. These are distinct failure mechanisms, not interchangeable leaderboard scores. La-JEPA · Criteria exposure · RoboARCade
