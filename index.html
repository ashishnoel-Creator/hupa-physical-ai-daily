    Data annotation & validation

    Grana, November 18: sparse subtask boundaries plus action-quality labels become dense progress targets. Elapsed time alone can confuse slow execution, failure and progress reversal. This is a concrete annotation protocol to inspect for long-horizon demonstration QA. Paper

    HandWorldBench and StrideWorld, November 18: hand control does not guarantee persistent object states; visually plausible world-model output can still deform geometry or lose action consistency. HandWorldBench evaluates 500 manually selected clips from a 20,500-clip curated pool. StrideWorld uses fixed references; unresolved count placeholders in its abstract are not converted into invented totals. HandWorldBench · StrideWorld

    SeRA and F³: failed/autonomous experience can contribute useful supervision if relabelled or compressed appropriately. Agreement between later policies is not proof of correct labels; SeRA retains uncertainty through action sets. These papers support tracking corrections and failure context, not automatically labelling all rollout video as good demonstrations. SeRA · F³

    Original submission Paper Why it matters / evidence limit
    2026-11-17 LuxTac: A Flexible, Scalable, and Open-Source Optical Taxel Array with Distributed Three-Axis Force Sensing for Tactile Robot Learning LuxTac resolves normal and shear forces in a flexible optical taxel array; calibration uses a CNC indenter and force/torque reference. Hardware evidence, not a camera-only force label.
    2026-11-17 How Uncertain Is My Action? Self-Correcting Visual Planning with Frozen World Models A frozen world model detects and compensates action-interface shifts; a real-arm test reduces reach error from 36 mm to 3–5 mm. Bounded shift experiments.
    2026-11-18 Finetuning VLAs for Dexterous Manipulation: The Right Checkpoint Is All You Need Two simulated LEAP-hand tasks show that the strongest supervised checkpoint need not be best for subsequent diffusion-steering RL; no universal adaptation recipe established.
    2026-11-18 ZERITH V2.5: From Predictive Power to Policy Learnability ZERITH V2.5 studies predictive adaptation under matched demonstrations and update budgets; multi-noise-level video training improves subsequent control in the reported tests.
    2026-11-18 TacPilot: Does Touch Need to Be Everywhere? TacPilot separates tactile feature refresh from action intervention; 56.4% fewer encoded regions in offline replay, plus exploratory simulation and four real tasks.
    2026-11-18 3DTouch: A Tactile-aware VLA Model with 3D Geometric Relations for Dexterous Manipulation 3DTouch aligns RGB-D geometry and five-finger forces in the robot-base frame; five real tasks report a 42-point gain over the corresponding VLA baseline.
    2026-11-18 CoRA-VTLA: Contact-Guided Attention and Queryable Response Memory for Tactile Post-Training CoRA-VTLA combines contact-focused attention with action-response memory; authors report 66.7% mean success across three real-robot tasks.
    2026-11-18 LaST1.0: A Unified Latent World-Action Model with Control-Centric SpatioTemporal Reasoning LaST1.0 predicts geometry-aware future wrist-view latents instead of complete video; evaluated across two embodiments, five manipulation tasks and simulation suites.
    2026-11-18 WAM-Policy: World Action Models with On-Policy Distillation for Quadrupedal Loco-Manipulation WAM-Policy uses 1,000 synthesized demonstrations across ten simulated quadruped tasks; distillation yields 14× faster sampling and a Go2 Air deployment demonstration.
    2026-11-18 Ego2X: Contact-aware Transfer from Egocentric Human Data to Any Robot Ego2X preserves object contacts when converting ego videos to robot data; RoboTwin success rises from 40.5% to 67.0%. Public pipeline and annotated-data release are promised, not verified downloads.
    2026-11-18 Neural Network-Based In-Contact Task Segmentation of Demonstrated Robot Motions via Hand Guiding Hand-guided motion segmentation uses end-effector twists, wrenches and manual ground truth. Uploaded November18, but this is a non-peer-reviewed 2025 conference extended abstract.
    2026-11-18 Old Experience, New Lessons: Relabeling Autonomous Robot Rollouts in Interactive Imitation Learning SeRA relabels autonomous experience using later-policy agreement and set-valued targets; real tomato-picking success is 80% versus 40% with matched human feedback.
    2026-11-18 HOForce: Motion and Force Reconstruction of Hand-Object Interaction Using a Single-View RGB-D Video HOForce estimates interaction forces from single-view RGB-D and known object properties, validated against tactile measurements. It does not recover force from arbitrary RGB alone.
    2026-11-18 TOMAS: Topology-Aware Operation Modeling and Adaptive Staging for Egocentric Hand Forecasting TOMAS links hand topology, objects and manipulation intent to forecast ego hand trajectories; supervision depends on available dataset annotations.
    2026-11-18 StrideWorld: Benchmarking Scene Rigidity and Action Consistency in Interactive Video World Model StrideWorld fixes geometry/action references before evaluation so scene drift cannot be absorbed by fitted references; model/video-count placeholders remain unresolved in the abstract.
    2026-11-18 EgoJev: Finding Evidence in Egocentric Video by Reading VLM Decisions EgoJev selects evidence frames with one frozen VLM; QAEgo4D accuracy improves from 51.2% to 62.0% using eight answer frames. A single-level version runs on Mac and iPhone.
    2026-11-18 HandWorldBench: A Unified Benchmark for Hand-Centric World Models HandWorldBench curates 20,500 ego clips from 600+ hours and manually selects 500 for evaluation; eleven models reveal gaps between hand control and persistent object-state fidelity.
    2026-11-18 F3^3: What Makes Good Experience in World-Action Models? F³ compresses successful and failed rollouts into verified operation records; experience usefulness depends on representation and context, not source success alone.
    2026-11-17 RFW-S: Everyday Deformable Objects with Independent Simulation Verification RFW-S supplies 83,365 deformable assets across 485 categories with independent simulation checks distinct from calibration interactions; downloads and licences not tested.
    2026-11-18 Single-Controller Teleoperation and Multimodal Dataset Generation for a Mobile Manipulator A single-controller Dingo-O/Gen3 Lite/RGB-D pipeline synchronizes and filters ROS demonstrations into Zarr; validation is a bounded bottle pick-and-place task.
    2026-11-18 SHAPE: Discrete Supervision and Parallel Continuous Streams for Learning Dexterous Manipulation from Egocentric Human Data SHAPE combines human action-token supervision with joint and end-effector output streams; the best readout differs between precise robot-data control and human transfer.
    2026-11-18 HINT-WAM: Hindsight-Informed Training for World Action Models HINT-WAM uses demonstration-future video as training-only privileged supervision for on-policy self-distillation; inference needs no future video.
    2026-11-18 RoboMorpher: Scaling Robot Morphology Representations for Cross-Embodiment Action Prediction RoboMorpher learns from 40 million morphology-state pairs and tests held-out robot descriptions; link-pose supervision helps cross-embodiment action prediction.
    2026-11-18 DiWALI: Diffusion Policy Learning in World Models via Latent Action Inference from Human and Robot Play DiWALI trains world models from action-free human and robot play, but still grounds latent actions using separately labelled robot commands; not zero robot data.
    2026-11-18 Render the Robot, Generate the World: One Video World Model Across Embodiments Doppel conditions video prediction on rendered robot motion across eight human/robot embodiments and about 600 hours; prediction metrics are not task-success evidence.
    2026-11-18 Grana: Compositional Reward Modeling for Long-Horizon Robot Manipulation Grana turns sparse subtask boundaries and action-quality annotations into dense progress targets; elapsed time alone can mislabel failures and unequal task durations.
    2026-11-18 Phoenix: Learning Scene Structure Representations for Generalizable World-Action Models Phoenix curates approximately 39,000 hours of ego and robot data for representation learning; robot demonstrations remain the world-action pretraining subset.
    2026-11-18 Counterfactual World Models: JEPAs That Reason About “What If” Counterfactual JEPA models reuse factual predictions under smooth additive-force assumptions; results are on rendered simulators, with discontinuity and command-scaling failure cases.
