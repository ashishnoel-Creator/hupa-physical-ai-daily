    Data collection

    October 27 research, newly added today: RoboSTD constructs pseudo-bimanual supervision from single-arm examples; ThinkWorld uses unlabelled human video alongside action-labelled demonstrations. These are evidence for improving the utility of collected experience, not proof that task-specific real collection is obsolete.

    The egocentric tennis study combines one moving ego camera with five external views and event/position labels. Joint camera–LiDAR calibration addresses intrinsic, distortion and extrinsic parameters together. Interpretation: synchronization, geometry and event-level annotation are concrete ways a collection deliverable can offer more than an undifferentiated video-hour count. Neither paper advertises a paid collection mandate.

    Data annotation & validation

    Robotics

    Original date Paper Contribution / limitation
    2026-10-26 Phase-conditioned loco-manipulation Uses task phases and a hierarchical controller to learn multi-stage behavior without motion demonstrations; hardware demonstrations on G1 and Spot, not a universal replacement for demonstrations.
    2026-10-27 Stochastic motion tokens Compresses whole-body references into discrete motion tokens; reports physical G1 dance/walk/fight sequences and a 35-second transition sequence.
    2026-10-27 GRAFT Keeps a behavior tracker frozen and adds terrain-aware lower-body corrections; simulation across four trackers and physical G1 deployment using onboard depth.
    2026-10-27 TOPS Adapts flow-policy source priors and schedules to tasks; benchmark and physical results suggest better data efficiency and cheaper sampling, with task-specific evaluation limits.
    2026-10-27 HC2Robot Predicts future foothold information from deployable observations. Its 97.60% average traversal success is a simulation result, not a measured deployment rate.
    2026-10-27 Keeping Contact Plans task-adaptive friction margins for bimanual tilting; physical tests address object variation and model mismatch.
    2026-10-27 Remember Where You Walk Spatial matrix memory preserves maze layout while matching exploration coverage; threefold reconstruction-error reduction is benchmark evidence, not a deployed navigation guarantee.

    Data annotation

    Original date Paper Contribution / limitation
    2026-10-26 HIDRA Separates instruction-based expert retrieval from contextual routing for replay-free continual imitation; evaluated on LIBERO, including language and task-order variation.
    2026-10-27 Auditable self-driving labs Proposes hybrid robot/API actions, timestamped execution records, runtime checks and an episode-level benchmark. Primarily an architecture/protocol proposal, not proven general laboratory autonomy.
    2026-10-27 ExecDiffuser Evaluates the trajectory actually selected and executed rather than only the best generated candidate; closed-loop driving benchmarks, not evidence of road deployment.
    2026-10-27 KEPT Tests sensing-calibrated keepouts on real smoke recordings and closed-loop simulated flight; per-regime confidence intervals include zero for its goal-reaching improvement.

    Data collection

    Original date Paper Contribution / limitation
    2026-10-27 RoboSTD Mirrors single-arm observations, state and actions, then imposes coordination constraints to create pseudo-bimanual supervision. ALOHA tests include real hardware; reuse does not establish broad task coverage.
    2026-10-27 Joint camera–LiDAR calibration Learns correspondences from raw images to jointly estimate intrinsics, distortion and extrinsics; zero-shot checks on KITTI and three real platforms support capture-rig QA.
    2026-10-27 EgoThumb Palm-facing egocentric video drives an imitation policy for an extra robotic thumb. Authors state hardware/software are open source; dataset licence and broader deployment not independently checked.
    2026-10-27 Egocentric tennis benchmark Synchronizes an ego camera with five external views, global positions, contact times and stroke labels; seven prediction tasks expose depth/forecasting limitations. Capture design matters more than raw video hours alone.
