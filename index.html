    Data collection

    November 20 · RoboCap-10k makes calibration and uniform hardware part of the dataset claim. The authors report unscripted manipulation during workers’ paid shifts, spanning 18 task families, 200 task types and 2,000 tasks. The 250g hat has six synchronized global-shutter cameras and dual IMUs; online calibration targets below 1 px epipolar error. Outputs include gravity-aligned 6DoF head trajectories, metric hand poses and task/subtask captions. These are author-reported specifications, not independent acceptance measurements. The associated dataset card lists CC BY 4.0 and manual access approval; no access request was submitted. Its LeRobot action field is a next-frame hand-keypoint placeholder, not recorded robot control. The card says human reviewers checked captions for 1,000 hours, with the remaining captions generated by the tuned pipeline. Dataset card Paper

    November 19 · TouchBench adds sensing for hidden properties. Its 900 clips from five people across 11 tasks synchronize head-mounted video and 137-channel whole-hand tactile sensing. Four tested VLMs average 49.8% on hidden-property relative questions, against 50% chance. Text descriptions of tactile statistics improve that result. Interpretation: a capture specification should distinguish visually observable actions from force/compliance/fill-state labels requiring another sensor. Paper

    November 20 · EgoSurgHands is a specialized training dataset, not clinical footage. Multi-sensor glasses capture open-surgery practice on synthetic pads: 55 training recordings and 13 held-out recordings, four trainees and 13 tasks. Evaluation combines sensor-derived pseudo labels, human review, surgeon adjudication and an independent multi-view reference. Procedure- and wearer-disjoint evaluation helps expose transfer limits. Paper

    Additional capture work below covers tactile-assisted metric reconstruction, synthetic tracking trajectories, generated collaborator views, and real LiDAR sampling replayed in simulation. Generated or pseudo-labelled data is kept distinct from measured ground truth.

    Data annotation & validation

    November 20 · EgoLAM separates wearer motion from manipulation. Measured wrist motion supervises action codes while camera motion is provided separately during training. At code extraction, two RGB frames suffice. The authors report 79.38% mean LIBERO success across three suites; this is not a physical production KPI. Interpretation: retain camera and wrist coordinate frames and synchronization when collecting motion-rich ego data. Paper

    World-model QA needs failures that prettier video can hide. NoPeBench measures obstacle penetration; NoPeWorld reduces the mean violation rate from 74% to 16% across six tested video models. A separate round-trip planning check shows that optimizing predicted goal proximity can make an already successful plan fail, and tests action/state consistency instead. Neither is a physical-robot safety certificate. NoPeBench/NoPeWorld, November 20 · Round-trip check, November 20

    The paper table also includes missing-action supervision, VISTA assembly/disassembly state graphs, sparse temporal memory and uncertainty-based demonstration selection.
