### Data collection

**Human-video scale comes with heterogeneous supervision.** [Being-M0.7](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11283), October 8, uses over 10,000 hours mixing video-only, motion-only and paired human data, followed by robot mid-training. [VioLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12435), October 8, uses 140.6M frames, 93.2% human, and a shared body/hand action representation. These are author-reported training compositions, not open purchase requests; VioLA's code/checkpoints are prospective.

**Contact and calibration matter.** [TACROSS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11945), October 8, studies a $10.86 prototype glove with 285 sensing points. That is a component/prototype cost, not a commercially supplied kit price. Human contact events supplement robot demonstrations, which retain ground-truth actions. The promised 150+hour dataset is not verified released. [YOCO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11657) calibrates operator/session glove bias. Together, these support treating operator and device calibration as collection metadata, not assuming all human streams are interchangeable.

**Repeat the same action as well as collecting new tasks.** [Same Action, Different Outcome](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10801), October 7, synchronizes 269 cloth rollouts with stereo, optical motion capture and robot logs; four calibrated simulators still miss real variability. This is evidence for repeated-trial coverage, not a general simulator ranking.

### Data annotation & validation

**DreamTrue, October 8:** human-annotated video defects and counterfactual post-training address physical inconsistencies in world models. Reported human-assessed defect prevalence drops 48.12%→6.25%; do not read that as robot-policy failure rate. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12468)

**ManiUnit, October 8:** 137,899 segments, 21 skills and 417 subtasks from 50 BEHAVIOR-1K activities support evaluation from intermediate states. This makes skill diagnosis more specific; strong local skill scores still do not imply strong end-to-end completion. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12089)

[Task Scrubbing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10912), October 7, tests visual shortcuts such as background/camera cues. [RoboAware](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11480), October 8, uses counterfactual branches from restored simulated states to assess executable capabilities. Both strengthen the case for distribution and failure audits alongside label correctness. No additional verified physical-data vendor tender emerged from annotation-company searches; generic agent products were excluded.

### Embedded AI systems

**OMNIVISION OG05D, October 5 catch-up.** Official specifications describe a 5MP, 2.2µm global-shutter sensor with three-exposure HDR at 60 FPS. Linear modes reach 130 FPS with cryptographic operation or 146 FPS without it. Sampling is current; mass production is planned for Q4 2026. Sensor specifications do not establish a complete calibrated recording rig. [Product page](https://www.ovt.com/products/og05d/)

**Compute results need their hardware context.** [WAMCache](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11401) reduces prefill FLOPs 32–42% with a reported 2.5-point real-task success cost. [LiteNWM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12368) reports 128× speedup against its stated baseline on RTX5090. Neither figure establishes Jetson latency or battery-powered collection performance.

### World models & sim-to-real

The workflow explicitly converts raw Zarr to LeRobot and passes robot assets/policies across URDF, ONNX and ROS2 stages. Its supplied 27 UR3 pick-and-place episodes are a worked example; users bring their own production data. [Official technical walkthrough](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/physical-ai/introducing-aws-physical-ai-toolchain/)
