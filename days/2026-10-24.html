    Data collection

    Catch-up, October 22 — physical task knowledge becomes shared training infrastructure. TUM describes around 100 humanoid/service robots initially learning in 48 training cells. Robot interactions feed a curated cloud data lake; industrial and intralogistics tasks are an initial focus. NEURA’s announcement places the facility at Munich Airport’s LabCampus, across more than 2,300 m². It combines robot data, human demonstrations and simulation; participating companies contribute workflows and process expertise. This is collaboration and infrastructure evidence, not a published paid collection-vendor programme.

    Newly discovered open real-to-sim resource: Nova-Wanderland reconstructs 20 outdoor captures into editable, object-level environments with semantic labels, joints, collision geometry and exports for Isaac Sim, MuJoCo, Gazebo and PyBullet. The raw dataset card was accessible when the normal page failed. Inputs include image sequences assembled into videos, rather than original continuous recordings. The card declares Apache-2.0 and CC0 for included Poly Haven resources; this is not an audit of every upstream right. Its author’s Reddit announcement explicitly says the release is not a validated robotics benchmark and some geometry/physics is inferred. Exact release timestamp was not established; checked October 24.

    Research implications: Co²Gen generates collaborative demonstrations without human teleoperation; STAMP reuses human motion priors to connect robot motions; TerraTransfer++ removes expert-trajectory supervision but still uses paired images and scene states. These are different substitutions for specific training inputs, not evidence that all real-world collection disappears. Original abstracts and limits are in the table below.

    Embedded AI systems

    October 23 — iNGage secures €4M for inertial-sensor industrialisation. The company release describes grants and loans from Bpifrance and banks, supporting technology transfer and a dedicated MEMS test laboratory. Its accelerometers, gyroscopes and IMUs target autonomous navigation when GNSS is unavailable. This is non-dilutive financing, not a new €4M equity round or proof of mass-production readiness.

    Inference research: One-Step Euler finds that sufficiently trained, sufficiently large flow-matching VLAs can often match multi-step sampling with one inference step across six studied policies. This is a conditional result, not permission to remove control validation. F2G-Pose reports 28 FPS pose-only inference from segmented RGB-D, using synthetic task-specific supervision plus an externally pretrained backbone; it is not a fully synthetic-from-scratch system. Multi-Tool Selection quantifies a local-retrieval versus large-cloud-model quality/latency tradeoff. See original papers below.

    Research papers worth a look

    23 newly added preprints, originally October 21–22, discovered during today’s catch-up sweep. Original abstracts were read; reported results are author claims within the stated experiments, not independently reproduced. Availability of linked code and every dataset licence was not audited. Priority reads: Co²Gen and STAMP for collection strategy; Route or Adapt, PhysTrace and Dual Exam for evaluation; One-Step Euler for deployment cost.

    Original date Paper Contribution and limit
    2026-10-21 TerraTransfer++: Demonstration-Free End-to-End Planner Self-play supplies policy/planner targets; paired image/scene-state data remain necessary even without expert trajectories. Results are benchmark-specific.
    2026-10-21 MPBRS: Multi-Potential-Based Reward Shaping for Unified Reward Configuration in Humanoid Locomotion Uses the same potential-based reward structure and weights across nine humanoids, with robot-specific metadata; not universal transfer without setup.
    2026-10-22 OASIS: Online Anchor-centric Scene Inference System for Open-Vocabulary Retrieval and Navigation in Dynamic Environments Sparse semantic anchors combine memory and transient observations to find relocated objects; simulation and real-world evaluations.
    2026-10-22 CMAR-search: Commonsense and Memory Augmented Reasoning for Object Search in Dynamic Interactive Environments Functional 3D scene graphs combine commonsense and memory for container/area search in changing environments.
    2026-10-22 Co^2Gen: Collaboratively Generating Task Plans for Decentralized Multi-Robot and Human Collaboration Executable multi-robot plans generate collaborative VLA demonstrations, matching human-teleoperated training in the reported tasks.
    2026-10-22 When One Step Is Enough: An Empirical Study of Euler Integration in Flow-Matching Policies Across six flow-matching VLAs, one-step inference largely matches multi-step at sufficient training/capacity; not a guarantee for every policy.
    2026-10-22 Causal Analysis of Failures of Cyber-Physical Systems with Continuous Inputs Distance-weighted causal responsibility explains failures with continuous inputs, tested on two driving simulators.
    2026-10-22 Adaptive Aerial Loco-Manipulation Policy Learning via LLM-Assisted Proactive Task Generation ALMA generates difficult aerial-manipulation training tasks using LLM feedback; includes zero-shot real-platform flight tests.
    2026-10-22 Robot Motion Transition with Human Motion Prior STAMP aligns human-space motion boundaries then retargets transitions to robots; Unitree G1/ROBOTIS K1 evaluations and physical demonstrations.
    2026-10-22 IORA: Irregular Object Immobilization under Restricted Actuation Support-aware low-DoF grasp synthesis validates contacts in rollouts and real cooking-task immobilisation experiments.
    2026-10-22 LAST-V2X: Language-Driven Adaptive Scenario Synthesis and Testing for Communication-Induced Failures in V2X Safety Applications LAST-V2X pairs degraded/ground-truth communication runs to isolate communication-induced failures in CARLA scenarios.
    2026-10-22 TRACE: Traversability-aware Reactive Navigation via VLM-driven Adaptive Constraints Estimation VLM confidence maps become soft navigation constraints, evaluated on real forest terrain; task-specific trials, not a general safety certification.
    2026-10-22 Revisiting Distribution Matching for State-to-Vision Policy Distillation in Laparoscopic Robotic Manipulation State-to-vision laparoscopic policy distillation uses RGB and proprioception; results are in simulation, not clinical deployment.
    2026-10-22 Multi-Tool Selection for Robotic Systems: Comparing Semantic Retrieval and LLM 164 annotated prompts compare local semantic routing with a 120B cloud model; lower retrieval latency trades off against selection quality.
    2026-10-22 AstroBench: Benchmarking Free-Floating Capture and Space Robotic Manipulation AstroBench has nine task definitions and 29 task-target combinations; a frozen generalist captures few targets, while a specialised policy does better in a separate experiment.
    2026-10-22 To Route or to Adapt? Multi-Expert Diffusion Planners in Unseen Regions Twenty target-region scenes improve open-loop planner offsets more than expert routing; nuPlan closed-loop performance deteriorates despite offline gains.
    2026-10-22 Point-Level Refinable Semantic Mapping for Spatial Understanding Point confidence is revised using multiview temporal evidence, suppressing stale geometry rather than only accumulating observations.
    2026-10-22 Humanoid Mobility via Attention-based Contacts Human-motion data serve as training priors for contact planning/tracking; onboard deployment needs no reference motion input.
    2026-10-22 Navigating human-robot interaction challenges in private and residential aged care homes Review of home/aged-care HRI stresses accessibility, visible consent controls, fault recovery and long-term reliability; no new deployment trial.
    2026-10-22 Inside-JEPA: From Prediction to Relational Grounding in Latent World Models Inside-JEPA uses inverse-dynamics supervision to ground relational channels; prediction alone is weak, with tests on video and held-out RH20T tasks.
    2026-10-22 PhysTrace: Interpretable Temporal Evaluation of Physical Consistency in Video Generative Models PhysTrace localises physical violations in video and adds IntPhys2-Timing intervals; up to 70.7% hit rate is benchmark-specific.
    2026-10-22 F2G-Pose: Geometry-Aware Foundation Feature Lifting for Direct RGB-D Category-Level Object Pose Estimation F2G-Pose reports 28 FPS pose-only inference from segmented RGB-D; synthetic task supervision uses an externally pretrained frozen backbone.
    2026-10-22 Dual Exam: Controlled Evaluation of Multimodal Agents and World Models Dual Exam fixes actions or environment responses to separate agent/world-model errors; a 150-case coupled pilot has zero successes, and component scores are not directly comparable.
