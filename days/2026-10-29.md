### Data collection

**October 27 research, newly added today:** [RoboSTD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36685) constructs pseudo-bimanual supervision from single-arm examples; [ThinkWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37666) uses unlabelled human video alongside action-labelled demonstrations. These are evidence for improving the utility of collected experience, not proof that task-specific real collection is obsolete.

The [egocentric tennis study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37619) combines one moving ego camera with five external views and event/position labels. [Joint camera–LiDAR calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37350) addresses intrinsic, distortion and extrinsic parameters together. **Interpretation:** synchronization, geometry and event-level annotation are concrete ways a collection deliverable can offer more than an undifferentiated video-hour count. Neither paper advertises a paid collection mandate.

### Data annotation & validation

[Auditable self-driving laboratories](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36761) proposes timestamped causal records, typed device actions and runtime monitoring; it is primarily a framework, not demonstrated general autonomy. [ExecDiffuser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36837) distinguishes generated candidate quality from the action actually selected and executed. [KEPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37217) explicitly reports uncertainty in goal-reaching gains. These are useful reminders to demand deployment-relevant evaluation and acceptance criteria.
