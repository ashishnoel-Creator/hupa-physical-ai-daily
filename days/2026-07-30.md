### Data collection

- **Delta D1 is the closest thing yet to a funded Chinese analog of the rig-first thesis.** A head-mounted device worn during normal operations, no mocap studio or site retrofitting, recording first-person panoramic vision and whole-body joint trajectories, with the output described as training-ready immediately after collection. A handheld D1-Gripper accessory captures end-effector detail for fine dual-arm tasks. Delta was spun out of the Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence in January 2026 and has model deployment partnerships with Zhiyuan (AgiBot), Leju, Galaxea and Unitree. Six rounds in six months, backers including Hillhouse, Fosun RZ, Lenovo Capital and three humanoid OEMs, and the round proceeds explicitly earmarked for mass production of collection devices and a data closed loop. The lane keeps getting funded: Ropedia in Singapore three weeks ago, Delta in Beijing now. Neither claims audit-trailed validation against a buyer spec. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/seeds-backed-by-agibot-leju-and-galaxea-ai-who-is-delta-intelligence-2082453965206691841)
- **Being-H0.8's TactoHand system supplements massive volumes of ordinary human video with tactile supervision without gloves or sensors, inferring contact probability and continuous proximity at dense spatial points.** BeingBeyond says it has extended tactile labels to 500,000+ hours of first-person video this way, and its TopoHand action space gives human hands, dexterous hands and parallel grippers one unified kinematic interface. Same direction as last week's FELT paper, but at pre-training scale and from a company with dozens of named data collaborators. Every sensor a pipeline can infer from pixels is a sensor a capture spec no longer has to carry, and the paid work moves further toward verifying the inference. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/let-robots-understand-by-touch-the-world-beingbeyond-releases-new-generation-model-being-h08-2082453538088128513)
- **HiFi-UMI (arXiv 2607.25895, submitted July 29) trains deployable manipulation policies from high-fidelity handheld UMI data alone, no teleoperation, and ships the HiFi-UMI-2K dataset openly on Hugging Face.** The cheap-capture-hardware lineage keeps compounding: if handheld gripper data alone gets policies to deployment, the value concentrates in how carefully the capture was run, which is an operations argument. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25895)
- **TechCrunch ran a feature asking whether brain waves are the next unlock for physical AI, built around operators generating robot training data on leader-follower rigs.** Filed as a pointer: neural and EMG signals as a capture modality is a thread worth watching, not yet a market. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/26/are-brain-waves-the-next-unlock-for-physical-ai/)

### Data annotation & validation

- **Gasgoo published the sharpest practitioner synthesis yet on why the data bottleneck is not scale: quality imbalance, fragmented standards, and a missing mid-pipeline.** The named specifics matter. XYZ Embodied AI tried training on 200,000+ datapoints mixed from 20+ robot brands and got an unusable mess until it built its own standardization platform. Manifold AI's CEO says embodied collection is all front-end acquisition while the cleaning, labeling and QC stages that autonomous driving pipelines treat as mandatory are simply missing. XYZ discloses a 90/10 split, non-robot data for pre-training and real-robot data for post-training alignment. JD Logistics argues failure and recovery data is severely underpriced, and that warehouse WMS records (weight, dimensions, material) are ready-made labels nobody joins to visual data. The piece also sorts the market into four player types, with third-party data services framed as the industry's utilities layer. Read the whole thing. It is the demand spec for a validation business, written by the buyers. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/icv/embodied-ai-faces-more-than-just-a-data-shortage-2082113725099118593)
- **Data Pyramid for Embodied Manipulation (arXiv 2607.24744, submitted July 28) is a large multi-institution survey that codifies the internet-video / human-capture / robot-teleop pyramid the industry keeps describing informally, with a maintained repo of datasets at each layer.** Useful as the citable reference for where ego capture sits in the stack and what annotation each layer is expected to carry. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24744)

### Robotics & humanoids

- **The FCC ban is the biggest structural move in the humanoid market this year.** What it covers: new Chinese-made humanoid and quadruped robots plus network-connected power inverters, on supply chain and cybersecurity grounds, with the FCC citing remote control and surveillance risk. What it does not cover: anything already through FCC equipment authorization, so existing Unitree and AgiBot models keep selling in the US. The exposure math: Unitree holds nearly a fifth of the global humanoid market, and Unitree and AgiBot each shipped more than 5,000 of the roughly 15,000 humanoids sold globally in 2025. It also lands 48 hours after AgiBot formally started its Hong Kong IPO and while Unitree's approved STAR listing sits unpriced, so both prospectuses now need a US-market risk rewrite. China's foreign ministry responded on July 29 that it firmly opposes the move and will take necessary measures. For every Western robot program, the second-order effect is a higher hardware floor: the cheapest capable platforms just got fenced out of the largest buyer market. ### Research papers worth a look

- **Being-H0.8: implicit tactile world-action model (BeingBeyond, released July 28).** Full read in the collection section above. Tactile modality in large-scale pre-training via TactoHand pseudo-labels over 500K+ hours of ego video, TopoHand unified action space, slow-fast action expert for high-frequency tactile feedback. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/let-robots-understand-by-touch-the-world-beingbeyond-releases-new-generation-model-being-h08-2082453538088128513)
- **HiFi-UMI: Learning Deployable Manipulation Policies from High-Fidelity UMI Data Alone (arXiv 2607.25895, July 29).** Full read in collection above. Includes the open HiFi-UMI-2K dataset. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25895)
- **Data Pyramid for Embodied Manipulation (arXiv 2607.24744, July 28).** Full read in annotation above. The citable codification of the data pyramid, with a maintained dataset repo. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24744)
- **τ: Learning Touch-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Models from Future Visual Supervision (arXiv 2607.24485, July 28).** Trains touch-augmented VLAs by supervising against future visual states instead of dedicated tactile ground truth. Second paper this window deriving touch without touch sensors; the trend is now unmistakable. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24485)
- **When Does Legacy Data Start to Help? Emergent Transfer in Cross-Configuration Robot Learning (arXiv 2607.25593, July 29).** Asks when data collected on old robot configurations transfers to new ones and finds transfer emerges rather than accruing linearly. Directly relevant to dataset resale value: data that outlives the hardware it was captured on is worth more than the market prices it at today. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25593)
- **ArmnetBench v0.1 (arXiv 2607.24481, July 28).** Parallel real-world evaluation of manipulation policies on a low-cost arm farm, data on Hugging Face. Cheap parallelized physical eval is the missing acceptance-testing infrastructure; every step it takes makes validated delivery more checkable. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24481)
