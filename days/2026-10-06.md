### Research papers worth a look

**October 2 catch-up · SceneFactory-3D isolates physical road conditions in simulation.** Matched traffic scenarios vary friction and grade while holding controllers and scene setup fixed. Across its tested learned policies, reducing friction from 1.0 to 0.18 lowers safe work-zone completion by **6–90 percentage points**. The spread matters: a single mean hides policy-specific sensitivity. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02874)
