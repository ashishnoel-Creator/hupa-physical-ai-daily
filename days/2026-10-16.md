### Data collection

**Newly discovered, article undated · Natura-1.** NatureWill describes a 50B mixture-of-experts model pretrained on over one million hours of egocentric human experience, followed by robot adaptation. Its pipeline deduplicates video/clips, screens visual and sensor anomalies, controls task quotas, reconstructs dense 3D hand poses from monocular/stereo video and adds goal/subtask/step language. This is a useful view of the processing expected beyond raw footage. The company reports dexterous-hand benchmarks and demonstrations; its baseline comparisons use different embodiments, and its longer workflow demonstrations include task-specific tuning. Do not describe every demo as zero-shot or infer external purchasing from dataset scale. [Original technical account](https://www.naturewill.com/blog/natura-1)

**October 15 · touch and controller-compatible data.** Spark-0's HumanTouch dataset contains 1,000 hours of full-hand tactile manipulation. The authors report gains even when touch is removed at inference. KODA converts ego RGB into aligned whole-body trajectories, robot-view observations and controller-specific action labels; it tests two controllers on four G1 tasks. Interpretation: synchronized contact information and reusable alignment records may be more valuable than a bare footage-hour count, depending on the buyer's training stack. [Spark-0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22342) · [KODA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21152)

**October 14–15 · several routes from human video to robot learning.** EgoJEPA learns latent actions without rule-based retargeting; MoCaP extracts navigation/approach cues from passive video; TDV uses object trajectories. DemoStream instead automates real demonstration generation through task-and-motion planning, reporting 66 demos/hour in its setup. These are different data-production strategies with different hardware and validation requirements, not interchangeable industry throughput figures. [EgoJEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20904) · [MoCaP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20998) · [TDV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21792) · [DemoStream](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22020)

### Data annotation & validation

**October 13 catch-up · Google's robotics workflow examples.** Gemini 4 Argon examples cover simulation reconstruction from ego video, robot-camera/force matching, and hierarchical hindsight labels with task progress and failed-segment weighting. The human-video example also supplies camera information and 3D hand keypoints; it is not evidence that any uncalibrated video alone produces a reliable twin. The post reports 87% on ERQA and shows representative prompts/demos. These examples are not an independent validation of production annotation accuracy. [Google technical post](https://developers.googleblog.com/gemini-4-argon-for-robotics/)

**October 15 · retain failure timing, force and execution context.** MAIA introduces CRIA: 2,852 trajectories over 18 contact-rich tasks, with frame-level anomaly onset/evolution labels. CarryOn combines preference memory, predicted-contact supervision and recovery; VeriVLA ranks action candidates at additional inference cost. The Bench2Drive audit separately shows how a comfort metric can reward standing still and how evaluation-controller mismatches distort conclusions. [MAIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21686) · [CarryOn](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21453) · [VeriVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21985) · [Audit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21741)

### Embedded AI systems

**October 14–15 · faster control by reducing repeated computation.** Fast-VLA invokes its frozen 4B VLM once per episode, then uses a lighter feedback actor; its 10.9–17.1× inference improvements are benchmark/hardware-specific. Causal analysis of VLAs identifies conditioning layers that can be pruned from the action interface. LIN-SLAM demonstrates onboard mapping on Go2/Orin NX. An event-driven MAV study compares spiking modules on Loihi against ANN counterparts; its module power ratios are not full-drone energy savings. [Fast-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21389) · [Causal analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20750) · [LIN-SLAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21199) · [Neuromorphic navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21978)
