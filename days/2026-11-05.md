### Data annotation & validation

**November 2 catch-up · RoboColosseum.** The paper reports **5,699 pairwise comparisons at 124 sites** across four robot embodiments. Open-track rankings poorly predict fine-tuned rankings; the G1 comparison has zero complete successes on five tasks, with partial progress between 13.3% and 23.2%. Interpretation: task coverage, physical execution and partial-progress labels answer different questions from one aggregate leaderboard. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02497)

**November 3 catch-up · A correct-sounding explanation is not an action check.** A study of Alpamayo and InstructVLA shows visual perturbations can alter actions while preserving the generated rationale. Its evaluated attacks are not an estimate of ordinary field failure frequency. [Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04166)

**November 2–3 catch-up · QA at the data boundary.** Two UrbanTwin challenge reports identify coordinate frames, null returns and post-processing as substantial sources of sim-to-real error. A vegetation study constructs visibility-aware LiDAR references for visual reconstruction evaluation. [Roadside LiDAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03936) · [V2X LiDAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03940) · [Visibility-aware references](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05361)
