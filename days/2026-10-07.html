    Data collection

    Collect where policies fail. Mulligan, submitted October 5, allocates supervised episodes to observed failures and untried initial states. The authors report 10–34 percentage-point final real-task gains when combined with value-based action selection, across three real tasks and matched collection budgets. Their 98% completion figure includes operator intervention, so it is a human–robot collection-team result, not autonomous success. Commercial implication, as an inference: task/state coverage and useful recovery segments deserve their own delivery metrics.

    History and contact become explicit data requirements. Grounded in Time combines real/UMI demonstrations with counterfactual history-dependent tasks. TacOT, DexForge and InterMimicGen study contact-aware transfer from human motion to robots. These are research releases, not paid collection invitations. Details and original dates are in the paper table.

    Research papers worth a look

    30 newly added preprints, originally submitted October 2–5. Today’s accessible arXiv cs.RO listing was dated October 6 (136 new submissions); these are discovery catch-up, not October 7 publications. Results below are author-reported, mostly from abstracts and project descriptions, without independent reproduction.

    Original date Paper Why it matters / evidence limit
    2026-10-05 Mulligan: Performance-Guided Data Collection for Efficient On-Robot Learning Targets failures and untried starting states instead of uniform collection; reports 10–34 percentage-point gains on three real tasks at matched budgets with value-based selection.
    2026-10-02 REDIRECT: A 1% Fix for Bad Robot Habits Repairs locally defective demonstration continuations using episode labels and 1% of full-training sample-backward compute; tested in simulation and on PiPER.
    2026-10-02 Reward-DAgger: Robot-Gated Interactive Imitation Learning with General-Purpose Progress-Based Reward Models Uses a general progress reward model to request human intervention; eight simulated/real tasks without task-specific gate retuning.
    2026-10-03 Grounded in Time: A Multi-Source Dataset and Benchmark for Temporal Grounding in Robotic Manipulation History-dependent benchmark combines 18 real/UMI bimanual tasks and nine simulation tasks, with subtask labels and counterfactual pairs.
    2026-10-03 TacOT: Learning Contact-Rich Dexterous Manipulation from Human Demonstrations via Tactile-Guided Optimal Transport Tactile dynamics align human and robot demonstrations without predefined frame pairs; four real dexterous tasks.
    2026-10-03 Frame-Level Temporal Alignment for Human-to-Robot Visual Adaptation Aligns task progress despite different execution speeds, without frame-level correspondence labels; human-video encoder adaptation.
    2026-10-03 Robot Learning with Visual Predicted Force Calibrates force estimates from compliant-gripper deformation and adds them to demonstrations; avoids force sensors at deployment, not calibration data.
    2026-10-03 ExStereo: Lifting 2D Vision-Language-Action Models to 3D with Explicit Stereo Representations Adds explicit stereo geometry to pi0.5 and SmolVLA with stereo mid-training; bimanual PiPER and simulation evidence.
    2026-10-04 R²-WAM: Repair-and-Reject Post-Training for World Action Models Repairs action-conditioned video predictions before rejecting inferior actions; reported RoboTwin and real shirt-folding results are task-specific.
    2026-10-04 DiVeR: Decision-Critical Verifier Learning for VLA Test-Time Scaling Weights verifier learning toward decision-critical states using sampled-action dispersion; no extra step labels required.
    2026-10-04 Recursive Self-Improvement of Visuomotor Policies through Local Recovery Supervision Collects local corrective segments at audited failures; preliminary simulation gains, not a real-world scaling result.
    2026-10-05 When to Switch: Reliable Action-Chunk Extension for Vision-Language-Action Models Predicts subskill transition timing for longer action chunks; reported reduction in idle time on a real robot.
    2026-10-05 FreeSpeed: Training-Free Speed Control for Generative Robot Policies Adjusts speed by resampling action chunks according to directional inconsistency; evaluated speed limits are not universal safety guarantees.
    2026-10-05 How (and How Not) to Use Data Augmentation in VLA Post-Training Critic-only image augmentation improves LIBERO-Plus results for pi0.5 and GR00T N1.5; actor augmentation collapsed training in the study.
    2026-10-02 SUAVE: Unified Video-Action Models via Masked Diffusion Shared discrete video/action/language tokens support masked diffusion and action-free human-video co-training; real-robot inference uses RTX5090.
    2026-10-03 ForeAct3D: Policy-Grounded Future World Modeling for VLA Policies Action-conditioned semantic 3D forecasts shape policy training; future prediction is unnecessary at inference.
    2026-10-03 PerturBot: Breaking Shortcut Priors in Vision-Language-Action Models with Perturbative Training Perturbs visual shortcuts, enriches instructions and relabels failures; GroundingFscore tests whether success reflects relevant evidence.
    2026-10-04 Now You Feel It, Now You See Me: Digital-Twin-based Teleoperation Interface for Dexterous Manipulation Bare-hand teleoperation interface visualizes tactile measurements and mitigates occlusion through a digital twin; user-study evidence.
    2026-10-02 GOTT: Object-centric Dexterous Manipulation with a Reusable Cross-Embodiment Primitive Reuses a contact-acquisition primitive across hands and tasks, with object trajectories from human or model intent.
    2026-10-03 Continual Humanoid Motion Learning Similarity-guided low-rank progressive learning adds humanoid skills without revisiting old data; demonstrated on Unitree G1.
    2026-10-04 Have I Scene This Before? Spatially Grounded Conversational Memory for Complex Queries in Egocentric Assistants Ego-SpaCR couples spatial and conversational memory; 620 ScanNet sessions, 95 conversations and 3,100 queries, not newly recorded commercial ego data.
    2026-10-05 Robotizing Human Videos with Physically Consistent Interactions Contact reconstruction and depth-aware compositing turn human videos into robot-compatible training; monocular depth limitations remain.
    2026-10-05 Inspect Robots: Evaluating the Capabilities and Safety of Embodied AI Open-source physical evaluation framework demonstrated with six frontier-model policies; the preprint is new, the software predates it.
    2026-10-05 DexForge: High-Fidelity Physics-Informed Dexterous Retargeting Physics-based human-video retargeting tested on 130 DexYCB/HOT3D demonstrations and seven hands; real-robot transfer included.
    2026-10-05 ArtifactArena: Evaluating Models by What They Build in the Physical World Evaluates hardware/software robot designs in a simulated competition; physical grounding does not mean real hardware was manufactured.
    2026-10-05 SimForcing: Distilling Simulation Motion Priors into Real-Domain Robot World Models Distills simulation motion priors into real-domain video prediction; Bridge/InternData-A1 and downstream LIBERO evaluation.
    2026-10-05 AffordCraft: Scalable Construction of Task-Ready Simulation Assets from Single Images Retrieves and fits articulated assets to images/tasks; 1,703 of 2,000 images produce physically valid assets under its test.
    2026-10-05 InterMimicGen: Scaling Humanoid Loco-Manipulation through Self-Evolving Motion Imitation Retargets human-object motion, trains a general tracker, and retains successful task-preserving simulated variants; real transfer reported.
    2026-10-05 Future Anchored Verification and Online Recovery for World Action Models Uses predicted future frames to detect deviations and guide recovery; reported LIBERO gains are small, not a large breakthrough.
    2026-10-05 Encoded but Not in Control: Revealing the Grounding Gap in Vision-Language Robot Policies Scene-fixed instruction changes expose policies that encode the requested target but still act on the scene-favoured object.
