### Data collection

- **The OneRobotics and 58.com deal is the collection story of the window, and it reads like the Moqi playbook scaled through a platform. OneRobotics already runs a full loop of real-world capture (ego-centric vision, UMI, teleop), physical robot training and scenario verification, converting "action processes, operational experience, environmental changes, and interaction feedback" into trainable, evaluable, replayable data assets.** 58.com and its Xingxingkexing subsidiary bring the lifestyle-services scenarios, user connectivity and, critically, the talent network: the two will pilot "employees and robots collaborating on real-world tasks," which means service workers generating training data during paid work. OneRobotics claims products in 90+ countries and 5M+ households, so the deployment surface for verification is already there. Wearable capture during paid labor keeps getting institutionalized in China while it remains a startup thesis in the West. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/market-industry/onerobotics-58com-reach-deep-cooperation-2088508124096126977)
- **OmniShare (in AdvDex, arXiv 2608.14028) pairs human demonstrations with tactile measurements and kinematic supervision at large scale, then trains policies that transfer zero-shot to robot embodiments through an SE(3) wrist plus 15-finger-joint aligned action space and domain-adversarial appearance stripping.** The stated motivation is that embodiment-aware teleop data is too expensive. Every serious human-demo corpus now ships with the force or contact channel the plain video corpora lack. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14028)
- **HumanTracker (arXiv 2608.13555, Tsinghua and PKU authors with He Wang and Li Yi, ECCV 2026) releases roughly 153 hours of professional-performer optical mocap for humanoid motion tracking, plus HumanScore, a preference-aligned quality metric trained on 12K motion pairs that catches contact and stability failures (foot skating, mistimed touch-downs) that kinematic metrics miss.** A learned judge of motion quality is QC infrastructure for every teleop and retargeting pipeline. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13555)
- **RHOAS (arXiv 2608.10847, TU Braunschweig) puts active force support behind kinesthetic teaching: model-based force estimation, no wrist F/T sensor, and a 16-participant study on a KUKA iiwa showing significant reductions in operator effort.** Demonstrations per operator-hour is the unit economics of collection, and this attacks it at the physical-effort layer. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10847)
- **Hand2Bot (arXiv 2608.13028, NTU Singapore) is an RGB-D human-to-robot handover dataset that keeps body posture, facial context and real sensor noise, paired with a video-diffusion pipeline for sim-to-real depth-noise editing.** Handover is a contact-rich capture spec that plain manipulation corpora skip. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13028)
- **Amazon published a full record-train-deploy loop for robot data on Hugging Face: Strands Agents plus LeRobot streaming demonstrations straight into HF Storage Buckets, so capture, training and deployment run from one place.** Never covered here before (posted August 13). The tooling for small-team capture pipelines keeps consolidating into the LeRobot ecosystem. [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/amazon/strands-lerobot-streaming-data-loop)
- **DaViNCi (arXiv 2608.11901, SJTU) is the first outdoor vision-language navigation dataset with continuous actions and dynamic elements: 6,933 trajectories across 6 maps, and success rates drop more than 10 points against prior datasets.** Outdoor dynamic capture remains an underserved spec. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11901)

### Data annotation & validation

- **PRM-as-a-Judge 1.5 (arXiv 2608.14284, a 25-author BAAI-orbit release with Yao Mu and Zhongyuan Wang) turns rollout videos into dense progress curves and fine-grained quality metrics: failure-side progress, post-drawdown recovery, success-side execution quality, all beyond binary success.** It ships RoboPulse++, a testbed for evaluating the reliability of process reward models themselves, plus an open assessment suite. Judging the judges is now its own released toolchain, and every layer of it needs curated judged rollouts underneath. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14284)
- **FACT (arXiv 2608.10232, Nicklas Hansen and Xiaolong Wang, UC San Diego) makes failure data a first-class training signal for world-action models: the model predicts future video AND task progress, so failed rollouts supervise action consequences instead of being discarded, and the progress head scores candidate actions at inference.** Performance improves as more failure data is added. The industry habit of throwing away rejected takes just got a direct rebuttal from a top lab group. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10232)
- **VIScore (arXiv 2608.11174, Brown, with Randall Balestriero and Levine on the author list) is a metric that predicts the planning success of latent world models across encoder, predictor and planner, with Spearman correlation above 0.75 on unseen models and datasets.** World-model QC keeps getting formalized, one released metric at a time. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11174)
- **StageWAM (arXiv 2608.10780, Shanghai AI Lab orbit) bolts a Stage-JEPA progress predictor onto a world-action model, hitting 90.25% across 50 RoboTwin 2.0 tasks with 5.97% fewer execution steps.** Stage-level progress labels are becoming a standard supervision channel, which is a labeling workload. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10780)
- **Temporal GRPO (arXiv 2608.13026, CAS Institute of Software) fixes credit aliasing in VLA reinforcement post-training by comparing only rollouts that reached the same detectable task stage.** Another consumer of stage annotations. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13026)

### Embedded AI systems

- **RIFT (arXiv 2608.11521, ANU with Hongdong Li) shows world-action models can skip iterative video rollout entirely by consuming a fixed future K/V cache built in one backbone pass: 98.8% on LIBERO while cutting action-chunk latency 68 to 89%, plus best-observed 92.9% on RoboTwin 2.0.** The WAM inference-cost objection is being dismantled paper by paper. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11521)
- **Reflex (arXiv 2608.14379) targets reaction-critical manipulation: ReflexBench stresses 6 dynamic tasks under configurable latency, and ReflexVLA cuts deployment latency with batched visual encoding and CUDA Graph replay plus latent future prediction, validated on real hardware.** Latency as a first-class benchmark axis is new and overdue. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14379)
- **Neural Introspection Gating (arXiv 2608.10824, University of Tokyo JSK lab, IROS 2026) makes KV-cache reuse in VLAs self-aware: it watches the logit margin between top-2 action tokens and invalidates the cache when confidence drops, recovering the accuracy blind caching loses while keeping 80% of the compute savings.** Training-free, so it composes with any deployed VLA. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10824)
- **Flex-pi (arXiv 2608.10860, a UW/NVIDIA-orbit team with Dieter Fox) is a 6B world-action model supervised on RGB, 3D pointmaps and DINO semantics through one shared frozen video-VAE latent, with per-stream dropout so a single checkpoint scales from fast action-only mode to full joint generation.** Claims 2-7x over the strongest baselines on real bimanual dexterous tasks while running faster than pi-0.5. Compute-flexible inference is the deployment feature Jetson-class budgets have been waiting for. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10860)
- **Horizon Robotics' Starry chip took a designation from a leading NEV maker as its Pateo collaboration enters mass production.** Automotive, but Horizon is a standing name on the China robotics-compute watch and its volume economics flow downstream into robot brains. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/horizon-starry-chip-secures-designation-from-leading-nev-manufacturer-horizon-robotics-and-pateo-collaboration-enters-new-mass-production-stage-2088520544357203969)

### Robotics & humanoids

- **MiDAS (arXiv 2608.11363, CMU with Aviral Kumar and Max Simchowitz) claims the first reliable adaptation of a generalist policy from a single task demonstration: BC-anchor the pretrained VLA on one demo, then run value-based online RL on a residual policy.** On a real bimanual YAM platform a fragile one-demo policy improves over roughly 6 hours of autonomous interaction. If one demo plus autonomous practice becomes the recipe, the value per demonstration hour goes up, not down. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11363)
- **StellaVLA (arXiv 2608.11671, University of Sydney) adapts at test time by conditioning on one retrieved structured demonstration with an auto-generated task plan and verbalized 3D motion, at zero annotation cost, and ranks first on VLA-Arena at 0.63 against 0.44 for pi-0.5.** It accepts real-robot, human-hand or XR demonstrations as the retrieval source, which widens what counts as usable demo inventory. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11671)
- **SALT (arXiv 2608.10484, CMU with Yonatan Bisk) shows reconstruction-only action tokenizers erode verb grounding, and fixes it by making a frozen VLM recover the instruction from quantized action latents: 71.9% average SimplerEnv success against 42.7% for a recon-only VQ-VAE and 31.2% for FAST.** Tokenizer choice just became a first-order design decision for VLA training. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10484)
- **Seeker (arXiv 2608.13422, KTH and Freiburg, Kragic and Valada groups) learns label-free visual bottlenecks from frozen DINOv3 features and lifts real-robot success from 48.3% to 76.7% in-domain, and from 20.0% to 60.0% under lighting and background shift.** Domain-shift robustness without new labels keeps improving, which shifts the data premium further toward distribution coverage. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13422)
- **SHAPER (arXiv 2608.11350, Microsoft Research Asia) does train-free adaptation by evolving reusable skills and a "context-code harness" from target-environment rollouts around a frozen model.** MSR independently reaching for the harness vocabulary is worth logging. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11350)
