    Data collection

    Catch-up: better demonstrations require more than more footage. HALO, submitted November 12, combines kinesthetic bimanual teaching with a low-resistance mobile platform, then calibrates motion, aligns timing and removes demonstrator hands from observations. TouchTwin, November 13, uses head/wrist RGB-D and an 880-taxel glove to reconstruct contact-aware simulated demonstrations. Its reported 20/20 held-out replay successes are simulation results. The collection implication is specific: pose video does not directly measure contact force, and training/deployment alignment needs explicit processing. HALO · TouchTwin

    MiniPose explores lower-cost real RGB-D capture using miniature industrial pipe replicas, while Fog-FWL adds waveform-level fog data and matched synthetic training. These are research approaches and datasets, not commissioned collection calls. MiniPose · Fog-FWL

    Original date Paper Finding / limit
    2026-11-13 Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots PEAR trains a 27B reasoning model with robot-use reinforcement learning entirely in simulation without demonstrations; 60% success in the authors’ real dual-arm trials versus zero for the base model.
    2026-11-13 TouchTwin: Scaling Human Touch Data via Real-to-Sim-to-Real TouchTwin uses head/wrist RGB-D and an 880-taxel glove to ground simulated replays in measured human touch; replay success results are predominantly simulation tests.
    2026-11-13 Beyond Prediction Gain: LASR as a Metric for Action-Grounded Latents in World Models LASR audits whether latents predict actions and physical state changes beyond state or task shortcuts using episode-held-out data; predictive support is not proof of causality.
    2026-11-13 Joint World Action Models through Differentiable Trajectory Optimization WATO uses differentiable trajectory optimization during training, then generates and ranks candidates at deployment; average success rises from 79.9% to 91.7% over seven visual-control environments.
    2026-11-13 VLAN: Vision-Language Accessible Navigation VLAN describes 1,000 scenes, 20,000 trajectories and 160,000 keyframes with embodiment-specific accessibility labels; dataset/code availability is author-reported, not tested here.
    2026-11-12 HALO: Human-Aligned Data Collection for WhoLe-body MObile Manipulation HALO aligns kinesthetic whole-body demonstrations with deployment through calibration, temporal alignment and hand masking; up to 2.9x faster collection in the authors’ comparisons.
    2026-11-12 ActWorld-JEPA: Action-Conditioned JEPA World Models Meet Latent Planning for End-to-End Autonomous Driving ActWorld-JEPA combines latent future prediction and candidate reranking; NAVSIM v1 selector calibration uses aggregate NAVTEST feedback, limiting clean holdout interpretation.
    2026-11-12 Fog-FWL: A Large-Scale Full-Waveform LiDAR Dataset for Recovering Fog-Hidden Objects Fog-FWL proposes 7,527 real foggy full-waveform LiDAR frames and 12,000 synthetic frames with aligned labels; public dataset/simulator release is promised, not verified.
    2026-11-11 MiniPose: Miniature-Based Real Data Collection for 6D Pose Estimation of Large Industrial Pipe Segments MiniPose combines at most 1,000 real miniature RGB-D images per pipe type with 10,000 synthetic images; tests two pose estimators, not arbitrary industrial objects.
    2026-11-11 Fast Inference-Time Policy Refinement via Action-Affine World Models A²WM learns local action sensitivities offline and refines with a quadratic program; 3.8ms mean overhead in four simulated tasks excludes base-policy sampling.
    2026-11-11 Bind-WAM: Binding-Uncertainty-Guided Selective Future Imagination in World Action Models Bind-WAM pseudo-labels object-reference mismatch, invisibility and identity drift from demonstrations to train a router for selective future imagination.
    2026-11-11 What World Model Does Active Object Search Need? A Study under Imperfect Detection BAMOS tests belief updates and detector errors in simulated active object search; maintaining target belief matters more as detection errors grow.
    2026-11-11 Driving World Models Move the Camera, Not the Scene Driving-world-model evaluation separates camera motion from actual scene response; retimed logs and stereo views provide real counterfactual references.
    2026-11-11 Anytime-Valid Empirical Likelihood Inference from LLM Annotations with Adaptive Human Auditing AAELI allocates human audits using LLM outputs and estimates human-label means with anytime-valid confidence sequences; experiments are not robotics-specific QC validation.
    2026-11-11 AFTER: A World Model with Adaptive Fast–Slow Timescales for Error-Driven Retention AFTER-WM adapts to persistent dynamics changes with fast and slow corrections; five simulated datasets plus real UAV data, with code/data release still promised.
    2026-11-09 Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation LingBot-Touch learns shared representations across magnetic, piezoresistive and visuotactile sensors; source is Apache 2.0, while linked model weights require access permission.
