### Data collection

- **Fewer than 200 examples to onboard a new robot body is the day's structural signal for collection economics.** DeepMind says On-Device 2 inherits the motion-transfer techniques from Gemini Robotics 1.5 and adapts to embodiments with drastically different shapes, sensors and degrees of freedom (demonstrated on Dexmate, SO101 and Trossen platforms) in a few hours of adaptation time. When 200 demonstrations onboard a robot, each demonstration carries roughly 0.5% of the outcome, and a bad one is no longer noise that averages out. Small-N regimes price validation, not hours. [DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/)
- **KAI (arXiv 2607.24493, Shanghai AI Lab, submitted July 27) hits 82.9% average success across six manipulation tasks using half the demonstration data, by injecting a kinematic-structure prior into policy learning.** The action-agnostic design also co-trains on human interaction videos, holding over 70% success under heavy visual distraction. Same direction as the DeepMind number from the academic side: the demo count keeps shrinking, and human video keeps substituting for robot data. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24493)
- **S2A2 (arXiv 2607.26047, submitted July 28) introduces acoustic-aware manipulation tasks where the robot must localize and identify sound sources to act, and a framework fusing visual features with spatial audio across ACT, Diffusion Policy, VQ-BeT and π₀.** Audio has been the throwaway channel on every capture rig. If policies start consuming spatial sound for target selection and contact events, microphone placement and audio sync move from an afterthought to a spec line. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26047)

### Data annotation & validation

- **DeepMind shipped ASIMOV-Agentic alongside Gemini Robotics 2, a benchmark for agentic safety orchestration: whether the reasoning agent refuses unsafe tool calls from a VLA, predicts task feasibility, and proactively asks for human intervention when uncertain.** Released openly on Hugging Face with a safety technical report. Safety benchmarks are acceptance tests by another name, and every dimension a lab formalizes eventually shows up in what buyers require delivered data and deployed systems to prove. [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/asimov_agentic/blob/main/README.md), [Safety report](https://storage.googleapis.com/deepmind-media/gemini-robotics/Gemini-Robotics-2-Safety.pdf)

### Embedded AI systems

- **Gemini Robotics On-Device 2 is the strongest on-device VLA release to date: DeepMind's most efficient VLA optimized to run locally on robot hardware, natively multi-embodiment, positioned explicitly for deployments without network connectivity or latency budgets.** The VLA and on-device models are early-access only; ER 2 is live on Google AI Studio and in private preview on the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. The frontier lab on-device tier now exists as a product line, not a research demo. [DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/), [Google developer blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/gemini-robotics-er-2/)
- **πR² (arXiv 2607.26055, CMU, submitted July 28) makes action-chunking flow policies reactive in real time: fine-tuned onto GR00T-N1.7 on a real xArm6 + XHand, it replans closed-loop ~4× faster than the base policy, ~25Hz on a single A5000, acting on a fresh observation every 40ms.** Up to +30% real-world success over the strongest baseline. The trick is splitting fast proprioception from slow vision-language conditioning and emitting actions in one denoising step. Big-backbone VLAs reacting at control-loop rates on commodity GPUs is exactly what the edge-inference stack has been waiting on. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26055)

### Robotics & humanoids

- **Gemini Robotics 2 in detail: one checkpoint controls Apptronik's Apollo 2 with 22-DoF SharpaWave five-finger hands, Apollo 2 with Inspire hands, and a Franka Duo with Robotiq grippers.** Whole-body tasks land at 45.7-76.3% success, gripper dexterity at 74.2-89.6% (precise insertion the best at 89.6%), and multi-finger dexterity stays hard: 36% screwing a bulb, 32% using a dustpan, 44% tying a trash bag, against 92% unscrewing a bulb. ER 2 runs multi-minute tasks involving hundreds of decisions, knows when tasks begin and end, and coordinates multiple different robots on one workflow. Publishing the failure numbers alongside the demo reel is the most credible thing about the release. Partners named: Apptronik, Boston Dynamics, Agile Robots. [DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/google-unveils-gemini-ai-for-robots-struggling-with-dexterity)
- **The Robot Report gathered industry reaction to the FCC import ban (July 29): the consensus is that it may help US robot makers near-term but raises hardware costs and slows research, since Unitree platforms are the default lab and startup testbed.** Adds named-expert texture to the structural story covered Tuesday. [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/industry-reacts-fcc-ban-u-s-imports-new-humanoid-quadruped-robots/)
- **Transformer Transformer (arXiv 2607.25798, Stanford: Huy Ha, C. Karen Liu, Shuran Song, submitted July 28) generates whole robot designs conditioned on target motions from human demonstrations, one diffusion transformer spanning wheeled bimanual, quadruped and humanoid design spaces.** They fabricated an optimized ALOHA variant that cut tracking error by over 70% versus the original design. Robot morphology becoming a learned output of human motion data is a new consumer for ego capture nobody prices yet. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25798)
