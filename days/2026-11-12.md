### Data collection

**Ego video is being used in several distinct ways.** November 10's Ego Guidance paper studies action-free human video as a visual-state learning signal alongside robot actions. November 9's SWAM instead studies a roughly **47,000-hour** mixed pool of general video, ego, UMI and robot demonstrations, including language-annotation quality. Its real-robot result covers only three tasks. Neither proves all unlabeled video is equally useful or creates an open buyer mandate. [Ego Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13685) · [SWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12870)

**Preserve the information downstream models need.** The November 10 Ego2Robot survey separates four learning routes; DexSeed transfers and expands human hand–object interactions into simulated robot demonstrations; EgoHMP uses head motion plus ego images. Practical inference: define synchronization, task labels, interactions and camera metadata against the intended training recipe before optimizing collection volume. [Survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13610) · [DexSeed](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14092) · [EgoHMP](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13654)

**Capture QA lesson:** a small SO-101 experiment changed room illumination 11.5-fold, but auto-exposure made the actual image change much smaller. The result is not evidence that lighting never matters; it argues for recording exposure/white-balance settings and image statistics alongside lux. [November 10 study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13810)
