### Data collection

**Catch-up, October 22 — Maxinsights connects collection, QA and tactile capture.** The company reports 2M captured hours and 1.5M model-ready hours. MaxLoop provides same-day operator feedback; MaxGlove is being developed with Genesis AI, targeting 1M force-data hours in 2027. These are company figures and a future target, not audited production or an external order. [Company release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261022672609/en/)

**Revised older research — MEgoVista.** Originally September 15, revised October 22: calibrated stereo supports metric hand/head reconstruction in unprepared environments, with independent optical motion-capture evaluation. The companion MEgoDeploy GPU pipeline reports a 14.05× throughput improvement. This is evidence for richer annotation from capture, not proof that arbitrary monocular footage meets the same requirements. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16684)

**October 22 capture example — robotic feeding.** A demonstration pipeline records spoon position/orientation with motion capture, segments scooping and feeding, and combines RGB-D/AprilTag scene registration with a Kinova arm. The result is a feasibility experiment, not a clinical or large-scale deployment claim. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31020)

### Data annotation & validation

**MaxLoop's relevance is earlier rejection and correction during capture, rather than waiting until dataset delivery.** Performance claims remain vendor-reported; no independent head-to-head audit was recovered.

**NavBind, October 21; revised October 22:** 3,252 paired route-fork contexts hold the navigation state fixed while changing instructions. The evaluation shows why goal completion alone can conceal weak instruction following. **Failure-Boundary Learning, September 5; revised October 22:** real demonstrations and digital-twin rollouts localise where progress becomes failure, then shape the policy around that boundary. Both support testing counterfactuals and recovery cases, not just counting successful episodes. [NavBind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29203) · [Failure boundaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06114)

**FluxVLA Engine, September 15; revised October 22:** shared dataset, evaluation and robot interfaces connect simulation, human takeovers, correction collection and reward annotation. This is an engineering platform contribution, not a new dataset purchase. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17210)
