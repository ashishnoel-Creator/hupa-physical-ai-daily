### Data annotation & validation

**November 25 — failure intervals and rejected attempts are useful evidence.** FailPoint-X records **2,823 takeover attempts**, including **2,400 accepted recoveries**, and evaluates from matched failure states. Its reported recovery gains coexist with losses from nominal task starts. It supports preserving failure context, rejected attempts and held-out normal-task tests when assessing recovery datasets. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33769)

**Also newly discovered:** BOP-Refer combines machine-generated, human-verified language with pose-derived spatial labels; AgenTAP reconnects occluded tracks for EgoTAP-4D; a failure-driven driving framework allocates synthetic data to measured weaknesses. These are distinct evaluation/annotation methods, not interchangeable claims that automated labels need no review. Original links and limits below. Checks of major annotation vendors did not establish another fresh, source-verified collection-partner mandate.

### Robotics & humanoids

**November 25 — HoloBrain-0.5 technical report.** The authors describe over **10,000 hours across 15 embodiment categories**, combining robot, simulated and human-manipulation data. Standardized joint axes, frames and tool-centre points, plus calibrated camera observations, support a shared representation. Its task-specific progress and success metrics should not be read as one universal robot-performance score. [Original report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34449)

**Newly found application research:** the forklift study adapts a pretrained policy with a small real-data component and simulation; Nav4Manip couples approach positioning to dexterous manipulation. SEEK and RegHarness study retaining and governing procedural execution knowledge across attempts. These remain research systems with the benchmark limits summarized below.

### Research papers worth a look

**26 newly discovered preprints**, all originally **November 25** except PTQ4WAM (**November 23, revised November 25**). These are author-reported research findings; release promises are not treated as completed downloads or commercial licences. All are now in the [searchable library](../papers.html).

| Paper | What it adds and limits |
|---|---|
| [Lab2Sim: Agentic Generation of Interactive Laboratory Equipment from Operation Videos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33705) | Converts ego or multi-view laboratory operation videos into editable simulation assets, using separate evidence, construction, verification and integration stages. Research result, not a collection order. |
| [M2-WAM: Efficient Hierarchical Memory Across Modalities and Timescales for World-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33710) | Separates visual and executed-action memory at short and long timescales; reports bounded online storage and gains on memory-dependent tasks. Benchmarks do not establish universal long-horizon reliability. |
| [FailPoint-X: A Policy-Matched, Failure-Anchored Benchmark for Offline VLA Post-Training from Human Takeovers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33769) | Logs 2,823 human takeover attempts and 2,400 accepted recoveries. Recovery cloning improves held-out failure recovery but can damage nominal-start performance; both tests matter. |
| [Integration of Vision-Language-Action Control for Autonomous Forklift Unloading with Limited Real-World Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33880) | Forklift adaptation uses 9.38 hours of simulation and 0.8 hours of real demonstrations, tested in 72 trials. Narrow task evidence, not proof that all industrial collection needs little real data. |
| [HoloBrain-0.5 Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34449) | Pretraining combines over 10,000 hours across 15 embodiment categories with standardized kinematics and calibrated cameras. Reported progress and success metrics are task-specific, not interchangeable. |
| [SkySearcher: High-Altitude Onboard VLM Reasoning for Aerial Semantic Search](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34485) | Combines onboard aerial VLM reasoning with a lightweight probabilistic map; includes simulation evaluation and a drone deployment demonstration, without proving fleet-scale reliability. |
| [Quantization for Action, Execution for Deployment: Joint Precision and Execution Selection for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30195) | PTQ4WAM jointly selects weight precision and execution routes under memory, latency and task-evaluation limits. Original November 23, revised November 25; code release is promised. |
| [JOVE ICE: Intent Guided Cloud Edge Device Collaboration for AI Glasses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33735) | Routes AI-glasses requests between glasses, phone and cloud by intent; evaluates latency and token reductions. Useful workload-design evidence, not a standalone hardware launch. |
| [A Failure-Driven Data Flywheel for Post-Training Vision-Language-Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34698) | Targets synthetic scenario generation at measured VLA failure conditions. Driving tests expose the difference between anticipatory slowing and braking after a pedestrian appears; real-vehicle sample is small. |
| [Who Is Ego? Scaling Cross-View Camera Wearer Identification and Segmentation beyond Humans](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34489) | FindEgo-1M covers 1.16 million real/simulated ego-exo pairs across 12 wearer types. Studies wearer identification and segmentation; data/code/models are promised, not verified downloaded. |
| [BOP-Refer: Benchmark for Monocular 3D and 2D Object Grounding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34862) | Pairs nine BOP datasets with generated, human-verified referring expressions and pose-derived 2D/3D boxes. Reported monocular depth errors remain much larger than 2D grounding errors. |
| [GlassGround: GUI Grounding Beyond Native Screenshots with Egocentric Wearable Vision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33760) | GlassGround pairs screenshots with wearable-camera views in 3,250 tasks across four devices. Benchmark exposes a large camera-domain gap; paper links dataset and code. |
| [Event-WAM: Learning to Act with Event-Derived Futures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33917) | Uses event-like changes derived from RGB as auxiliary future supervision, requiring no event camera at inference. Robustness benefits vary by benchmark. |
| [WorldInteract: Controllable Streaming Video Generation for Multi-Material Interactions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34730) | Separates material behavior from contact response, training on 30,000 simulated non-interaction videos across 22 materials. Video-control gains are not robot-policy validation. |
| [Tracking Any Point with Agentic Visual Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34943) | AgenTAP reasons over point identity and occlusion, reconnecting tracks to build EgoTAP-4D from long ego videos. Human-agreement findings are sampled, not a guarantee of error-free labels. |
| [RegHarness: Regulating What Code-as-Policy Agents Keep from Steps to Skills](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33739) | RegHarness evaluates proposed program steps and governs reuse of revised skills. Simulation improvements depend on controlled reset/replay and author-enabled updates. |
| [SEEK: Self-Evolution through Execution Knowledge for Agentic Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33915) | SEEK retains executable procedures and measured action effects between attempts with a fixed base model; evaluates improvement and fewer model calls on RoboDojo. |
| [Targeted Adaptation of World-Action Models via Interaction and Motion Heads](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33963) | HeadLight adapts interaction and motion attention heads using 1.1% of parameters; reports transfer and real-robot tests. This is targeted policy adaptation, not reduced capture requirements by default. |
| [Hetero-Associative Visuomotor Memory: Soft Recall as a Base for Learned Residuals in Few-Demonstration Imitation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34014) | HAVM retrieves and corrects demonstrations for rapid few-shot imitation; gains depend on diverse human demonstrations and weaken on dense/scripted data. Broad unseen-task generalization is a tradeoff. |
| [M2AdaptVLA: Multimodal Memory-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Model with Predictive Test-Time Adaptation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34560) | Combines visual and force/torque history with online prediction-based adaptation. Contact-sensitive tasks motivate synchronized wrench measurements alongside video. |
| [Nav4Manip: From Navigation to Dexterous Mobile Manipulation through Co-Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34655) | Nav4Manip couples approach positioning and dexterous manipulation; introduces Nav-N-Manip and paired trajectories with affordance supervision. Includes reported real-world evaluations. |
| [DriftWAM: Faster World-Action Models via Action-Aware Video Drifting](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33797) | DriftWAM distills video/action generation into fewer steps using action-aware matching; compares against other distillation methods at matched inference speed. |
| [Dynamic Forcing: Learning Action-Grounded Sparse Dynamic Representations with Dense Future Guidance for World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33799) | CompactWAM retains current context and sparse dynamic tokens, avoiding dense future generation at inference. Reports benchmarks and real-world manipulation, not independent deployment validation. |
| [FoundSurface: Feedforward Ray-Based 3D Scene Surface Reconstruction from Unposed Images](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34128) | FoundSurface reconstructs continuous surfaces from unposed images using pointmap priors and ray-based refinement; evaluates cross-dataset generalization for scene reconstruction. |
| [ProManO: Beyond Sim(3) for Metric Upgrade of Feed-Forward Geometry with Visual-Inertial Anchors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34480) | ProManO upgrades feed-forward geometry with calibrated visual-inertial anchors and uncertainty weighting. Explains why a simple similarity alignment can leave depth-dependent error. |
| [Measuring the Value of One Shared Image at Robot Handoffs in Vision-and-Language Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33865) | Controlled navigation handoffs test one image alongside executed actions. Benefits are measured against a frozen receiver; route-specificity and some controlled-start questions remain unresolved. |
