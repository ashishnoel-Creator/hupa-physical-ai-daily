### Data collection

**October 19 catch-up · Ropedia W0 / Xp-Data.** The authors describe **10,000 hours of human egocentric data and 3,000 hours of robot data**, with camera poses, projected skeletons, manual keypoint correction and systematic QA. Human pretraining improves their cross-embodiment evaluations. This is evidence for synchronized geometry and corrected annotations alongside footage, not proof that those hours were externally bought or that all assets are openly downloadable. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26855)

**October 20 · lower-cost capture and operator design.** A [head-camera UMI glove](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27622) uses printed fiducials and an electronics-free glove to recover pose and jaw labels from the same moving-camera frames. Authors report a $122 capture camera and $22 glove; those are prototype component costs, not a production collection budget. [LocoPilot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27998) instead records synchronized on-robot base/arm actions with single-operator bilateral teleoperation. [PointHand's operator study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27907) finds that what novices see during teleoperation changes subsequent policy learning; its evaluation is simulated.

**October 18–20 catch-up · broader collection modalities.** [AQUAACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25534) pairs underwater images with joystick and inertial data, separating **1.9 real hours from 17.9 simulated hours**. [Multi-view latent action pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27794) studies synchronized views for recovering action information. [Gaze2HOI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27877) and [EgoACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27946) tackle intention and action disentanglement in first-person video. These suggest distinct dataset requirements, not interchangeable video-hour specifications.

It is a software experiment, not a buyer request. [Original repository](https://github.com/abhishek0093/roboreview) · [Author's Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/robotics/comments/1xalost/built_an_open_source_ecosystem_to_label_factory/)

**October 19–20 · reward quality needs its own tests.** [RoboValue](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26663) covers task state, progress, recovery and consistency over 15 simulated and 20 real tasks; good outcome scores can hide weak recovery judgments. [RoboJudge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27782) evaluates generated embodied video against human ratings. [RoboCasa-Shift](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27861), [failure attribution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27277) and [HATCH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27485) address distribution shifts, corruption causes and instrumented manipulation evidence respectively. HATCH is a proposed benchmark protocol, not an external certification.

### World models & sim-to-real

**October 20 · Cosmos3-H-Dreams.** NVIDIA describes an action-conditioned surgical world model using synchronized clinical video and CMR Versius kinematics, plus separately trained distilled students for lower latency. Its 10 Hz training alignment is distinct from runtime performance. The supported capture/tool conditions are narrow; the authors explicitly separate visual realism from biomechanical accuracy. Released checkpoints do not imply that clinical training footage or its rights are open. [Technical release](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/cosmos3-h-dreams)

**October 19–20 · controllability and contact matter.** [Action Influence Maximization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27387) finds that a small actively collected set can improve simulated world-model controllability beyond much larger expert-only data. [Physical Canvas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28613) combines vision and tactile observations; its four-task real-world study links more consistent simulated outcomes to policy improvement. [HuTact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27995) and [Touch-JEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28519) use human hand-object video to reduce tactile-supervision requirements, not eliminate the need for grounded tactile measurements in all settings.

[SceneRig](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28585) iterates scene reconstruction inside a physics simulator; [AEON](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28631) combines video generation with persistent 4D geometry; [UniWALM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28626) jointly generates language, actions and video, including a textual memory loop. These pursue different forms of consistency. No shared headline metric demonstrates that one solves physical simulation generally.
