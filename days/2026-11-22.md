### Data collection

**November 19 catch-up: capture metadata is part of the product.** The GO1 utility logs joint states, IMU, foot forces and gyroscope data without requiring ROS expertise. Its 51-recording experiment exposes how windows from the same recording can leak into both training and evaluation. The approximately 38% held-out-recording result is still above ten-class chance, but far below the approximately 99% leaky result. A practical collection specification should retain session/recording IDs and make the split policy explicit. [Original](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26146)

**Adjacent capture methods:** AGE-Capture chooses UAV viewpoints and stopping conditions across sensing configurations, tested on ten simulated and nine real scenes. The radar/event-camera/IMU calibration paper estimates spatial and timing alignment using a trusted motion reference. These suggest additional deliverables worth understanding: coverage/completion evidence and calibration provenance. They do not establish HuPa's ownership of UAVs, robots or these sensors. [AGE-Capture, November 19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26048) · [Calibration, November 19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26083)

### Data annotation & validation

**Three November 18–19 additions:** EgoPass-Bench asks which objects constrain a camera wearer's passage; Online Mistake Detection assesses instruction-conditioned procedural errors as video arrives; RefreshGrad changes demonstration-window sampling during training. Together they distinguish useful labels, trustworthy evaluation units and training value. These are research results, not vendor procurement notices. [EgoPass](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24995) · [Mistake detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25567) · [RefreshGrad](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25398)

**Proposed collection QA:** separate real recording provenance from generated imagery, preserve unsuccessful attempts, and define whether an error can be identified online or only after task completion. Benchmark-specific F1 scores should not become promised production acceptance rates.
