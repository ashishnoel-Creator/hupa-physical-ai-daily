### Data collection

**Newly discovered Preload capture stack:** synchronized first-person video, tactile-glove contact/pressure and EMG target skilled manipulation. The founder reports **350+ hours** collected in non-surgical settings and hospital LOIs; these are company claims, not an external procurement volume or validated surgical deployment. The explicit partner route is above. [YC founder account](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XWg-preload-internet-scale-touch-data-for-physical-ai)

**November 18 catch-up · WACO and Homeward:** both examine camera changes that break policies trained on fixed views. WACO addresses camera and robot-base shifts using RGB-D, kinematics and a static-world anchor; Homeward adapts a frozen policy's observations using estimated scene geometry and rendered robot links. Practical implication: record camera/base configuration and changes as collection metadata; generic geometric correction can itself damage performance. [WACO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24360) · [Homeward](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24455)

**November 18 · ART / HPAD:** 2,137 objects, 80 categories, 13 action primitives and over 27,000 object-action pairs join human affordances to simulation-validated robot labels. This is a reminder that plausible human contact labels are not automatically executable by a robot's end effector. Dataset licence/downloads were not tested. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24182)

### Data annotation & validation

**November 18 · LAGR** reports **2.68M scored grasp annotations** over 100,911 image-task pairs across 17 categories, separating feasible grasping from task-appropriate grasping. Its Franka demonstration is bounded research evidence. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23858)

**World-model QA needs interventions:** latent safety-filter conservatism measures how many safe actions a representation rejects; the driving semantic-forecasting study tests whether changing actions actually changes predictions. SibPO addresses outcome-distribution collapse during video-model post-training. These are different validation problems, not interchangeable “realism” scores. [Safety filters](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23685) · [Action dependence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24840) · [SibPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24512)
