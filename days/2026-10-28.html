    Data collection

    October 27 · OpenJaws makes localization quality explicit. Stereo metric trajectories and an open gripper interface target the drift/scale problems that corrupt action labels in handheld demonstrations. Its UMI-Loc-Bench-v1 evaluates localization, and the authors report downstream physical policy execution. Commercially permissive release terms are claimed by the abstract; exact repository licences and fabrication readiness were not audited here. Paper

    Contact, alignment and corrective data have distinct value. CARD unifies video, motion capture and tactile gloves around contact targets; forces inferred from plain video remain predictions. ActiveAlign uses hand motions and touch to correct calibration and object placement. Adaptive Residual Correction uses sparse human corrections to constrain subsequent learning, rather than simply copying them. These are different collection products with different hardware and QA requirements. CARD · ActiveAlign · Correction study

    Data annotation & validation

    More hours are not a sufficient quality metric. The October 27 SO-101 study compares 12–200 demonstrations over 1,340 physical episodes. Removing idle lead-in changed whether one policy started moving; fine-tuning recipe substantially changed another. Small evaluation blocks were noisy. Its single task and fixed recipes limit generalization, but it gives concrete reasons to preserve temporal trim decisions, training recipes and failure-stage labels. Study

    October 26 · SOMA finds a body-understanding gap. Its 1,632 human-verified questions test configuration, motion dynamics and timing. Best tested model accuracy is 50%, versus 68.5% for humans; the authors identify laterality and counting errors. Video-only human performance is itself imperfect. Benchmark

    October 27 work also separates task success from motion waste, and quality attributes such as speed/smoothness from binary completion. These are candidate evaluation methods, not established universal acceptance criteria. Constraint-referenced evaluation · Quality-controllable policies

    Embedded AI systems

    Reduce inference work without discarding useful training supervision.
