### Data collection

- **Catch-up, never covered here: the N0 pair out of NeoteAI and Fudan's TEAI team (arXiv 2607.23782 and 2607.23783, announced July 28, trending on Hugging Face August 3) claims the first VLA pretrained on tactile data at scale, built on NeoData, their own large visuo-tactile corpus, plus NeoForce, a unified force-based representation spanning 6 embodiments and 450 tasks.** The standing thread in this section has been touch getting inferred from RGB rather than sensed. N0 is the counter-bet: capture the tactile channel at corpus scale and pretrain on it directly, with the collection hardware and the dataset as the differentiating asset. If N0's nine-for-nine real-robot task wins hold up, tactile capture moves from nice-to-have to a spec'd channel in collection contracts. [N0-VTLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23782), [N0-TWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23783)

### Embedded AI systems

- **Catch-up, never covered here: Recti-Q (arXiv 2607.18540, University of Maryland, Ming Lin's group, IROS 2026) names a "quantization-induced robustness gap": 4-bit post-training-quantized vision backbones hold their clean accuracy but degrade badly under distribution shift, exactly the regime deployed robots live in.** The fix is a tiny LoRA adapter, as small as 6 KB, that patches the quantized model's feature space and can be shipped over the air to a deployed fleet. On-device inference keeps pulling perception onto cheap edge silicon, and this is the first clean result showing what that costs in out-of-distribution robustness and how little it takes to buy it back. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18540)

### Research papers worth a look

- **N0-VTLA: Scaling Vision-Tactile-Language-Action Model with Latent Tactile Tokens (arXiv 2607.23782, NeoteAI + Fudan TEAI, announced July 28).** Full read in collection above. Also ships ALTER, an advantage-conditioned offline-RL recipe for improving already-deployed policies. Wins all nine real-robot NeoReal tasks. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23782)
- **N0-TWAM: Scaling Tactile-Native World-Action Model for Contact-Rich Manipulation (arXiv 2607.23783, same teams).** The companion world-action model: predicts future vision and future contact jointly through the NeoForce representation, with an asymmetric Mixture-of-Transformers for real-time control across 6 embodiments and 450 tasks. Touch as a first-class predicted channel inside the world model, from teams that captured the tactile corpus themselves. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23783)
- **WorldScape Policy 2.0 (arXiv 2607.18840, SJTU and Tsinghua-linked authors, July 22 mailing, back-filled).** A steerable world action model with reasoning-augmented memory, and the part that matters here is ManipEvent-5M: roughly 5M event segments with aligned actions, subtask captions, goal images and video demonstrations. Event-grounded pretraining data is an annotation-heavy product, and 5M segments is a statement about what scale the labs now assume. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18840)
- **Recti-Q: Feature-Space Rectification for OOD-Robust Quantized Perception in Edge Robotics (arXiv 2607.18540, UMD, July 22 mailing, back-filled).** Full read in embedded above. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18540)
