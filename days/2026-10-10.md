### Data collection

**October 8 catch-up: capture physical properties and viewpoint diversity.** [How Firm Should a Grasp Be?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11221) combines measured produce geometry, mass, elasticity and friction with visuotactile estimation and real gripper tests. [VersaCamVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12451) tests variable camera configurations and unseen poses. Practical inference: a collection specification should distinguish task footage, calibration/pose metadata and physical-property measurements; they are different deliverables.

**Recovery-focused demonstrations:** [RESETTLE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12185), October 8, uses action disagreement to trigger same-task demonstration retrieval. [TAPNAV](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10748), October 7, adds tactile observations to navigation. Both broaden the useful capture space beyond clean successful RGB clips. Neither paper constitutes an order for data.

### Data annotation & validation

**October 7–8 catch-up:** [NegaAlign/NegaBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11952) addresses negated instructions; [ARC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12386) builds action-causal traces on DROID; [instance-anchored interaction evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12157) preserves object identity when interpreting pointing and handling. WatchAct's evaluation is symbolic execution, not a physical-robot success claim.

[Traceable World State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12033) detects injected trace corruption and supports deterministic replay in simulation. That establishes tamper evidence, not correct physical observations. [iAm.md](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10962), [SafeInferCom](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11223) and [Contextual Safety Filter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12467) respectively study grounded records, plan verification and constrained motion. None provides blanket safety certification. No new verified collection-linked annotation tender emerged from the checked vendor sources.

### Research papers worth a look

All 18 below are newly added **catch-up**, originally submitted October 7–8. The current cs.RO listing still showed Friday October 9; no Saturday batch was inferred. Abstracts and dates were individually opened. Results are preprints and author-reported; code, dataset downloads and benchmark reproduction were not independently audited.

| Submitted | Paper | Why it matters / evidence limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-10-07 | [TAPNAV: Humanoid Navigation through Tactile Active Perception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10748) | Combines tactile contact, IMU and odometry for uncertainty-aware navigation; simulation and Unitree G1 demonstrations. Contact observations can complement vision. |
| 2026-10-07 | [Skill-SLM: Agent Skill-driven Small Language Models for Reliable Robot Operation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10812) | Trains a small onboard language model against a skill grammar with synthetic supervision; UAV and ground-vehicle evaluations, not a general hardware latency guarantee. |
| 2026-10-07 | [iAm.md: Robot Skill Self-Assessment through Agentic Introspection for Unknown Open-Vocabulary Domains](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10962) | Persistent object records ground plans in deployment evidence. Evaluation uses simulated TIAGo; this is not proof of physical deployment. |
| 2026-10-08 | [How Firm Should a Grasp Be?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11221) | Pairs produce shape, mass, elasticity and friction measurements with visuotactile force estimation and real gripper tests. Material-property labels matter alongside video. |
| 2026-10-08 | [SafeInferCom: Safe Inference-Time Compute via Verifier-Guided Mid-Generation Intervention for Robotic Task Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11223) | A formal verifier corrects intermediate plans, evaluated in VirtualHome and a real arm demonstration; this does not certify overall robot safety. |
| 2026-10-08 | [PathTime-VLA: Path-Time Decoupling for Factorized Post-Training of Vision-Language-Action Policies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11771) | Separates geometric path from execution speed. Reports 39–52% shorter successful-trial completion times, with success 58/60 versus 57/60. |
| 2026-10-08 | [Tell Robot What Not to Do: A Negation Understanding Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11952) | Trains negated instructions from existing demonstrations with image-language supervision. Benchmark-specific gains support including explicit negative constraints in labels. |
| 2026-10-08 | [REACT: Rolling Denoising and Dual Decoupling for Reactive Robot Control with VLA Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12007) | Decouples sensing, reasoning, diffusion and execution through a rolling action buffer. RoboTwin2 and real bimanual dynamic tests do not establish arbitrary edge-device latency. |
| 2026-10-08 | [Humanoid World Action Model With Joint State--Action Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12026) | Predicts post-execution body state as well as reference actions to address the action-execution gap; evaluated on three LimX OLI tasks. |
| 2026-10-08 | [Traceable World State: A Provenance-Aware State Representation and Deterministic Replay Framework for Robotic Systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12033) | Hash-linked provenance and deterministic replay detect injected corruption in simulated G1 traces. Tamper evidence cannot establish physical truth or correct labels. |
| 2026-10-08 | [Instance-anchored interaction evidence: Grounding robot plans in human pointing and handling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12157) | Tracks object identity and pointing/handling evidence. WatchAct evaluates symbolic execution over 1,255 requests, not actual physical task completion. |
| 2026-10-08 | [RESETTLE: Robotic Recovery through Disagreement-Triggered Retrieval and Efficient Corrective Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12185) | Action disagreement triggers retrieval of same-task demonstrations for recovery. Reported latency savings concern its monitor/recovery component, not whole-robot latency. |
| 2026-10-08 | [ARC: A Reasoning Recipe for Robot Foundation Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12386) | Automatically builds action-causal reasoning traces on DROID. Existing demonstrations gain richer supervision; task-specific gains do not mean annotation is cost-free. |
| 2026-10-08 | [LeWAM: A JEPA World Action Model with Diffusion-Steering-Based MPC](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12407) | Joint latent world/action training supports policy learning; planning over raw actions can exploit model error, while policy-noise planning helps in its experiments. |
| 2026-10-08 | [RoboRSI: Stable, efficient, and reusable robot self-evolution in complex real-world environments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12424) | Revises a skill tree with contracts and human objectives/corrections. Cleanup experiments are human-steered, not fully autonomous recursive improvement. |
| 2026-10-08 | [VersaCamVLA: Camera-Configurable VLA Policies for Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12451) | Maps variable camera views to fixed latent tokens; simulated and real unseen-pose evaluations make camera diversity and pose metadata relevant collection variables. |
| 2026-10-08 | [SpatialHarness: Test-Time Spatial Scaffolding for Fine Robotic Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12457) | Synchronizes an online simulation and adds virtual views to a frozen policy across four real tasks. Results depend on reconstruction quality and task scope. |
| 2026-10-08 | [CSF: Contextual Safety Filtering for Motion Generators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12467) | Filters generated motion using contextual references and barrier-function constraints; Unitree G1 demonstrations and benchmark risk reductions are not safety certification. |
