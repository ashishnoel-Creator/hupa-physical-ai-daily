### Robotics & humanoids

- **Delta and United have banned humanoid and animal-like robots from passenger flights, joining Southwest, which moved first in May after a robot named Stewie got its own seat on a Las Vegas to Dallas flight.** United bars them from carry-on, checked luggage and cargo outright; Delta calls its restriction temporary and clarifies the machines do not qualify as assistive devices. The stated reason is multi-cell lithium-ion packs capable of thermal runaway, plus articulated limbs that defeat standard screening and stowage. Worth logging because this is the first US consumer-transport rule written specifically about humanoids, and it arrived through battery safety desks, not AI policy. The mundane regulatory surface (batteries, screening, insurance) will keep arriving ahead of the autonomy debates. [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/airlines-ban-humanoid-robots-battery-safety-delta-southwest-united/), [The Hill](https://thehill.com/policy/transportation/5998226-delta-united-airlines-ban-humanoid-animal-robots-lithium-battery-risk/)
