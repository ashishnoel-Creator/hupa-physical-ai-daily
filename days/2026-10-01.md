### Data collection

**September 30 · Ego4WAM isolates several properties of human data.** With a fixed world-action backbone, the authors vary human/robot alignment, duration, task diversity, action supervision and training-stage use. They report that aligned demonstrations reduce robot-data requirements and improve generalisation; video-only supervision can also help before video-action training. Evaluation includes real robots and RoboDojo. The abstract does not provide one universal improvement percentage or exchange rate between human and robot hours. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40341)

**September 30 · IronMind uses camera-space actions to avoid explicit body retargeting.** Its pretraining budgets span 250–10,000 hours of heterogeneous human and robot data. After post-training, the largest model reaches **55.0%** across six real-robot tasks with unseen objects, affordances and prompts, versus at most **11.7%** for budgets up to 5,000 hours and **5.0%** without pretraining. This is a striking result within one setup, not a universal 10,000-hour threshold or a claim that all those hours were purchased human video. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39403)

**September 29, missed overlap · DYNA 2.1 makes human/robot compatibility explicit.** Its Unified Robot Representation records wrist, elbow, chest and footprint poses in a shared local frame, using whichever components are available. The technical release connects human capture, controller training and reuse of older robot data. This is a useful buyer-specification reference, without an open supplier invitation. Its workflow claims are discussed below. [Technical release](https://www.dyna.co/dyna-2.1)

**September 30 · TacEx collects contact-rich experience through tactile curiosity.** The method directs exploration toward uncertainty in touch rather than arbitrary free-space motion, then reuses collected interaction data for offline policies and VLA post-training. The abstract reports downstream improvement without a single numerical headline. This is autonomous collection research, not commissioned human-data demand. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40134)

### Data annotation & validation

**September 30 · Counterfactual demonstrations test whether instructions are grounded.** “When Instructions Retrieve Trajectories” finds that a policy can respond to language while selecting a familiar trajectory inappropriate for the changed scene. Matched counterfactual data and training raise pi-0.5 position-swap success from **36% to 59%** on LIBERO-PRO and unseen-position success from **8% to 88%** on a real UR5e under a fixed demonstration budget. The lesson is to collect the same instruction under conditions requiring different actions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39971)

**September 30 · Blackout and frozen frames require different acceptance tests.** A study of pi-0.5 and GR00T finds different physical failures despite similarly poor task-success scores. Mitigations can improve completion while increasing unintended contact. Real-robot trials support reporting completion, contact and disturbance separately. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39145)

### Embedded AI systems

**September 30 · SteerQuant measures quantisation by action impact.** Across three world-action models, its low-bit approach keeps mean LIBERO success within 0.8 percentage points of full precision and reports up to 2.23× denoising acceleration. The separate real dual-arm W4A8 result is **1.35× end-to-end inference acceleration**, with average task success maintained. Do not interchange kernel and system speedups or assume an embedded-chip result. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39056)

**September 30 · Two-step denoising exposes the rest of the latency budget.** A study reduces model inference from 61.557 ms to 21.956 ms, while separately measuring perception, communication and physical response. Its garment-folding tests compare execution schemes and preserve action-provenance logs. Faster inference alone is not equivalent to faster or safer physical work. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39822)

### Robotics & humanoids

**September 29, missed overlap · DYNA introduces Taku and workflow-level orchestration.** The wheeled, two-arm robot combines a manipulation policy, whole-body controller and slower vision-language orchestrator with memory. The company describes an end-to-end laundry workflow and argues for measuring time without intervention. This is a company release, without an independently audited fleet reliability figure. Its interactive failure-rate example is explicitly illustrative, not a measured result. [DYNA](https://www.dyna.co/dyna-2.1)

**September 29 · Rho releases an embodiment-adaptation recipe.** The open-weights VLA family targets YAM Box, UR AI Trainer and FR3 Duo. Its report tests embodiment-specific midtraining and a small latent module that adapts a frozen action expert using as few as 15 corrected episodes in the evaluated edge cases. That is not a promise that any task can be learned from 15 recordings. [Paper and release links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38164)

**September 30 · NEXUS adapts teleoperation to different terrain.** Its nearly 1,000-hour paired motion corpus is generated through terrain-aware adaptation, rather than described as 1,000 hours of paid capture. A perceptive controller transfers to real humanoid teleoperation on unseen terrain. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39000)

### Research papers worth a look

In addition to the nine preprints above, nine distinct studies merit inclusion:

| Submitted | Paper | Result and practical limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| September 30 | [Dream4ACT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40153) | Represents actions as four URDF-derived virtual views, then recovers joint configurations without a learned per-embodiment decoder. Reports 88.98% RoboTwin 2.0 success; this is benchmark evidence. |
| September 30 | [Magic-W0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39870) | Couples current geometry, 3D motion and future semantics to actions, using ego, UMI, robot and simulation data. Latent geometric supervision comes from pretrained models, not necessarily measured ground truth. |
| September 30 | [OccluDex](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39017) | Pretrains a visuo-tactile representation on synchronised human demonstrations, then transfers to manipulation under self-occlusion. Real Shadow Hand tests complement simulated results; avoid extrapolating to all object classes. |
| September 30 | [The Planning Limits of Latent World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39235) | Short rollouts struggle with distant goals even under perfect prediction. Nearby subgoals and feedback help; predictor size alone does not solve the planning-horizon problem. |
| September 30 | [DynaHarness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40306) | Execution contracts, failure attribution and regression checks coordinate a frozen policy with reusable skills. Reports 75.2% on 800 new LIBERO-Pro initial states versus 17.5% for the frozen policy. Research title alone does not establish affiliation with DYNA Robotics. |
| September 30 | [FailBank](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39820) | Converts runtime counterfactual corrections into guarded policy updates. Benchmark success improves while cumulative cost falls; the collection-time teacher is observe-only, not an active safety guarantee. |
| September 30 | [Scale and Selection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39304) | Robot-agent harness revisions generalise better with larger rollout batches and comparison against an incumbent. Unconditionally accepting edits degrades results. Useful for designing reliable evaluation gates. |
| September 29 | [V-JEPA Policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37250) | Builds a 0.9B world-action model on a frozen V-JEPA 2.1 encoder; action-free DROID video/instruction pretraining helps downstream control. Tested benchmarks do not establish universal transfer. |
| September 29 | [MotorMind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38078) | A VLM proposes mid-level actions through deterministic controls and asynchronous feedback. Reports 95% average on its real xArm6 tests; this is a bounded experimental setup, not fleet uptime. |
