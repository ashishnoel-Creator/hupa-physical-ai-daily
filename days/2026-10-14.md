### Embedded AI systems

**October 13 · Ubitium raises $14 million; evaluation units planned for 2027.** The company reports $18 million raised since 2024, with the new round led by Ibex Investors and OpenOcean. Its UB108 test chip has 256 programmable elements on Samsung's 8nm process; the company says it runs signal processing, inference and control on a common architecture. The planned commercial UB410 expands to 4,096 elements. Initial applications include radar and audio. This is announced silicon progress and a future evaluation schedule, not current volume availability or independently measured robot performance. [Company release](https://www.ubitium.com/ubitium-raises-14-million-to-build-the-first-universal-processor-for-physical-ai/)
