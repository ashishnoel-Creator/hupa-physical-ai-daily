### Data collection

- **PaXini is the day's collection story. The new 1B yuan strategic round came from industrial groups, state funds and financial institutions (names undisclosed), five months after the 1B+ yuan Series B in March.** The operational numbers are the point: nearly 1M proprietary tactile chips consumed in a year, and four new embodied-data collection factories added in 2026 (Suqian, Wuhan, Zigong, Ganzhou) on top of the Tianjin "Super EID Factory." A capture-factory network for contact-rich demonstration data, funded like infrastructure. The N0 papers argued tactile pretraining works at corpus scale; PaXini is the industrial version of the same bet, and Chinese capital keeps writing it checks. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/icv/seeds-paxini-tech-secures-another-1-billion-yuan-in-strategic-financing-2084896931091283968)
- **Meta Reality Labs published an 18-page user study showing semantic haptic feedback, event-coded cues rather than raw force rendering, measurably improves dexterous teleoperation (arXiv 2608.02780).** Teleop rig design detail that translates directly into data economics: better operator feedback means more successful demonstrations per paid hour. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02780)
- **RoboReact (arXiv 2608.03387, CUHK-Shenzhen) distills whole-body manipulation skills from generated egocentric videos rather than collected ones.** Worth tracking as a competitive vector: if synthesized ego video keeps improving, it pressures the price of low-diversity real capture while raising the premium on validated footage of things generators get wrong. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03387)

### Data annotation & validation

- **ValueFormer is the annotation story of the day, because of who wrote it. Chef Robotics authors, including founder and CEO Rajat Bhageria, describe a value function trained with stage-aware labels that watches a semi-autonomous VLA policy and predicts when execution is degrading, gating human takeover.** The labels are the product here: someone has to segment rollouts into stages and mark where they fail, per task, at production scale. Runtime verification keeps showing up in research (CheckVLA, WCM), but this one comes from a company with real customers, which makes stage-labeled failure annotation look like a commercial deliverable rather than an academic artifact. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02958)

Robot companies buying equity in their own data supplier is a structural signal: the buyers want the pipeline captive. [36Kr](https://eu.36kr.com/en/newsflashes/3925089016100995)

### Embedded AI systems

- **Georgia Tech Research Institute ran a full bimanual imitation-learning stack on an 8 GB Jetson Orin Nano Super (arXiv 2608.03938): TensorRT FP16 cuts ACT inference from 114 ms to 17.9 ms, INT8 reaches 12.7 ms, with task success preserved at 19/20.** Two findings nobody had documented: TensorRT INT8 calibration accepts the ResNet18 backbone but zero of 145 transformer layers, and whether quantization helps at all depends on the action-chunking configuration. Entry-level edge silicon is now sufficient for bimanual manipulation, and the paper doubles as a spec sheet for anyone designing a low-cost vision node. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03938)
- **PhyAI (arXiv 2608.03682, BUPT) is a unified inference runtime that serves VLAs and world-action models across onboard, edge and cloud, claiming 1.40-4.65x speedups over the official pi0, pi0.5, GR00T N1.7 and MiniCPM-Robot implementations, plus a "control-time Roofline" that separates inference-bound from environment-bound control.** Open source. The serving layer for physical AI is starting to consolidate the way vLLM consolidated LLM serving, and it is happening in a university lab, not a vendor. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03682)
- **Faster-WAM (arXiv 2608.02365, Huawei Noah's Ark Lab Montreal) asks whether world-action models need deep action modules and answers no: a single-layer action head docked onto a 30-layer video backbone matches deep designs at 66.5 ms latency, 3.2x faster, with +23.5 points out-of-distribution on LIBERO-Plus.** The action module is shrinking toward free, which pushes the value into the video backbone and the data that trains it. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02365)
- **Avnet and Weston Robot launched an AI-powered autonomous inspection platform for industrial environments, pairing Avnet's edge compute with Weston's quadruped fleet.** A distributor-plus-integrator packaging of edge physical AI, aimed at buyers who want inspection as a product rather than a robotics project. [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/avnet-and-weston-robot-partner-to-launch-edge-ai-inspection-platform/)

### Research papers worth a look

- **ValueFormer: A Causal Transformer Value Function with Stage-Aware Labels for Semi-Autonomous VLA Policies (arXiv 2608.02958, Chef Robotics).** Full read in annotation above. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02958)
- **Bimanual Manipulation Within an 8 GB Budget: Zero-Copy Sensing and Quantized ACT on an Entry-Level Jetson (arXiv 2608.03938, Georgia Tech Research Institute).** Full read in embedded above. Also finds ACT converges where Diffusion Policy fails outright (0/10) at double the training budget. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03938)
- **PhyAI: Real-Time Physical AI at the Edge, Scalable Rollouts in the Cloud (arXiv 2608.03682, BUPT).** Full read in embedded above. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03682)
- **Faster-WAM: Do World Action Models Need Deep Action Modules? (arXiv 2608.02365, Huawei Noah's Ark Lab).** Full read in embedded above. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02365)
- **Track4Action: Distilling World-Centric 3D Tracker into VLA Policies (arXiv 2608.03727, SJTU, Cewu Lu's lab).** Training-only 3D tracking supervision distilled into VLAs: +7.6 average zero-shot on LIBERO-Plus, 20+ point gains on real bimanual tasks. Another instance of the pattern where rich labels exist only at training time, which is an annotation-pipeline product. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03727)
- **LiLa-WAM: Lightweight Latent Reasoning World-Action Model (arXiv 2608.03701, Tianjin University).** A full world-action model trainable end to end on a single 24 GB GPU, 90.48% on RoboTwin 2.0 and 97.1% on LIBERO. World-action modeling exiting the datacenter class. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03701)
- **How Should Vision-Language-Action Models Use Proprioceptive State? (arXiv 2608.03052, AI2 Robotics and collaborators).** Empirical study of proprioception conditioning in VLAs. Decides what channels a capture rig must log and how they get aligned. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03052)
- **DigitCode: Symbolic Tokenization of Hand Motion by Anatomical Units (arXiv 2608.03127, Tsinghua SIGS).** A candidate lingua franca for dexterous demonstration data and a natural annotation schema for hand-tracking pipelines. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03127)
- **Semantic Haptic Feedback Enhances Dexterous Robotic Teleoperation (arXiv 2608.02780, Meta Reality Labs).** Full read in collection above. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02780)
- **RoboReact: Agentic Skill Distillation from Generated Egocentric Videos (arXiv 2608.03387, CUHK-Shenzhen).** Full read in collection above. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03387)
- **Continue or Replan? Bernoulli-Continuation Policy Learning for Adaptive Horizon Execution (arXiv 2608.03483, Microsoft Research).** Learns when to keep executing an action chunk versus replan, the runtime complement to the why-does-chunking-work theory covered yesterday. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03483)
- **Toward Certified Functional Safety for Industrial Humanoid Robots: The Fail-Passive Gap (arXiv 2608.02809, Siemens-affiliated).** Full read in humanoids above. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02809)
- **ETA: A New Agentic Paradigm for Embodied Tasks (arXiv 2608.03924, Fudan/OpenMOSS).** LLM planner with robot capabilities exposed as tools reaches up to 90.0% across 130 LIBERO tasks through a 3-tool interface. The agentic-wrapper-beats-monolithic-VLA counter-thesis, from the same group as WCM. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03924)
- **The trending world-model cluster, never covered: Quo Vadis, World Modeling? (arXiv 2608.02713, Shanghai AI Lab), a position paper reframing world models as agent-centric interactive proxies; WorldExam (arXiv 2608.02603), a benchmark separating visual fidelity from reactive correctness; and MiniWorld (arXiv 2608.01127, Peking University), a recipe and codebase for training video world models cheaply.** The evaluation and taxonomy layer of the world-model wave, arriving right as WAMs multiply. [Quo Vadis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02713), [WorldExam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02603), [MiniWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01127)
