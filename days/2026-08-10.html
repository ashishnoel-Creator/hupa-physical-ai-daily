    Data collection

    • Tacta Systems is the collection story of the window. The TactaBot system is three parts: a 15-DoF five-finger hand on the company’s Fluidic Tendon actuation, a Dexterous Intelligence model fine-tuned per customer, and the Tacta Glove, a capture rig workers wear during normal factory work. The sensor spec is the point: each sensor is smaller than a grain of sand at 1 mm pitch, spans 250 to 700,000 pascals (the claimed full dynamic range of human touch), measures temperature to 0.1 C, and streams wirelessly to a local host. CEO Vikram Pavate’s pitch is explicitly the data gap (“there’s no Shakespeare and the web and Reddit” for the physical world), and the deployment model is capture-at-work: the glove needs no training, the worker just does the job. This is the same negative-cost-capture logic as Tau’s cleaning service, applied to skilled manufacturing and with the tactile channel PaXini and the N0 papers have been arguing is the next corpus. The Robot Report
    • EgoAffordance (arXiv 2608.05215, University of Tokyo JSK lab, IROS 2026) extracts 5.6M visual and 11.6M grasp/trajectory affordances across 204K episodes from egocentric human video via SfM and hand-mesh reconstruction, then trains VLAff, a unified affordance foundation model for zero-shot manipulation. Another published pipeline that turns raw ego footage into robot-usable labels, which keeps raising what a validated ego corpus is worth. arXiv
    • SiMDex (arXiv 2608.04196, University of Tokyo, Yoichi Sato’s lab with industry co-authors) treats human-data selection for VLA post-training as a recommendation problem over a ~32M-sample egocentric pool: mining just 1.49M task-relevant samples, under 5% of the pool, lifts real success from 47.7% to 61.1% against equal-size random sampling. Curation beats volume, on hard numbers. The commercial read: the value is not the hours, it is knowing which hours. arXiv
    • JoyAI-RA 0.5 (arXiv 2608.05674, JD.com’s JoyAI robotics team) aligns action-free human egocentric video, sim and robot data in one Vision-Language-World-Action framework, and reports that task score keeps scaling with egocentric pretraining volume with no plateau at their largest scale. A Chinese commerce giant’s robotics team putting human ego video forward as a primary scaling axis for manipulation. arXiv
    • ω-0 (arXiv 2608.06375, Peking University and NTU) ships ω-HOME, a 40+ hour real-world household humanoid dataset with synchronized multi-view video, SMPL human motion, robot states and action latents, backing a whole-body world-action model that beats VLA and WAM baselines on 11 household tasks. Household humanoid data with aligned human motion is exactly the paired-embodiment shape frontier buyers keep asking for. arXiv

    Data annotation & validation

    • Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab and HKU proposed SSC (arXiv 2608.04425), a machine-verifiable structured representation for labelling bimanual manipulation demonstrations: subtask chains with after-state scene graphs, sitting between free-text captions and rigid templates, with VLM-assisted labelling functions. A major lab publishing an annotation schema, not a model. Verifiable label formats are what turn robot-demo labelling from artisanal work into a spec’d deliverable, which is the entire annotation-thesis argument. arXiv
    • SAFECAST (arXiv 2608.04246, USC) hardens hidden-state failure detection for VLA policies using contrast-set training and calibration, evaluated under deployment shift on DROID and LIBERO. The runtime-verification thread (CheckVLA, WCM, ValueFormer) keeps growing, and every one of these detectors trains on labeled failures. arXiv
    • SUTD ran 5,670 trials of physical prompt injection against VLM-controlled robots (arXiv 2608.05715): a piece of paper with adversarial text in the scene hijacks GPT-4o and Gemini-driven robots 27-29% of the time, and simple defenses cut it to near zero. Adversarial-robustness QC is about to be a line item in deployment validation. arXiv
    • The named vendors (Scale, Mercor, Surge, Labelbox, Snorkel, Sama, iMerit, Invisible, Centific, Toloka) published nothing on-thesis dated this window. Scale’s only new post is a sovereign-AI policy piece.

    Embedded AI systems

    • 36Kr reports NVIDIA is urgently courting Chinese AI base-station suppliers and plans to launch “end-side computing power networking” within two years, pushing inference capacity out of datacenters toward distributed edge nodes. Treat as single-sourced, but if the direction holds, the edge-inference layer robots depend on becomes telco-shaped infrastructure, and the supplier list is Chinese. 36Kr
    • An adaptive VLA inference framework from Huawei-affiliated authors (arXiv 2608.06434) fully decouples the fast and slow systems of dual-system VLAs and switches between a large deliberative model and a lightweight reactive one based on the environment. Latency management as a deployment-engineering discipline, arriving before the models are settled. arXiv
    • Bosch Research and KIT showed driving intent in a driving VLA is linearly decodable after a single decoder layer at 97.7% accuracy, and pruned the model’s depth accordingly (arXiv 2608.07361, ECCV 2026 workshop). A tier-1 automotive supplier publishing how it squeezes VLA compute for in-vehicle silicon. arXiv
    • ShanghaiTech built a near-sensor streaming hardware Poisson solver that reconstructs tactile depth at a fixed 0.211 ms latency on 347 mW, closing a robot reflex loop 6x faster than host-based processing (arXiv 2608.05725). Compute is migrating into the fingertip. Pairs directly with the Tacta launch above. arXiv
    • Adaptive-WAM (arXiv 2608.06008) puts quality-guided early-exit trajectory heads on a 5B video-diffusion backbone and reports the best front-view world-model planner on NAVSIM (92.6 PDMS) at 170 ms, 47% below full depth. World-model inference cost is now its own research thread, third entry this week alone. arXiv

Finding: no engine is uniformly faithful, and video models get the equation form right with the physics wrong. Ammunition for the real-data side of the sim debate. arXiv

One industrial lab, three attacks on the same WAM reliability problem. Robust-WAM, DyPES-VLA, PSG-JEPA

The WAM wave, still cresting. DreamWAM, PILOT, GeniWorld, WNM-3D
