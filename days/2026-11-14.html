    Data collection

    Capture structure is becoming more explicit. VBot’s November 12 paper pairs ego/exo recording with camera geometry, semantic masks and hand keypoints. HEIR pairs gaze, speech and ego video with teleoperated robot trajectories and session continuity. VI AMO adds action-and-mission labels to teleoperation episodes. These are different data products; none is an advertised paid supplier brief. Practical inference: ask buyers which synchronized streams and temporal labels they need before committing to volume.     Data annotation & validation

    Evaluate information and decisions, not only plausible video. New studies test whether imagined rollouts preserve obstacle information, whether predictions respond correctly to alternative actions, and how much performance comes from the predictor versus simply retaining the latest observation. These support evaluation sets with counterfactual actions, failure states and persistence baselines. Evidence is bounded by the studied models and tasks.     Date Paper Finding / limit
    2026-11-11 The Posterior-Prior Epistemic Gap: When World Model Imagination Fails to Carry Task-Relevant Signals Single-seed CARLA study finds obstacle information decays in imagined rollouts; uncertainty disagreement does not reliably flag the loss.
    2026-11-11 Does Your World Model Know Actions Have Consequences? World-model evaluation should test sensitivity to alternative actions, alongside prediction error and task success.
    2026-11-11 Embedded Sim-to-Real Visual Servoing: Learned Reaching and Force-Guided Placement Jetson AGX Orin/Franka experiments report 70% learned-hybrid versus 74% force-guided completion over 50 real trials per strategy; perception latency is not full control latency.
    2026-11-12 I See What You Want: Decoding Hierarchical Operator Intent from Visual Teleoperation History VI AMO provides 6,600 teleoperation episodes with aligned action and mission labels; supports studying intent and human-to-robot handover.
    2026-11-12 EgoX: Egocentric Cross-Embodiment Manipulation with Embodiment Dreaming EgoX mixes human, real-robot and simulated demonstrations; progress gains across 13 task-embodiment pairs are not universal success rates.
    2026-11-12 HEIR: Harness Egocentric Intent for Human-Robot Interactions HEIR aligns measured gaze, speech, ego video and teleoperated robot trajectories with human annotations and successive episodes.
    2026-11-11 Prediction or Persistence? What Future-Prediction Scores Miss About World Models 27,000 ego-video retrieval trials separate a predictor’s contribution from a persistence baseline; downstream control usefulness remains a separate evaluation.
    2026-11-11 Topology-Aware 4D Gaussian Splatting for Egocentric Videos TopoEgo studies cutting, merging, opening and closing; Topo-4DGS models changing surface support. Code/data release is promised, not verified here.
