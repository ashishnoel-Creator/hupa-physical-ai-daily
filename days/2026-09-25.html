    Data collection

    September 23, missed yesterday · Gen-HumanEgo sets a useful packaging reference. The dataset card lists 44,632 episodes, 10,257 unique tasks, six synchronised RGB views at 1600 × 1300 and 30 FPS, packaged as MCAP. It includes reconstructed 21-point hands and meshes, depth, and video/task/subtask labels with temporal boundaries and success fields. These are model-produced supervision signals rather than proof that every released frame has measured ground truth. The publisher describes mocap-based evaluation separately. Its LinkedIn/Chinese launch pages use 10,357 tasks, differing from the card’s 10,257; use the card count provisionally and retain the discrepancy. Dataset availability does not establish a paid sourcing request. Dataset card, technical explanation, launch post

    September 24 · PolyUMI combines vision, touch and contact audio. Its wireless handheld gripper captures wrist imagery, optical tactile readings, contact audio and proprioception. The same sensing finger moves to the robot hand, reducing the sensing mismatch between demonstration and execution. The project estimates $236 in parts for the sensing finger, not an entire collection rig, and links hardware/code. Its VisTA policy is evaluated on object inference, slip control and contact-rich manipulation. No commercial supplier intake is stated. Paper, project

    September 24 · Human tempo becomes supervision. EgoSpeedUp aligns human and robot demonstrations of the same task, then retimes robot trajectories using phase-specific human tempo. Across two real-world tasks it reports a 25-percentage-point average success improvement and 36.5% shorter successful execution time. The scope is small, but the collection lesson is specific: preserve timing and task correspondence, rather than only action names. Paper

    September 24 · Ego-Exo4D-HM releases dense human motion and its reconstruction pipeline. Its project describes 2,649 takes across eight activities. The advertised 523 video hours sum five camera streams, corresponding to 104.6 hours per camera. Those are different accounting units and should not be treated as 523 distinct hours of activity. Paper, dataset documentation

Their proposed scope spans collection, processing, training and deployment feedback. This is an announced collaboration, with no verified completed venture or open subcontracting brief.     Data annotation & validation

    September 24 · RoboRecover tests the middle of a failed execution. It reconstructs intermediate deviation states by replaying action prefixes and evaluates whether policies can continue the original task. The benchmark has 2,000 scenarios across RoboTwin and LIBERO, with 800/200 train/test splits on each. Initial-state performance does not reliably predict recovery performance. Collection and evaluation should preserve intermediate failures and context, rather than only clean starts and final outcomes. Paper

    September 24 · SplatLabel adds uncertainty-aware 3D pseudo-labels. Its 4D Gaussian representation tracks dynamic scenes and produces LiDAR segmentation and occupancy supervision from 2D semantic priors, without pre-annotated 3D boxes. It evaluates selective precision/recall trade-offs on SemanticKITTI. This is an automated annotation recipe, not a replacement for validating labels on a new capture distribution. Paper

    Embedded AI systems

    September 23 announcement, newly verified · PrismML demonstrated a 2B-parameter vision-language model on Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 smart glasses. The company says its 1-bit Bonsai approach fits four times as many parameters within the same memory constraint on some glasses form factors. This is a local inference demonstration. The release does not establish sustained battery life, thermal performance or a generally available glasses product. PrismML announcement

    September 24 · Training-free Behavior Cloning keeps demonstrations in the control loop. Behavior Predictive Control retrieves observation/action windows and applies a residual correction, avoiding end-to-end policy fitting. The authors report fitting in seconds and closed-loop control above 75 Hz on Jetson Orin Nano, with simulation and real-robot comparisons. This creates a concrete reason to retain searchable, synchronised demonstration banks. Paper

    September 24 · Early exits need separate compute controls. A study varies VLM backbone depth, action-expert depth and denoising steps in SmolVLA and pi-0.5. Its benchmark configurations report up to 79.2% lower latency and 31.8% fewer FLOPs. The trade-offs depend on the task; these are not universal hardware speedups. Paper

RealSense’s facial-authentication business is to be spun out before closing.     Robotics & humanoids

    September 24 coverage · Feather offers a configurable wheeled, two-arm developer platform. Its current site lists a $29,990 promotional starting price, built-in teleoperation, simulation support and a Python/ROS 2 SDK. The technology page specifies two 7-DoF arms on a holonomic base. Availability, runtime and field-performance statements are company claims. This is hardware for developing applications, not a turnkey promise that it can perform arbitrary work. Current product, technical specification

    September 23 · Skild’s new result is self-play post-training. Starting from its S1 programme, it describes soccer learning against past policy versions in Isaac Sim and transfer to a physical humanoid after 140 simulated years. The company explicitly places self-play after human-data pretraining. This does not show that demonstrations were unnecessary to the full pipeline, nor establish general workplace reliability. S1’s original model launch was in August. Primary technical post

    September 24 · Res-HIL uses interventions to refine a frozen imitation policy. Across five contact-rich tasks, the paper reports better performance after ten minutes of online training from 20 initial demonstrations than the compared human-in-the-loop and residual baselines. Corrections supply both action supervision and a signal about preceding autonomous behaviour. Preserve intervention timestamps and pre-intervention context when collecting such data. Paper

    September 24 · Self-Adaptive VLA targets calibration and hardware shifts. It collects rollouts under injected shifts and conditions the policy on observation, proprioception and action context. Across four tasks, it reports recovering over 80% of the base policy’s performance under shifts. That is recovery relative to a reference performance, not an 80% absolute task-success guarantee. Paper

    Research papers worth a look

    Start with PolyUMI, EgoSpeedUp and RoboRecover for collection design and acceptance testing. The following add distinct results to the papers discussed above:

    Original submission Paper Result and limit
    September 24 RAPID Generates and tests robot programs from one visual human demonstration. Evaluated on eight nonprehensile tasks, including a real Franka arm, plus LIBERO-Pro. The abstract provides no single success percentage.
    September 24 BeyondRetarget Maps monocular human video directly into robot-oriented motion representations with contact-aware optimisation. Simulation and real-humanoid evidence, without a numerical gain in the abstract.
    September 24 HarnessPAI Revises executable programs between rollouts. Reports a 61.6-point gain over pi-0.5 on LIBERO-PRO and a 38.8-point gain from fine-tuning on its collected expert data. These are benchmark comparisons, not production deployment figures.
    September 24 Rolling-WAM Carries partly denoised future chunks between replanning cycles. Reports 4.5× steady-state replanning speedup, with LIBERO, RoboTwin and real G1 evaluations.
    September 24 Streaming-WAM Conditions prediction on already committed actions. Reports 98.35% LIBERO success and a real Stamp Paper task reduction from 90 to 38 seconds versus a synchronous baseline.
    September 24 OSRAM Adapts reference commands using the deployed controller’s measured response, leaving the policy unchanged. Evaluated in simulation and hardware.
    September 23 DeltaWAM Predicts visual changes and caches context. RoboTwin success rises from 81.3% to 85.4% in clean scenes and from 75.8% to 83.9% with visual randomisation versus Fast-WAM.
    September 23 LiMA Separates slower intent generation from fast refinement. Reports 45.8% lower inference latency than Cosmos-Policy and 70.8% overall success across six bimanual tasks.
    September 23 Watch, Recall, Act / ARMS A streaming policy and dual-arm teleoperation dataset that tracks past actions and changing instructions. Reports 45% combined-task success versus 28% for its strongest main baseline.

    The world-model papers converge on reducing prediction latency while retaining feedback. Their different baselines and tasks prevent a meaningful cross-paper speed ranking.
