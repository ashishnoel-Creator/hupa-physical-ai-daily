    Data collection

    November 9 · Human-1M’s open 100,000-hour slice makes the expected deliverable more concrete. NVIDIA’s dataset card describes synchronized mono/stereo ego footage, optional wrist views, task summaries, timed action segments, head pose and 21 hand keypoints with confidence/masks. It specifies at least 30 FPS / 480p, unaltered speed, and hands visible for at least 95% of an episode. Timed segments cover 1–10 seconds and identify the hand, action and object. The card labels the release CC BY 4.0. The public viewer contains a small preview; the entire 100,000-hour payload was not downloaded or independently audited. The October Human-1M announcement was already covered; the open subset and published specification are today’s follow-through.

    November 9 · Noitom HiPHI-Hand is accessible as a gated research dataset. The company announced 11 hours of synchronized dual-fisheye video with optical hand-motion ground truth used for EgoHand training. The dataset card specifies 1360×1360 H.264 at 30 FPS, hardware synchronization, per-dataset calibration and 21-/25-joint hand annotations in a shared IMU coordinate frame. It requires a questionnaire and research-license acceptance; commercial licensing is a separate route. No access request was submitted. November 9 company release.

    Interpretation: the useful comparison is accepted, synchronized, well-described episodes with traceable calibration and rights. Neither dataset release is a public request to hire collection vendors.

    November 9 · GR00T 2 predicts future video and actions together. NVIDIA reports pretraining on 402,730 hours of human ego video, 10,184 hours of handheld UMI demonstrations and 23,500 hours of robot teleoperation.
