### Data collection

**October 15 catch-up · RoboCloth.** Authors describe 500 real cloth materials, each captured in approximately 580 HDR images with robot-controlled camera and lighting. Calibrated poses, sparse geometry and reflectance observations support neural material reconstruction. The abstract states that dataset/calibration/splits are released under CC BY 4.0; the complete downloadable assets and licence files were not separately audited. This is material appearance data, not a cloth-manipulation demonstration corpus. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21090)

**October 13 catch-up · coastal ground-truth collection.** CSIRO, CQUniversity and the Great Barrier Reef Foundation are trialling a towed underwater camera with an uncrewed surface vessel for murky-water seagrass surveys. Georeferenced imagery is processed onboard and transmitted by radio; aerial surveys can complement intertidal ground-truthing. The release describes trials, with the first full field-performance report expected later in 2026. It does not establish an open supplier programme or completed scale deployment. [Institutional release](https://www.csiro.au/en/news/All/News/2026/October/TUV-Eyes-beneath-the-surface)

Jupiter's request makes synchronization, calibration, sensor rate and delivery format explicit. Those are procurement requirements for this particular brief, not a universal egocentric standard.

### Data annotation & validation

**October 14 catch-up · Orbia.** The benchmark uses 720 cases and six exploration/revisit protocols to test input preservation, persistence of generated content and 3D self-consistency across 27 models. It combines RGB-D references, anchors and cross-view checks. A plausible individual frame is insufficient evidence of a usable persistent world. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20693)

**October 14–15 catch-up · labels for change and coverage.** [VLA-Δ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21306) derives semantic and motion-change supervision for pretraining, then executes without an extra inference stage. [Mind the Tail](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21007) selects images using sparse-concept coverage to strengthen underrepresented concepts. The latter is an image-learning study, not direct proof of robot-task improvements from a particular vendor's data.

### Embedded AI systems

**October 9 catch-up, revised listing · EV-JEPA.** A single trained V-JEPA supports variable width and split execution across devices, including narrow-to-wide processing. Authors report accuracy close to separately trained baselines; the result concerns representation inference, not measured production robot latency on every device. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14324)

**October 14 catch-up · CF-Prune.** Training-free future-token pruning retains dense computation during precision phases. At 35% pruning, authors report 1.19–1.27× average inference speedups; more aggressive results are simulation-specific. Policy inference speed is not total job throughput. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19957)

### World models & sim-to-real

**October 12–15 catch-up · learning dynamics and actions together.** [OneWM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21095) tests reciprocal benefits between action generation and action-conditioned world prediction using a shared architecture. [RAP-WAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16443) uses predictive rehearsal and action perturbations to direct supervision. Its reported 89% macro-average across 15 real tasks must not be conflated with 15.62% success on its different 42-task RoboDojo setting.

[WorldCast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20731) tests multi-step visual state prediction; [3DCodeVerse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22341) creates executable, checkable 3D code; [DK-VLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22356) puts estimated geometry directly into attention. These tackle different evaluation problems and should not be ranked on a shared headline score. Google's Argon examples were covered October 16 and are not repeated as fresh news.
