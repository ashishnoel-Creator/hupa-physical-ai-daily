### Data collection

**November 19 · Awaken-Human / Awaken-HI:** the authors pair approximately **50,000 hours** of stereo egocentric video with synchronized whole-body motion across more than 100 skills. Joint human-motion and retargeted robot-action supervision supports T800 adaptation; reported average success is **93.75% across eight individual tasks**. This is bounded author-reported evidence, not a universal deployment rate; dataset downloads/licences were not independently tested. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26420)

**November 19 · HumanCOIN:** **12,500 hours / 38 scene classes** comprise 10,000 hours of inferred-attention data, 2,000 hours with EMG and 500 hours with EEG; higher tiers inherit lower-tier signals. It also describes multi-view RGB, depth, point clouds, head/wrist pose and step labels, with timing/calibration records. Inferred gaze in the broad tier is distinct from directly measured gaze in the controlled tier. Interpretation: collection quality increasingly includes sensor provenance and synchronization, alongside video hours. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26434)

**PUPPET separates task completion from a usable record:** its 30-person remote-lab study reports **85.6% validation-ready VR trials versus 66.0% with 2D teleoperation**. A successful manipulation can still yield an incomplete dataset. [November19 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26350)

### Data annotation & validation

**November 18 catch-up · SimAudit:** audits task instructions against simulated success checkers, finding **61 distinct defects across 49 tasks**: 40 false successes and 21 rejected valid executions. The relevant lesson is to validate both incomplete attempts and alternative correct completions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25179)

**EgoCaptioner** builds 50,000 training examples with coarse-to-fine event structure, detailed timed steps and separate factual/temporal rewards. **Ego-ExBind** shows retrieval improvements can arise from familiar verb–noun combinations without stronger compositional understanding. Neither a fluent caption nor a higher retrieval score alone establishes reliable action labels. [EgoCaptioner](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26380) · [Ego-ExBind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26120)

**AgentRoboCritic** describes 12,844 action annotations and a 400-action human-labelled evaluation set, exposing grasp, alignment and recovery failures that episode success conceals. **Mind2Act** separately tests reasoning, execution and their combination; strong isolated components still struggle together. [Critic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26613) · [Benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26444)

Searches across Scale/Labs, Mercor, Surge, Labelbox, Snorkel, Sama, iMerit, Invisible and Centific did not establish another sufficiently verified fresh operational release; coverage remains partial.
