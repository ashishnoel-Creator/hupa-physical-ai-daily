### Data collection

**Collect where policies fail.** [Mulligan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05882), submitted October 5, allocates supervised episodes to observed failures and untried initial states. The authors report 10–34 percentage-point final real-task gains when combined with value-based action selection, across three real tasks and matched collection budgets. Their 98% completion figure includes operator intervention, so it is a human–robot collection-team result, not autonomous success. Commercial implication, as an inference: task/state coverage and useful recovery segments deserve their own delivery metrics.

**History and contact become explicit data requirements.** [Grounded in Time](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04255) combines real/UMI demonstrations with counterfactual history-dependent tasks. [TacOT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04363), [DexForge](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06331) and [InterMimicGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06850) study contact-aware transfer from human motion to robots. These are research releases, not paid collection invitations. Details and original dates are in the paper table.

| 2026-10-03 | [PerturBot: Breaking Shortcut Priors in Vision-Language-Action Models with Perturbative Training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04616) | Perturbs visual shortcuts, enriches instructions and relabels failures; GroundingFscore tests whether success reflects relevant evidence. |
| 2026-10-04 | [Now You Feel It, Now You See Me: Digital-Twin-based Teleoperation Interface for Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05081) | Bare-hand teleoperation interface visualizes tactile measurements and mitigates occlusion through a digital twin; user-study evidence. |
| 2026-10-02 | [GOTT: Object-centric Dexterous Manipulation with a Reusable Cross-Embodiment Primitive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03861) | Reuses a contact-acquisition primitive across hands and tasks, with object trajectories from human or model intent. |
| 2026-10-03 | [Continual Humanoid Motion Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04231) | Similarity-guided low-rank progressive learning adds humanoid skills without revisiting old data; demonstrated on Unitree G1. |
| 2026-10-04 | [Have I Scene This Before? Spatially Grounded Conversational Memory for Complex Queries in Egocentric Assistants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05526) | Ego-SpaCR couples spatial and conversational memory; 620 ScanNet sessions, 95 conversations and 3,100 queries, not newly recorded commercial ego data. |
