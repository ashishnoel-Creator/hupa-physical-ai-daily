### Data collection

**October 8: PanTact adds the missing body-contact channel.** Wearable modular patches supplement ego vision and bimanual UMI grippers. A policy predicts contact goals and a controller realizes them on Unitree G1 without extra robot body-contact sensors. This expands demonstrations to support, pushing, sweeping and load sharing. It does not establish a commercial capture standard. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12558)

**October 9: Human2Dex separates reusable human data from robot-specific labels.** A wrist-centric corpus transfers to Linker O6 and Wuji through calibrated representations and retargeting. Reported success is 90.8% and 80.8% over six tasks; checkpoints and action labels remain hand-specific. Reusability is conditional, not plug-and-play across arbitrary hands. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13119)

**Timing and occlusion are part of the specification.** [Chronos](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13929) uses observation-capture and desired execution timestamps for asynchronous data. [EgoReveal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12999) learns to reposition the view before manipulating occluded objects. [Ego/IMU analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12926) warns that video embeddings can be dominated by where the head points. These motivate modality and synchronization QA rather than an hours-only rubric.

**Synthetic stereo changes the economics, but does not settle quality equivalence.** [StereoEngine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12734) generates stereo pairs from monocular demonstrations or simulation and reports bimanual policy gains. It is not proof that synthetic stereo satisfies BARG's unshared acceptance rubric. [Splat2Act](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13945) uses phone scene scans and object images to create over 68,000 simulated pickup demonstrations across 101 captured environments; its real Stretch 3 result is limited to a 12-task subset.

Additional collection research, including Evo-RL's reported 1,000-plus-hour dataset, urban capture design, smartphone object onboarding and calibrated stereo food capture, is indexed below. Data availability statements are author claims unless separately inspected; licences and downstream commercial permissions require separate review.

### Data annotation & validation

**Benchmark the reason for failure and the value of intervention.** [Spa-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12696) releases 1,200 teleoperated demonstrations and 2,892 evaluation rollouts, exposing spatial-grounding gaps despite familiar objects/actions. [RESCUE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13516) uses 128,340 simulated counterfactual branches to show that accurate alarms do not necessarily reduce intervention cost. Its 2.0–3.3% false-alarm tolerance belongs to a particular stop/reset model, not a universal operational threshold.

**Scene flow and failure evidence are becoming deliverables.** [MeshBind](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12895) combines six surround cameras with LiDAR, a correction tool and a 41,000-frame non-rigid scene-flow benchmark. [Robot-WATCHDOG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13403) evaluates object-level semantic failures. [JitterVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13628) separates controller-induced jerk from policy quality; its code/data are promised, not confirmed downloadable here.

### Embedded AI systems

**SmolWAM, October 9:** reports 57 ms per 16-action chunk and 2.4 GiB GPU memory, with 95.5% success across 400 LIBERO episodes. This is a compact inference result; the abstract does not establish those numbers on every edge device or a real production robot. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13740)

### Robotics & humanoids

**Reflex, October 8:** real G1 box-catching success is reported at 65% from 2.5 m and 40% from 5 m, below human baselines. Egocentric RGB-D history helps recover moving-object dynamics. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12772)

**Contact preservation links human video to robot execution.** [VICAR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13079) transfers ten third-person interaction skills while retaining contact timing/poses; [PriMoRetarget](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12642) prioritizes interaction constraints. [ATHLETE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13572) combines existing locomotion data and tennis videos for reactive rallies. These are bounded research demonstrations, not general task competence.

[AlphaNIST](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13354) learns all 20 assembly-board tasks with binary rewards in simulation. It should not be reported as demonstration-free real-world deployment. Further memory, adaptation and motion papers are listed below.

### World models & sim-to-real

Three distinct directions emerge: [LeWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12818) jointly learns latent dynamics and actions; [Forward Dynamics Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13117) use future-state supervision while skipping future generation at inference; [Grounded-Dreamer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13164) checks synthetic trajectories in reconstructed physics before using them for real-policy training. Their benchmarks are not directly comparable.

[HF-WM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13522) combines human interaction and synthetic failure-aware training. [Dream to Remember](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13816) rehearses old skills through a world model, with its use for new-skill learning dependent on fidelity. [CASTER](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12567) formalizes physical/semantic refinement of video-generated trajectories. Together these support hybrid capture, simulation and validation, not the claim that real data is obsolete.
