### Research papers worth a look

**One newly catalogued preprint, explicitly catch-up:** [Pixel-UMI: Learning Environment-Aware Whole-Body Manipulation from Sensorized Human Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03647), submitted **November 2**. Environment-Aware Retargeting combines reconstructed geometry with head/gripper trajectories to produce collision-aware robot targets; delay calibration assigns actions, and masking reduces dependence on mismatched viewpoints. On-policy human corrections address contact limitations. Authors report **87% average task progress across five tasks**, not 87% full-task success. Results are author-reported and were not reproduced here.

The four accessible arXiv subject listings remain dated November 6. No newer submission batch is claimed. AIRoA-0's paper was already catalogued; only its November 6 release notice is added. The [library](../papers.html) gains **eight records: one preprint and seven company/technical releases**, reaching **1,536**. LeRobot adds world-action-model integrations, but compatibility does not independently establish physical realism or sim-to-real performance.
