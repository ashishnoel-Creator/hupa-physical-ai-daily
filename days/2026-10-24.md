### Data collection

**Catch-up, October 22 — physical task knowledge becomes shared training infrastructure.** [TUM](https://www.tum.de/en/news-and-events/all-news/press-releases/details/making-robots-smarter) describes around 100 humanoid/service robots initially learning in 48 training cells. Robot interactions feed a curated cloud data lake; industrial and intralogistics tasks are an initial focus. [NEURA's announcement](https://neura-robotics.com/tum-robogym-munich-4ne1-gen4-launch/) places the facility at Munich Airport's LabCampus, across more than 2,300 m². It combines robot data, human demonstrations and simulation; participating companies contribute workflows and process expertise. This is collaboration and infrastructure evidence, not a published paid collection-vendor programme.

**Newly discovered open real-to-sim resource:** [Nova-Wanderland](https://huggingface.co/datasets/raresense/nova-wanderland) reconstructs 20 outdoor captures into editable, object-level environments with semantic labels, joints, collision geometry and exports for Isaac Sim, MuJoCo, Gazebo and PyBullet. The [raw dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/raresense/nova-wanderland/raw/main/README.md) was accessible when the normal page failed. Inputs include image sequences assembled into videos, rather than original continuous recordings. The card declares Apache-2.0 and CC0 for included Poly Haven resources; this is not an audit of every upstream right. Its [author's Reddit announcement](https://www.reddit.com/r/robotics/comments/1xdp3u1/we_turned_20_realworld_scans_from_new_yorknew/) explicitly says the release is not a validated robotics benchmark and some geometry/physics is inferred. Exact release timestamp was not established; checked October 24.

**Research implications:** Co²Gen generates collaborative demonstrations without human teleoperation; STAMP reuses human motion priors to connect robot motions; TerraTransfer++ removes expert-trajectory supervision but still uses paired images and scene states. These are different substitutions for specific training inputs, not evidence that all real-world collection disappears. Original abstracts and limits are in the table below.

### Embedded AI systems

**October 23 — iNGage secures €4M for inertial-sensor industrialisation.** The [company release](https://www.ingage-mems.com/ingage-secures-non-dilutive-deeptech-financing/) describes grants and loans from Bpifrance and banks, supporting technology transfer and a dedicated MEMS test laboratory. Its accelerometers, gyroscopes and IMUs target autonomous navigation when GNSS is unavailable. This is non-dilutive financing, not a new €4M equity round or proof of mass-production readiness.

**Inference research:** One-Step Euler finds that sufficiently trained, sufficiently large flow-matching VLAs can often match multi-step sampling with one inference step across six studied policies. This is a conditional result, not permission to remove control validation. F2G-Pose reports 28 FPS pose-only inference from segmented RGB-D, using synthetic task-specific supervision plus an externally pretrained backbone; it is not a fully synthetic-from-scratch system. Multi-Tool Selection quantifies a local-retrieval versus large-cloud-model quality/latency tradeoff. See original papers below.
