### Data collection

**November 18: Humyn's financing targets the whole data-to-deployment operation.** The official release names proprietary capture devices, lab-specific requirements and labelled data with provenance across sight, sound, motion and touch. Its reported **$50M+ annualised revenue** is a run rate, not audited annual revenue; its footprint and operational claims are company statements. **November 18: Ego2X and SHAPE make the supervision gap explicit.** Ego2X encodes contact anchors, relative forces and interaction states, improving retargeted contact realization on EgoDex from 27.9% to 62.9% in the reported comparison. Its pipeline/data release is promised, not verified as downloaded. SHAPE finds that joint readout suits precise control with robot demonstrations, while end-effector readout better supports human-to-robot transfer. Neither establishes that arbitrary POV hours are interchangeable with action-grounded recordings. [Ego2X](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24138) · [SHAPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24585)

**Catch-up: Axonnic's November 16 founder launch and November 4 benchmark page.** A 64-channel EMG sleeve records forearm muscle activity while leaving the hands bare, synchronized with hand tracking and force references. The founders report industrial recordings and a manufacturer LOI; this is not a proven production robot deployment or a paid outside-vendor request. Re-wear calibration, occlusion and force-reference tests matter more than a single attractive demo. Hardware-versus-headset comparisons across different studies are not controlled head-to-head trials. [Founder account](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50522776) · [Benchmark details](https://axonnic.com/benchmarks)

HOForce requires RGB-D and known object properties to estimate interaction forces. DiWALI learns from action-free human play but still uses a robot-command decoder trained on robot play. Phoenix's approximately 39,000-hour representation-learning corpus mixes ego and robot footage; its robot subset serves a different training role. See the original papers below.

### Data annotation & validation

**Grana, November 18:** sparse subtask boundaries plus action-quality labels become dense progress targets. Elapsed time alone can confuse slow execution, failure and progress reversal. This is a concrete annotation protocol to inspect for long-horizon demonstration QA. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24926)

**HandWorldBench and StrideWorld, November 18:** hand control does not guarantee persistent object states; visually plausible world-model output can still deform geometry or lose action consistency. HandWorldBench evaluates 500 manually selected clips from a 20,500-clip curated pool. StrideWorld uses fixed references; unresolved count placeholders in its abstract are not converted into invented totals. [HandWorldBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24731) · [StrideWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24408)

**SeRA and F³:** failed/autonomous experience can contribute useful supervision if relabelled or compressed appropriately. Agreement between later policies is not proof of correct labels; SeRA retains uncertainty through action sets. These papers support tracking corrections and failure context, not automatically labelling all rollout video as good demonstrations. [SeRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24273) · [F³](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24708)

| 2026-11-18 | [Ego2X: Contact-aware Transfer from Egocentric Human Data to Any Robot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24138) | Ego2X preserves object contacts when converting ego videos to robot data; RoboTwin success rises from 40.5% to 67.0%. | 2026-11-18 | [HOForce: Motion and Force Reconstruction of Hand-Object Interaction Using a Single-View RGB-D Video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23871) | HOForce estimates interaction forces from single-view RGB-D and known object properties, validated against tactile measurements. | 2026-11-18 | [StrideWorld: Benchmarking Scene Rigidity and Action Consistency in Interactive Video World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24408) | StrideWorld fixes geometry/action references before evaluation so scene drift cannot be absorbed by fitted references; model/video-count placeholders remain unresolved in the abstract. |

| 2026-11-18 | [HandWorldBench: A Unified Benchmark for Hand-Centric World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24731) | HandWorldBench curates 20,500 ego clips from 600+ hours and manually selects 500 for evaluation; eleven models reveal gaps between hand control and persistent object-state fidelity. |
| 2026-11-18 | [F$^3$: What Makes Good Experience in World-Action Models?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24708) | F³ compresses successful and failed rollouts into verified operation records; experience usefulness depends on representation and context, not source success alone. |

| 2026-11-18 | [SHAPE: Discrete Supervision and Parallel Continuous Streams for Learning Dexterous Manipulation from Egocentric Human Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24585) | SHAPE combines human action-token supervision with joint and end-effector output streams; the best readout differs between precise robot-data control and human transfer. |
