    Research papers worth a look

    One newly catalogued preprint, explicitly catch-up: Pixel-UMI: Learning Environment-Aware Whole-Body Manipulation from Sensorized Human Demonstrations, submitted November 2. Environment-Aware Retargeting combines reconstructed geometry with head/gripper trajectories to produce collision-aware robot targets; delay calibration assigns actions, and masking reduces dependence on mismatched viewpoints. On-policy human corrections address contact limitations. Authors report 87% average task progress across five tasks, not 87% full-task success. Results are author-reported and were not reproduced here.
