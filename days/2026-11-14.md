### Data collection

**Capture structure is becoming more explicit.** VBot's November 12 paper pairs ego/exo recording with camera geometry, semantic masks and hand keypoints. HEIR pairs gaze, speech and ego video with teleoperated robot trajectories and session continuity. VI AMO adds action-and-mission labels to teleoperation episodes. These are different data products; none is an advertised paid supplier brief. Practical inference: ask buyers which synchronized streams and temporal labels they need before committing to volume. [VBot](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16417) · [HEIR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17238) · [VI AMO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16419)

### Data annotation & validation

**Evaluate information and decisions, not only plausible video.** New studies test whether imagined rollouts preserve obstacle information, whether predictions respond correctly to alternative actions, and how much performance comes from the predictor versus simply retaining the latest observation. These support evaluation sets with counterfactual actions, failure states and persistence baselines. Evidence is bounded by the studied models and tasks. [Epistemic gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14955) · [Action consequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15048) · [Persistence baseline](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15082)
