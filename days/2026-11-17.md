### Data collection

**Catch-up: better demonstrations require more than more footage.** HALO, submitted November 12, combines kinesthetic bimanual teaching with a low-resistance mobile platform, then calibrates motion, aligns timing and removes demonstrator hands from observations. TouchTwin, November 13, uses head/wrist RGB-D and an 880-taxel glove to reconstruct contact-aware simulated demonstrations. Its reported 20/20 held-out replay successes are simulation results. The collection implication is specific: pose video does not directly measure contact force, and training/deployment alignment needs explicit processing. [HALO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16439) · [TouchTwin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17619)

MiniPose explores lower-cost real RGB-D capture using miniature industrial pipe replicas, while Fog-FWL adds waveform-level fog data and matched synthetic training. These are research approaches and datasets, not commissioned collection calls. [MiniPose](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15066) · [Fog-FWL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16742)

**Audit what an action representation actually captures.** LASR tests action-related predictive information against state/task shortcuts on held-out episodes. A separate driving study finds that plausible camera movement can conceal poor scene response to commanded plans. AAELI studies adaptive human auditing of LLM labels, but is not a demonstrated robotics-QC service. Together these support explicit error taxonomies and targeted review; they do not establish a universal replacement for human validation. [LASR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17775) · [Driving evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15260) · [AAELI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15197)

### Research papers worth a look

**21 additions: 16 preprints and five company/investor/technical records; 1,643 library entries total.** The accessible four arXiv subject lists showed November 16 listings. Original submission dates below are November 9–13, so these are dated catch-up, not papers published November 17. Results are author-reported; experiments were not reproduced and promised code/datasets were not presumed available. Already-covered RobotAgentBench, E0.5 and WorldOracle were deduplicated.

| Original date | Paper | Finding / limit |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-13 | [Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18562) | PEAR trains a 27B reasoning model with robot-use reinforcement learning entirely in simulation without demonstrations; 60% success in the authors’ real dual-arm trials versus zero for the base model. |
| 2026-11-13 | [TouchTwin: Scaling Human Touch Data via Real-to-Sim-to-Real](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17619) | TouchTwin uses head/wrist RGB-D and an 880-taxel glove to ground simulated replays in measured human touch; replay success results are predominantly simulation tests. |
| 2026-11-13 | [Beyond Prediction Gain: LASR as a Metric for Action-Grounded Latents in World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17775) | LASR audits whether latents predict actions and physical state changes beyond state or task shortcuts using episode-held-out data; predictive support is not proof of causality. |
| 2026-11-13 | [Joint World Action Models through Differentiable Trajectory Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17626) | WATO uses differentiable trajectory optimization during training, then generates and ranks candidates at deployment; average success rises from 79.9% to 91.7% over seven visual-control environments. |
| 2026-11-13 | [VLAN: Vision-Language Accessible Navigation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18346) | VLAN describes 1,000 scenes, 20,000 trajectories and 160,000 keyframes with embodiment-specific accessibility labels; dataset/code availability is author-reported, not tested here. |
| 2026-11-12 | [HALO: Human-Aligned Data Collection for WhoLe-body MObile Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16439) | HALO aligns kinesthetic whole-body demonstrations with deployment through calibration, temporal alignment and hand masking; up to 2.9x faster collection in the authors’ comparisons. |
| 2026-11-12 | [ActWorld-JEPA: Action-Conditioned JEPA World Models Meet Latent Planning for End-to-End Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16579) | ActWorld-JEPA combines latent future prediction and candidate reranking; NAVSIM v1 selector calibration uses aggregate NAVTEST feedback, limiting clean holdout interpretation. |
| 2026-11-12 | [Fog-FWL: A Large-Scale Full-Waveform LiDAR Dataset for Recovering Fog-Hidden Objects](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16742) | Fog-FWL proposes 7,527 real foggy full-waveform LiDAR frames and 12,000 synthetic frames with aligned labels; public dataset/simulator release is promised, not verified. |
| 2026-11-11 | [MiniPose: Miniature-Based Real Data Collection for 6D Pose Estimation of Large Industrial Pipe Segments](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15066) | MiniPose combines at most 1,000 real miniature RGB-D images per pipe type with 10,000 synthetic images; tests two pose estimators, not arbitrary industrial objects. |
| 2026-11-11 | [Fast Inference-Time Policy Refinement via Action-Affine World Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15011) | A²WM learns local action sensitivities offline and refines with a quadratic program; 3.8ms mean overhead in four simulated tasks excludes base-policy sampling. |
| 2026-11-11 | [Bind-WAM: Binding-Uncertainty-Guided Selective Future Imagination in World Action Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15368) | Bind-WAM pseudo-labels object-reference mismatch, invisibility and identity drift from demonstrations to train a router for selective future imagination. |
| 2026-11-11 | [What World Model Does Active Object Search Need? A Study under Imperfect Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15689) | BAMOS tests belief updates and detector errors in simulated active object search; maintaining target belief matters more as detection errors grow. |
| 2026-11-11 | [Driving World Models Move the Camera, Not the Scene](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15260) | Driving-world-model evaluation separates camera motion from actual scene response; retimed logs and stereo views provide real counterfactual references. |
| 2026-11-11 | [Anytime-Valid Empirical Likelihood Inference from LLM Annotations with Adaptive Human Auditing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15197) | AAELI allocates human audits using LLM outputs and estimates human-label means with anytime-valid confidence sequences; experiments are not robotics-specific QC validation. |
| 2026-11-11 | [AFTER: A World Model with Adaptive Fast--Slow Timescales for Error-Driven Retention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15215) | AFTER-WM adapts to persistent dynamics changes with fast and slow corrections; five simulated datasets plus real UAV data, with code/data release still promised. |
| 2026-11-09 | [Learning Heterogeneous Tactile Representation for General Dexterous Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12806) | LingBot-Touch learns shared representations across magnetic, piezoresistive and visuotactile sensors; source is Apache 2.0, while linked model weights require access permission. |
