### Data collection

- **The Gasgoo data-gap survey is the most useful sizing document this column has seen all year.** Beyond the 500K-vs-10M-hour headline, the named specifics: Noematrix's RoboPocket has logged 100,000+ hours of home-scenario data across 47 Chinese cities. Daimon Robotics released Daimon-Infinity, billed as the largest tactile-inclusive full-modal dataset, 110,000 sensing units at 120Hz visuo-tactile. Machine Science claims its RoboMirage sim engine cuts per-datapoint cost to between 1/20th and 1/200th of real capture. JAKA collects from an installed base of 30,000 industrial robots. Read it as a competitive map: China's collection layer is already segmenting into home-scenario, tactile, sim and fleet-harvest specialists, all quoting hours and unit counts in public. Nobody in the survey claims validated delivery against a buyer spec. [Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/market-industry/embodied-intelligence-data-how-long-until-supply-is-sufficient-2080910421824348161)
- **NEURA Robotics opened a "NEURA Gym" at RWTH Aachen on July 24, one of ten planned dedicated physical-AI training facilities.** A university-sited building whose whole job is generating robot training data. Between this, Agility's 60,000 sq ft Fremont site and Tesla's data-first Optimus framing, the training farm is now a standard line item in every serious humanoid P&L. [The Robot Report](https://www.therobotreport.com/neura-robotics-establishes-neura-gym-rwth-aachen-train-physical-ai/)
- **AXIS (arXiv 2607.21588, submitted July 23) is a community-driven data engine that does crowd-scale demonstration collection through browser-based teleoperation, with automated success checking, quality filtering and trajectory smoothing built in.** Currently 207 tasks and 50K+ trajectories with a held-out eval protocol. The interesting part is the pipeline shape: collection and validation fused into one loop, which is the architecture argument we make for capture-time QC. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21588)

### Data annotation & validation

- **RoboInter1.5 (arXiv 2607.18709, submitted July 21) is the strongest annotation release this window: 230K+ manipulation episodes across 571 scenes with dense per-frame annotations spanning 10+ intermediate-representation types.** Subtasks, primitive skills, object and gripper grounding, segmentation, affordance, grasp poses, plus benchmarks and trained models. The expected annotation payload on a manipulation dataset keeps compounding. Every new representation type a public release ships with becomes a line in the next buyer spec. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18709)
- **Scale Up Strategically (arXiv 2607.21582, Northeastern + NVIDIA) formalizes "instruction factor bias" in fine-tuned robot policies and finds a consistent shortcut ordering across six foundation policies: color beats object beats spatial beats verb beats size.** It then prescribes which data to collect to fix compositional generalization. Papers that tell labs what to collect next are demand signals for collectors; this one says the next wave of orders is biased toward verb and size diversity, which is a capture-spec instruction. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21582)

### Research papers worth a look

- **AXIS: Growable Community-Driven Data Engine (arXiv 2607.21588, July 23).** Full read in the collection section above. Browser teleop collection with automated QC, 207 tasks, 50K+ trajectories. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21588)
- **RoboInter1.5: Intermediate Representation Suite (arXiv 2607.18709, July 21).** Full read in the annotation section above. 230K+ episodes, 571 scenes, 10+ annotation types per frame. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18709)
- **HOST: Robots Acquire Manipulation Skills in Seconds from a Single Human Video (arXiv 2607.20033, July 22).** One-shot human-to-robot skill transfer with no training-time loop: estimates task progress from the human video, translates it into predicted robot observations, derives actions, and keeps previously mastered skills intact. Every improvement in one-shot transfer raises the value of each captured human demonstration. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20033)
- **Scale Up Strategically: Bias-Aware Evaluation and Data Collection (arXiv 2607.21582, July 23; Northeastern + NVIDIA).** Full read in the annotation section above. The color-object-spatial-verb-size shortcut hierarchy, plus collection prescriptions. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21582)
- **DEED: Closing the Lab-to-Store Gap (arXiv 2607.20345, July 22).** Full read in the humanoids section above. GR00T N1.6 on a Unitree G1-Edu restocking a real supermarket. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20345)
- **KineBench (arXiv 2607.19876, July 22; ECCV 2026).** First IDM-free closed-loop benchmark for embodied world models, replacing brittle inverse-dynamics action extraction with explicit kinematic grounding. Cleaner world-model evaluation means cleaner attribution of failures to data, which buyers will eventually use in acceptance testing. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19876)
- **Masked Visual Actions for Unified World Modeling (arXiv 2607.19343, July 21).** One video model runs both directions: reveal robot motion and it predicts forward dynamics, reveal object motion and it recovers robot behavior, fine-tuned with only 15 hours of masked examples. 15-hour fine-tunes keep shrinking the data quantity story while sharpening the data quality one. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19343)
- **FELT (arXiv 2607.20683, July 22).** Synthesizes per-finger tactile signals from RGB video, reducing the need for tactile-equipped collection rigs. If touch can be inferred from pixels at usable fidelity, the capture spec debate shifts from which sensors to carry toward which ground truth to verify against. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20683)
