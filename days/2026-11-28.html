    Data annotation & validation

    November 25 — failure intervals and rejected attempts are useful evidence. FailPoint-X records 2,823 takeover attempts, including 2,400 accepted recoveries, and evaluates from matched failure states. Its reported recovery gains coexist with losses from nominal task starts. It supports preserving failure context, rejected attempts and held-out normal-task tests when assessing recovery datasets. Preprint

    Also newly discovered: BOP-Refer combines machine-generated, human-verified language with pose-derived spatial labels; AgenTAP reconnects occluded tracks for EgoTAP-4D; a failure-driven driving framework allocates synthetic data to measured weaknesses. These are distinct evaluation/annotation methods, not interchangeable claims that automated labels need no review. Original links and limits below. Checks of major annotation vendors did not establish another fresh, source-verified collection-partner mandate.

    Research papers worth a look

    26 newly discovered preprints, all originally November 25 except PTQ4WAM (November 23, revised November 25). These are author-reported research findings; release promises are not treated as completed downloads or commercial licences. All are now in the searchable library.

    Paper What it adds and limits
    Lab2Sim: Agentic Generation of Interactive Laboratory Equipment from Operation Videos Converts ego or multi-view laboratory operation videos into editable simulation assets, using separate evidence, construction, verification and integration stages. Research result, not a collection order.
    M2-WAM: Efficient Hierarchical Memory Across Modalities and Timescales for World-Action Models Separates visual and executed-action memory at short and long timescales; reports bounded online storage and gains on memory-dependent tasks. Benchmarks do not establish universal long-horizon reliability.
    FailPoint-X: A Policy-Matched, Failure-Anchored Benchmark for Offline VLA Post-Training from Human Takeovers Logs 2,823 human takeover attempts and 2,400 accepted recoveries. Recovery cloning improves held-out failure recovery but can damage nominal-start performance; both tests matter.
    Integration of Vision-Language-Action Control for Autonomous Forklift Unloading with Limited Real-World Data Forklift adaptation uses 9.38 hours of simulation and 0.8 hours of real demonstrations, tested in 72 trials. Narrow task evidence, not proof that all industrial collection needs little real data.
    HoloBrain-0.5 Technical Report Pretraining combines over 10,000 hours across 15 embodiment categories with standardized kinematics and calibrated cameras. Reported progress and success metrics are task-specific, not interchangeable.
    SkySearcher: High-Altitude Onboard VLM Reasoning for Aerial Semantic Search Combines onboard aerial VLM reasoning with a lightweight probabilistic map; includes simulation evaluation and a drone deployment demonstration, without proving fleet-scale reliability.
    Quantization for Action, Execution for Deployment: Joint Precision and Execution Selection for World Action Models PTQ4WAM jointly selects weight precision and execution routes under memory, latency and task-evaluation limits. Original November 23, revised November 25; code release is promised.
    JOVE ICE: Intent Guided Cloud Edge Device Collaboration for AI Glasses Routes AI-glasses requests between glasses, phone and cloud by intent; evaluates latency and token reductions. Useful workload-design evidence, not a standalone hardware launch.
    A Failure-Driven Data Flywheel for Post-Training Vision-Language-Action Models Targets synthetic scenario generation at measured VLA failure conditions. Driving tests expose the difference between anticipatory slowing and braking after a pedestrian appears; real-vehicle sample is small.
    Who Is Ego? Scaling Cross-View Camera Wearer Identification and Segmentation beyond Humans FindEgo-1M covers 1.16 million real/simulated ego-exo pairs across 12 wearer types. Studies wearer identification and segmentation; data/code/models are promised, not verified downloaded.
    BOP-Refer: Benchmark for Monocular 3D and 2D Object Grounding Pairs nine BOP datasets with generated, human-verified referring expressions and pose-derived 2D/3D boxes. Reported monocular depth errors remain much larger than 2D grounding errors.
    GlassGround: GUI Grounding Beyond Native Screenshots with Egocentric Wearable Vision GlassGround pairs screenshots with wearable-camera views in 3,250 tasks across four devices. Benchmark exposes a large camera-domain gap; paper links dataset and code.
    Event-WAM: Learning to Act with Event-Derived Futures Uses event-like changes derived from RGB as auxiliary future supervision, requiring no event camera at inference. Robustness benefits vary by benchmark.
    WorldInteract: Controllable Streaming Video Generation for Multi-Material Interactions Separates material behavior from contact response, training on 30,000 simulated non-interaction videos across 22 materials. Video-control gains are not robot-policy validation.
    Tracking Any Point with Agentic Visual Reasoning AgenTAP reasons over point identity and occlusion, reconnecting tracks to build EgoTAP-4D from long ego videos. Human-agreement findings are sampled, not a guarantee of error-free labels.
    RegHarness: Regulating What Code-as-Policy Agents Keep from Steps to Skills RegHarness evaluates proposed program steps and governs reuse of revised skills. Simulation improvements depend on controlled reset/replay and author-enabled updates.
    SEEK: Self-Evolution through Execution Knowledge for Agentic Robot Manipulation SEEK retains executable procedures and measured action effects between attempts with a fixed base model; evaluates improvement and fewer model calls on RoboDojo.
    Targeted Adaptation of World-Action Models via Interaction and Motion Heads HeadLight adapts interaction and motion attention heads using 1.1% of parameters; reports transfer and real-robot tests. This is targeted policy adaptation, not reduced capture requirements by default.
    Hetero-Associative Visuomotor Memory: Soft Recall as a Base for Learned Residuals in Few-Demonstration Imitation HAVM retrieves and corrects demonstrations for rapid few-shot imitation; gains depend on diverse human demonstrations and weaken on dense/scripted data. Broad unseen-task generalization is a tradeoff.
    M2AdaptVLA: Multimodal Memory-Augmented Vision-Language-Action Model with Predictive Test-Time Adaptation Combines visual and force/torque history with online prediction-based adaptation. Contact-sensitive tasks motivate synchronized wrench measurements alongside video.
    Nav4Manip: From Navigation to Dexterous Mobile Manipulation through Co-Evolution Nav4Manip couples approach positioning and dexterous manipulation; introduces Nav-N-Manip and paired trajectories with affordance supervision. Includes reported real-world evaluations.
    DriftWAM: Faster World-Action Models via Action-Aware Video Drifting DriftWAM distills video/action generation into fewer steps using action-aware matching; compares against other distillation methods at matched inference speed.
    Dynamic Forcing: Learning Action-Grounded Sparse Dynamic Representations with Dense Future Guidance for World Action Models CompactWAM retains current context and sparse dynamic tokens, avoiding dense future generation at inference. Reports benchmarks and real-world manipulation, not independent deployment validation.
    FoundSurface: Feedforward Ray-Based 3D Scene Surface Reconstruction from Unposed Images FoundSurface reconstructs continuous surfaces from unposed images using pointmap priors and ray-based refinement; evaluates cross-dataset generalization for scene reconstruction.
    ProManO: Beyond Sim(3) for Metric Upgrade of Feed-Forward Geometry with Visual-Inertial Anchors ProManO upgrades feed-forward geometry with calibrated visual-inertial anchors and uncertainty weighting. Explains why a simple similarity alignment can leave depth-dependent error.
    Measuring the Value of One Shared Image at Robot Handoffs in Vision-and-Language Navigation Controlled navigation handoffs test one image alongside executed actions. Benefits are measured against a frozen receiver; route-specificity and some controlled-start questions remain unresolved.
