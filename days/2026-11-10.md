### Data collection

**November 9 · Human-1M's open 100,000-hour slice makes the expected deliverable more concrete.** NVIDIA's [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-Human-1M-100K) describes synchronized mono/stereo ego footage, optional wrist views, task summaries, timed action segments, head pose and 21 hand keypoints with confidence/masks. It specifies at least 30 FPS / 480p, unaltered speed, and hands visible for at least 95% of an episode. Timed segments cover 1–10 seconds and identify the hand, action and object. The card labels the release CC BY 4.0. The public viewer contains a small preview; the entire 100,000-hour payload was not downloaded or independently audited. The October Human-1M announcement was already covered; the open subset and published specification are today's follow-through.

**November 9 · Noitom HiPHI-Hand is accessible as a gated research dataset.** The company announced **11 hours** of synchronized dual-fisheye video with optical hand-motion ground truth used for EgoHand training. The [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/noitomrobotics/HiPHI-Hand) specifies 1360×1360 H.264 at 30 FPS, hardware synchronization, per-dataset calibration and 21-/25-joint hand annotations in a shared IMU coordinate frame. It requires a questionnaire and research-license acceptance; commercial licensing is a separate route. No access request was submitted. [November 9 company release](https://portal.sina.com.hk/finance/finance-globenewswire/globenewswire/2026/11/09/1982111/noitom-robotics-releases-hiphi-hand-precision-hand-capture-data-used-to-train-nvidia-egohand-and-demonstrates-real-time-hand-reconstruction-at-corl-2026/).

**Interpretation:** the useful comparison is accepted, synchronized, well-described episodes with traceable calibration and rights. Neither dataset release is a public request to hire collection vendors.
