### Data collection

- **XDOF is the item of the window and it is covered in the TL;DR above.** The one detail worth pulling out separately is the job architecture. XDOF is not hiring "data collectors." It is hiring teleoperators and egocentric operators as two separate roles with different equipment, which is the first time a funded company has split the capture workforce that way in public. It is also releasing ABC with Berkeley AI Research at [abc.bot](https://abc.bot), billed as the largest high-quality robot training dataset assembled. The underlying ABC-130k set on Hugging Face is gated and was last modified August 29. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/xdof-just-three-months-out-of-stealth-is-in-talks-for-a-series-b-at-a-1-2b-valuation/), [ABC-130k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/XDOF/ABC-130k)
- **Figure's Index numbers are the first real operating metrics anyone has published for crowdsourced ego capture, and they are worth reading closely.** Announced August 25 and never covered here, so it is going in under the missed-signal rule. 16 million video uploads, 108 countries, 264,000 app downloads, 44,000+ weekly active users, 30 minutes of footage ingested per second which works out to roughly 4.9 years of human work per day. $15M paid out to contributors so far against $1B+ committed over twelve months. The diversity claim is the number to hold: per 1,000 hours logged they report 373 unique tasks, 1,146 distinct objects and 116 unique environments. Their pipeline runs filtering, fraud review, embedding-based deduplication, rebalancing against task quotas to surface rare edge cases, then hierarchical annotation aligning high-level task captions to low-level manipulations. Every one of those five stages is a QC problem, and four of them are the ones that break at scale. [Figure](https://www.figure.ai/news/introducing-index), [coverage](https://www.humanoidsdaily.com/news/figure-ai-unveils-index-crowdsourcing-real-world-human-video-to-train-helix)

Sellers set their own price, the platform takes a margin, the commercial licence settles at checkout. Datasets arrive in LeRobot v3.0 or proprietary formats with chain-of-custody documentation and get scored A+ to F on four quality pillars. Board chair Erik Ålgård previously backed 1X. Seed round opens Q4 2026. Note the disclosure attached to the source: humanoid.guide's publisher is a Kinetic Blocks co-founder, so treat the framing as promotional and the mechanics as real. ### Data annotation & validation

- **Scale Labs published the sharpest piece of the window and it is about verifiers, not labels.** "Who Grades the Graders? Rethinking Verifier Design for Computer Use Agents," September 2, fourteen authors. The argument is that the programmatic verifiers sitting behind benchmarks like OSWorld 2.0 are load-bearing for both evaluation and training signal, and that verifier design is currently under-engineered relative to how much weight it carries. Read that across to robot data and it says the validation layer, not the capture layer, is where quality is actually decided. That is our thesis being written up by the biggest incumbent in the category. [Scale Labs](https://labs.scale.com/blog/verifier-design-for-cua)
- **Mercor released a full 397B RL training recipe and then published the ablation that says data beats algorithms by 3x.** September 1, with Berkeley Sky Computing and SkyRL, open weights and eval traces. Post-training Qwen3.5-397B-A17B moved APEX-Agents Pass@1 from 16.11% to 27.29%, a 70% relative gain, on 1,928 expert-created tasks in consulting, banking and corporate law against 480 held-out tasks. The two numbers to keep: harness fixes with no training at all moved a base model from 22.74% to 28.69% mean reward, which they say is about what one epoch of training would have bought, and across the work algorithm choices moved things around 4 points while data moved both models 10 to 12 points. A data vendor publishing its own ablation showing data dominates is self-serving and also appears to be correct. [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/blog/training-frontier-knowledge-work-agents-a-397b-rl-training-guide-with-skyrl/), [code](https://github.com/Mercor-Intelligence/ApexAgents-SkyRL-Recipe)
- **Snorkel is trying to turn benchmarks into a subscription.** "Terminal-Bench 4.0: Why Continuous Benchmarks Require Continuous QA," August 28, argues benchmarks have to become continuously maintained products rather than static datasets. On September 3 they ran an OSWorld 2.0 reading group noting frontier agents complete only 20.6% of 108 long-horizon computer-use workflows outright after 300+ steps each. Continuously maintained ground truth is a recurring revenue line, and it is the same shape as a capture contract with a standing QC obligation attached. [Snorkel](https://snorkel.ai/blog/terminal-bench-4-continuous-qa/), [OSWorld piece](https://snorkel.ai/blog/osworld-2-0-why-computer-use-agents-fail-most-tasks/)
- **CometVLA is the best third-party evidence yet that an aligned annotation layer measurably moves policy success.** arXiv 2608.30289, August 31. It names the data pyramid directly, teleoperation and simulation and egocentric trajectories and VQA, and attacks the fact that egocentric video currently gets used only as auxiliary pre-training. It releases CometData and CometBench, an embodied physical-VQA corpus strictly aligned to the robot's own action data and embodiment, and shows correlation between VQA benchmark score and downstream VLA success. Alignment of the label to the embodiment is doing the work, not label volume. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30289)
- **Puffin-World auto-annotated 44.5M images with gravity-aware camera labels, and head-mounted capture is exactly where that matters.** arXiv 2609.04196, published September 3, ACE Robotics with NTU S-Lab. They used it to label 28 public datasets with absolute roll, pitch and vertical field of view, and released Puffin-16M with 15M vision-language-camera triplets and 1M trajectories. 29 datasets on Hugging Face already cite it. A headcam is never level. Absolute orientation labels on ego footage are a cheap annotation product that nobody is currently selling. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04196), [project](https://kangliao929.github.io/projects/puffin-world/)
- **A temporal-robustness study found an expert holds 84% where an imitation policy drops to 53%, on the same demonstrations.** arXiv 2609.01453, September 1. On the ParcelStow task a scripted expert and an ACT policy both hit 100% at nominal speed. At the maximum demonstrated speed the expert holds 84% and ACT falls to 53%. 35 of 47 ACT failures were insertion misalignments, and none of the 414 acquisitions that lacked force closure ever completed the task. This is the cleanest single citation available for demonstration count not being demonstration coverage, and it is a speed axis that no data contract currently specifies. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01453), [code](https://github.com/coenwerem/parcelstow)
- **Behavior-Skill says failure is highly non-uniform across skills, which is an argument for targeted collection over hours.** arXiv 2608.30536, August 31, NUDT and Hunan University. 235,492 skill instances from 10,000 demonstrations across 50 household tasks and 34 skill categories, each pairing a skill instruction with an aligned observation-action segment plus a restorable intermediate state and a success condition. Tested against π0.5 and GR00T. Contact-rich manipulation is the persistent bottleneck. Selling undifferentiated hours is the wrong product if the buyer's failures cluster in six skill categories. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30536)
- **SolarWM is the data-provenance paper of the window and it should be read as a spec.** arXiv 2609.02886, September 2, CUHK-Shenzhen. Their engine converts 1.43M canonical clips from 10 datasets into one frame-aligned contract carrying visual observations, metric camera geometry, captions, quality metadata, selection decisions and provenance, deliberately separating per-source processing from mixture construction. Four models at 5B to 33B, data and pipeline and recipes and weights and framework all released. Selection decisions as a first-class recorded field is precisely what an audit trail is. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02886), [project](https://junchao-cs.github.io/SolarWM-Web/)
- **A controlled study found open-loop rollout error picks the wrong estimator in 18 of 24 conditions.** arXiv 2609.02811, September 2. Replay position RMSE correlated with closed-loop cross-track RMSE at Spearman 0.923 against 0.774 for 20-step measurement-free rollout, and the rollout metric misranked in 18 of 24 sensing conditions against 5 of 24 for replay. Anyone validating capture quality against a world-model proxy rather than a closed-loop run is measuring the wrong thing. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02811), [code](https://github.com/rdharini2001/Robot_World_Model)
- **Scene-graph plus temporal logic gives you formally specified retrieval over unlabelled egocentric video.** arXiv 2609.00333, August 31, McMaster. Egocentric sensor streams plus HD maps become sequences of scene graphs, then Linear Temporal Logic formulas and an off-the-shelf model checker mine every instance of a specified scenario out of unlabelled data. Evaluated on Argoverse 2 across 850 fifteen-second real driving logs. This turns a corpus into something you can run coverage analysis against, which is a much better thing to sell than an hour count.  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00333)

### Embedded AI systems

- **Somebody ran 10,000 inferences of Cosmos-Reason2 on a Quest 3 and the results kill a few assumptions.** Published September 2 by XRLab at Manisa Celal Bayar University, benchmarked against Meta's Aria Digital Twin egocentric dataset, run on a Quest 3 and a Mac mini. Three findings. The 2B model at Q8 beats the 8B at Q4 on every metric and is the only one that fits. Vision encoding is the bottleneck rather than the language model, at 45.3 seconds per frame on-device against 3.2 seconds on the Mac. And power, not thermals, is the binding constraint, with the headset net-discharging under sustained VLM load even while plugged in. They also report a clean null result, that a good synthetic twin was statistically indistinguishable from real capture. This is community-published and unrefereed, no backing paper, so treat the numbers as directional. It is still the single most useful item in the sweep for anyone designing capture-time inference into a head-worn rig. [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/ErenAta00/cosmos-reason2-quest3)
- **AdaVLA accelerates flow-matching VLAs at inference with no training data, and says out loud why that matters.** arXiv 2608.29208, August 29, accepted at IROS 2026. Uses flow-trajectory curvature as an action-confidence metric to cut ODE steps and adapt MLP pruning ratios online. Benchmarked on LIBERO running on a Jetson AGX Orin. The motivation stated in the paper is that acceleration methods requiring training data are unusable because of privacy and proprietary concerns, which is a useful third-party citation the next time somebody argues edge inference is a preference rather than a constraint. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29208)
- **DriftingVLA generates a whole action chunk in one forward pass at 3.36x speedup.** arXiv 2608.29749, August 30, USTC. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29749)
- **A free signal already sitting inside every VLA tells you when to stop executing open-loop.** arXiv 2609.00908, September 1. Action-to-observation cross-attention entropy rises to a plateau as the prediction horizon extends, and that plateau correlates with higher action error. They turn it into a training-free truncation rule using attention weights the policy already computes, beating fixed-horizon and prior adaptive chunking on π0.5 and X-VLA across RoboTwin 2.0, LIBERO and three real tasks. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00908)

- **DemoMimic generalises from a single demonstration by conditioning on contact-local geometry.** arXiv 2609.01938, September 1, Stanford with Fei-Fei Li, Jiajun Wu, Dorsa Sadigh and Jeannette Bohg. ### Research papers worth a look

Papers already written up in the sections above are not repeated here. What follows is the rest of the window.

- **Motus2: A Self-Evolving General World Model for Dexterous Manipulation.** arXiv 2608.30237, August 31, Tsinghua TSAIL and Shengshu lineage with Fan Bao and Jun Zhu, 19 authors. One shared-weight model exposing three interfaces, policy and action-conditioned simulator and value evaluator, in a closed loop. The data curriculum is the part to read: large-scale monocular egocentric, then synchronized stereo egocentric, then robot-domain adaptation with human-robot alignment data. It also treats failed and suboptimal interactions as supervision for dynamics and value rather than discarding them, which quietly makes reject footage an asset. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30237)
- **Unified Motion Retargeting for Humanoids with Learned Point Cloud Correspondence.** arXiv 2609.02134, September 2. Replaces hand-crafted sparse keypoint correspondences with learned dense point cloud correspondence, using exterior point clouds as a shared interface between human motion and any robot. Dense surface anchors let it transfer interaction contacts rather than just joint angles, and it removes the manual semantic design step that currently gates how much human motion converts into humanoid training data. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02134)
- **FoldingAgent: Inferring Parametric Origami Procedures from Demonstration Videos.** arXiv 2609.00377, August 31, accepted to SIGGRAPH Asia 2026. Converts unstructured human demonstration video into an executable parametric program using a VLM agent with tools for simulating geometric transitions, checking physical plausibility and self-evaluating. Because it acts sequentially and can re-plan, it suppresses the compounding error that normally kills multi-step inference from video. Unstructured demo video plus verification tooling yields structured physically valid action labels, which is the annotation thesis at small scale. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00377), [project](https://maya-moriya.github.io/origami-page/)
- **Beyond Object Selection: Markerless Gaze-based Robot Placement at Arbitrary Position.** arXiv 2609.00478, August 31. Markerless headset-to-robot alignment plus a cross-device dataset, and a task-level metric, Gaze-Surface Intersection Error, that measures the spatial error of the gaze-specified target rather than raw pose error. The finding to cite: translational and rotational calibration errors can cancel inside the transformed gaze ray, so methods that rank best on conventional pose metrics are not best on GSIE. Headset-derived signals have to be validated on the downstream task metric, not on calibration error. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00478)
- **SA-WAM: Spatially Aware World Action Model via Geometric Latent Diffusion.** arXiv 2609.02531, September 2, Cordelia Schmid's group. Folds depth into a frozen pretrained VAE tokenizer with no 3D-specific fine-tuning, and analyses the rollout-to-control correlation from the positive side. Read alongside arXiv 2609.02811 in the annotation section. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02531)
- **Latent Cluster Analysis for Vision-Language-Action Models.** arXiv 2609.02634, September 2. LAVLA does layer-wise latent cluster analysis on NVIDIA GR00T N1.5 with cross-attention-based embedding weighting, extracting human-interpretable concepts per cluster, and finds clusters progressively disentangle spatiotemporal and kinematic features through the middle layers. A plausible route to auditing whether a dataset's claimed diversity actually reaches the policy. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02634)
- **DREAM: Deployment-Time Demonstration Generation via Real-to-Sim for Scalable Policy Adaptation.** arXiv 2608.29078, August 29, University of Tokyo, Matsuo Lab. Generates VLA fine-tuning data for a new workspace from one capture plus a language instruction, by reconstructing the workspace, turning the instruction into symbolic goals and success criteria with an LLM, producing trajectories with TAMP, randomizing and verifying, then rendering image-action pairs. It benchmarks its collection cost directly against human teleoperation. This is the strongest counter-argument in the window to everything else in this brief, and it should be read as such rather than dismissed. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29078)
- **GHOST in the Robots: Real-Time Exocentric Dual-Robot VR Teleoperation from Onboard Cameras.** arXiv 2608.29080, August 29, Brown University, Stefanie Tellex and James Tompkin. One operator controls two mobile manipulators with direct low-level commands using only onboard RGB-D, no external mocap or instrumented cell, by aligning real-time point clouds with learned scene completion into an exocentric 3D workspace. 1.6x to 4x success rate over a tablet interface with 15 novices, 1.47x faster for experts. Throughput per operator is the denominator on cost per demonstration, and it moves more than hardware price does. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29080)
- **Aero Hand Open: A Simulation-Ready Tendon-Driven Hand for Dexterous Manipulation Learning.** arXiv 2608.28578, August 28. Full open release of mechanical design, a sim model reproducing the cable transmission, an identified bidirectional actuation map including three-way thumb coupling, and an RL package. Zero-shot sim-to-real with no fine-tuning and no state estimation. Relevant to anyone doing CAD work on manipulation hardware. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28578), [project](https://tetheria.github.io/aero-hand-open/)
- **LightNav-0**, a 5B generalist navigation VLA on Qwen3-VL-4B with dual-channel pointing tokens for embodiment-agnostic spatial intent and a residual VQ action tokenizer, trained on 2K+ scenes and 4K+ hours. Apache-2.0, currently the top trending robotics model on Hugging Face, GGUF quantization already published. Paper arXiv 2608.30935, August 31. [model](https://huggingface.co/LightOriginsHQ/LightNav-0), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30935)
- **VLAct** deserves a flag despite a paper date of August 27, one day before the window, because the weights were updated September 4 and the headline number is the important one. On RoboCasa-GR1, an unseen humanoid embodiment, VLAct with only 20% of downstream trajectories beats full-data GR00T-N1.6. From Jiaya Jia and Hengshuang Zhao, 16 GPUs, fully open data. Representation-centric pretraining buying a 5x reduction in downstream demonstrations is a direct pressure on volume-priced collection. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27550), [weights](https://huggingface.co/StarVLA/VLAct_Qwen3_Pretrain)
- **VLANeXt**, a research codebase from S-Lab NTU published August 31, distills 12 design recipes from over 500 ablations with six released baselines from 0.8B to 8B. Two findings matter for head-worn capture. Action-unlabeled visual trajectories improve policies through latent-action pretraining, with VQ-VAE latents best at 97.6%. And adding visual frame history did not help and sometimes hurt, while historical proprioception did. The second one argues against longer video context and for better synchronized state, which is a rig design decision. [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/blog/cavanloy/vlanext)
- **NVIDIA Omniverse NuRec re-renders captured video from new sensor rigs** so a perception stack can move between platforms without recollecting, published August 31. This cuts against a rig-locked data moat, because captured data becomes viewpoint-portable. Worth tracking as a threat rather than a tool. [NVIDIA](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/scale-av-perception-across-vehicle-platforms-with-nvidia-omniverse-nurec/)
- **Two open datasets and one model release worth noting.** nvidia/SOMA-X v0.3.0 updated September 2 adds SOMAHandLayer, wrist-local hand layers at three levels of detail with MANO interoperability, unifying six identity backends onto canonical topology, Apache-2.0, which matters for retargeting hand data from human capture ([HF](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/SOMA-X)). OpenMOSS EasyWAM-MoT-Wan22 from Fudan, created August 31, a world-action model with separate Video DiT and Action DiT experts, 97.4% on LIBERO but weakest of its three variants on LIBERO-Plus at 56.8 against 72.4, which is a generalization warning ([HF](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/EasyWAM-MoT-Wan22)). MINT-SJTU RW-RL-Dataset, modified August 31, real-world robot RL with teleop plus human intervention, autonomous rollouts and reward and done labels, targeting 1000+ real robot hours ([HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MINT-SJTU/RW-RL-Dataset)).
- **Back-filled miss: NVIDIA GEAR's EgoScale, February 19, 2026, never covered in any prior brief.** A VLA trained on 20,854 hours of action-labelled egocentric human video, 20x larger than prior efforts, producing a log-linear scaling law between human-data scale and validation loss at R-squared 0.9983, and a 54% average success-rate improvement over a no-pretraining baseline on a 22-DoF hand. Lead author Ruijie Zheng, senior author Jim Fan. This is the canonical published scaling result for the egocentric thesis and it should have been in a brief seven months ago. Its companion, DreamDojo, built a generalist robot world model from 44k hours of egocentric human video using continuous latent actions as unified proxy actions to exploit unlabelled video. [EgoScale](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/egoscale/), [EgoScale arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16710), [DreamDojo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06949)
