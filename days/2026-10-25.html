Originally September 15, revised October 22: calibrated stereo supports metric hand/head reconstruction in unprepared environments, with independent optical motion-capture evaluation.     Data annotation & validation

    MaxLoop’s relevance is earlier rejection and correction during capture, rather than waiting until dataset delivery. Performance claims remain vendor-reported; no independent head-to-head audit was recovered.

    NavBind, October 21; revised October 22: 3,252 paired route-fork contexts hold the navigation state fixed while changing instructions. The evaluation shows why goal completion alone can conceal weak instruction following. Failure-Boundary Learning, September 5; revised October 22: real demonstrations and digital-twin rollouts localise where progress becomes failure, then shape the policy around that boundary. Both support testing counterfactuals and recovery cases, not just counting successful episodes. NavBind · Failure boundaries

    FluxVLA Engine, September 15; revised October 22: shared dataset, evaluation and robot interfaces connect simulation, human takeovers, correction collection and reward annotation. This is an engineering platform contribution, not a new dataset purchase. Paper

    Embedded AI systems

    October 23 — NVIDIA Bolt E4B availability now verified. The October 17 briefing described a scheduled release; the official model card now marks GA. It is a text-input/text-output assistant model based on Gemma-4-E4B, with 8B total / 4B effective parameters and Q4_K_M weights. Its intended local assistant and tool-use workloads are not a demonstrated VLA motor-control policy. The “4GB” product name should not be read as a measured total application-memory guarantee: the page also lists a 6.18GB GGUF artefact, and runtime/context overhead is separate. Official card

The announced Jetson Thor / AFE-A702 setup combines supported 2D, stereo and depth GMSL2 cameras, central configuration, hardware synchronisation and ROS 2 integration. The demonstration names Stereolabs, RealSense, Orbbec and Leopard Imaging models. Orin, Intel IPU7 and Qualcomm IQ9 expansion is described as planned, not all generally available. Compatibility and calibration still require checking against the exact capture rig. Company announcement
