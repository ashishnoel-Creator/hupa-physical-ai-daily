### Data collection

**October 1 · SoTa supplies a shared human/robot tactile layout.** Its conformable skin has **202 taxels**, with materials below **$10 per skin**; that is not a complete instrumented-glove price. Human and robot demonstrations share an encoder without a learned sensor mapping. With a fixed robot-data budget, human examples raise mean success from **22.8% to 45.9%** across eight conditions. Fabrication resources are promised, not established as released. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02338)

**October 1 · NEEDLEWORK preserves useful parts of failed recordings.** Short verified action bridges connect observations using RGB, proprioception and episode outcomes, without new environment interaction. Original demonstrations remain in training. The reported real-robot gain averages **21 percentage points over each task's strongest baseline**. Operational implication: retain outcomes and recoverable failure segments instead of treating every failed episode as waste. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02339)

**October 1 · MoRoOp presents an operational record, not just camera footage.** Nine eight-hour laboratory shifts yield **1,382 jobs, 4,815 operations, 19,352 dispatch events and 140,386 robot-state observations**. Delays, obstructed navigation and unsuccessful operations are retained, with raw and cleaned state tables. It is a laboratory logistics dataset, not a production-factory census. [Dataset paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02428)

**October 2 · DexJoCo-X controls the comparison across robot hands.** Seven hands, six tasks and **2,100 balanced demonstrations** share scenes, outcomes and execution interfaces. Results show that an action representation's usefulness depends on pretraining and architecture; simply expanding an action head is insufficient. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03278)

**October 2 · AdaTempo and SARI reduce different collection costs.** AdaTempo retimes demonstrations by task phase, reporting up to **3.57× speedup**, not a universal throughput gain. SARI collects physical contact phases and simulates diverse approaches: collection time falls **34.3%** in five tasks, but unseen-placement success is still **27.5%**. [AdaTempo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02706), [SARI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02804)

### Data annotation & validation

**October 1 · CriticHack shows why rising reward and success can conceal wrong-object errors.** Across the reported drawer-task runs, task success increases **10.2 points** while wrong-object failures increase **10.9 points**. A frozen outcome verifier redirects optimisation. The study argues for explicit error-type checks alongside aggregate success. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02527)

**October 2 · ManiPhysicsBench measures whether success damages the object.** Geometry, material properties and grasp conditions define solver-based damage thresholds. Continuous object-specific gripper labels improve preservation, but reduce ordinary task success and generalise imperfectly. A completed task and an undamaged object are separate acceptance criteria. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02802)

**October 2 · Dense instruction labels connect slower reasoning to fast local control.** A study combines autonomously generated demonstrations with corrective segments and language supervision at primitive, atomic and composite levels. Its long-horizon evaluations test whether local policies keep following changing instructions. This is technical evidence for richer labels, not a disclosed annotation contract. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03615)

### Embedded AI systems

**October 2 · HexVIO moves the visual frontend onto a commodity Hexagon DSP.** Stereo-inertial tracking retains a CPU backend and reports **30 FPS at 0.83 W**, equivalent to about 18 hours on the test device. The reported 67% power reduction and 86% throughput increase are evaluated configurations, not a guaranteed all-device improvement. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03283)

**October 2 · FastOPD trades some task performance for lower latency.** Its two-step distilled policy retains **84% of pi-0.5 performance** on LIBERO while reducing inference latency **78.1%**. The paper also evaluates other teachers and a compact real-robot student. Report the performance loss as well as the latency saving. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02832)

**October 2 · LLA-MPC adapts dynamics on embedded hardware.** F1TENTH experiments evaluate thousands of candidate models online to identify changing tyre parameters. Low-friction tests complete tracking tasks where a fixed model fails; this is adaptive control evidence rather than a new foundation model. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03616)

### Robotics & humanoids

**October 2 · EyeRobot 2.0 tests active gaze instead of wrist cameras.** It uses one swivelling stereo camera, fixation-relative actions and concentrated visual tokens. Across over 1,000 physical trials, it reports **69% versus 64%** success against an ego-plus-wrist policy when wrist views are clear, and **48% versus 22%** when grasped objects occlude them. These are camera-condition comparisons, not universal replacement of wrist sensing. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03710)

**October 2 · SimpleTouch adds touch during task fine-tuning.** A tactile expert uses a frozen pretrained tactile encoder and task demonstrations, without extra tactile-policy pretraining. It reports **71.3%** average across four real tasks. This still relies on pretrained representations and instrumented demonstrations. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02784)

**October 2 · Reflex policies and MobiAgent target recovery at different levels.** A frozen proprioceptive execution layer improves contact response, with peg-insertion success still **36–58%**. MobiAgent segments and verifies rollouts to extend a skill library, reporting Astribot S1 success rising **32.5% to 57.5%** through data recycling. Both retain meaningful failure rates. [Reflex policy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02811), [MobiAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03476)
