### Data collection

- **DexDirect (arXiv 2607.27784, UCLA RoMeLa, Dennis Hong's lab) proposes direct kinesthetic arm guidance for collecting dexterous demonstrations: the operator physically moves the robot arm rather than teleoperating through a leader rig.** Every reduction in collection-rig complexity lowers the capital cost of a demonstration and shifts the differentiator to how well the collection session is run and checked. Same week Fairino-style drag-teach recording is becoming the default on cheap industrial arms, the academic side is formalizing it for dexterous hands. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27784)
- **Practice Makes Policies (arXiv 2607.26809) bootstraps and consolidates robot capabilities from zero human demonstrations, letting the robot generate and refine its own training experience.** File under the standing tension in collection economics: every zero-demo result chips at volume demand for teleop hours, while raising the value of the human data that seeds and corrects what self-practice cannot reach. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26809)

### Data annotation & validation

- **CheckVLA is the strongest validation-layer release this window. The setup: VLA policies execute long-horizon mobile manipulation in open-loop action chunks, so one accidental deviation propagates silently through the remaining chunk.** CheckVLA runs a separately trained, frozen action-conditioned world model alongside the policy and flags divergence as it happens. At a matched 5% false-alarm rate it reaches 77.9% timely failure recall against 48.6% for an observation-only control and 37.9% for an action-shuffled control, and lifts RoboCasa365 success from 27.6% (periodic replanning) to 36.1%. Runtime verification with published recall and false-alarm numbers is acceptance-testing infrastructure, and every piece of it that matures makes validated delivery more checkable. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26789)
- **It's Not Just More Demos (arXiv 2607.27261, RSS 2026 Data-Centric Robotics workshop) asks which additional demonstrations actually repair a brittle policy, and answers with Counterfactual Nuisance Behaviour Cloning, an offline data-selection framework for targeted robustness repair.** Papers that rank which demos to collect next are purchase orders in preprint form. The buyer stops asking for more hours and starts asking for the specific coverage its policy is missing, which is a spec-driven collection business, not a volume one. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27261)

### Robotics & humanoids

- **Cross-Embodiment Transfer via Behavior-Aligned Representations (arXiv 2607.27549, Stanford, Dorsa Sadigh's group) tests whether embodiment-invariant intermediate representations (object boxes, language motions, end-effector traces) actually promote cross-embodiment transfer in VLAs, with a purpose-built simulation benchmark.** Behavior-aligned representations are annotation products. If they turn out to be the currency of cross-embodiment transfer, the annotation payload on every dataset grows again, and this benchmark is how labs will decide which representations to pay for. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27549)
