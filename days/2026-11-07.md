### Data collection

**November 6 · Physicl Studio targets failure-specific data.** The company announced a **$10M seed**, led by Inovia with US Venture Partners participating. Studio offers 10,000 environments, 100,000+ assets and controlled scene variations; its [current product page](https://physicl.ai/) identifies the offering as **private beta**. Rendered outputs include metric depth, normals, object instances, boxes and camera poses. Its SpAItial reconstruction improvement is company-reported, without an independently reproduced protocol here. This is a synthetic-data product, not a request to purchase footage. [Company release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/06/3397485/0/en/physicl-raises-10-million-to-solve-physical-ai-s-edge-case-gap.html).

**Catch-up · November 4–5 capture research.** [EMG-guided bottle manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07235) records human muscle activity as force references; [Semantics-to-Contact](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07532) uses human-in-the-loop residual RL and force feedback. Both show why RGB-only hours can miss task-relevant force information. [MetricTrail](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07555) improves streaming reconstruction with metric-depth constraints, while [FREE-LIVO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07463) tests asynchronous, nonrigid LiDAR-camera setups. These methods do not remove a customer's calibration, synchronization or sensor requirements.

### Data annotation & validation

**Catch-up · November 5: evaluate the annotations available at deployment.** [Egocentric Action Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07673) reports 68.4% Top-1 with annotated active-object boxes versus 49.0% with detector boxes; affordance filtering reaches 49.9%. The important comparison is against realistic detections, not an oracle that already identifies the relevant object.

[THG-Omni](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07836) links participant tracks to temporally grounded, checked semantic claims. [VideoRouteAudit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07329) holds model and frame budgets fixed: none of its tested selectors significantly beats uniform sampling on the full 900-item long-video set. [ReferClaw](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07683) asks clarifying questions before answering ambiguous ego-video requests. Together these are useful evaluation directions: trace the supporting evidence, isolate the intervention and record unresolved intent.

[Whole-body calibration](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07560) reports 0.50–1.11 mm mean held-out errors on two robots after jointly estimating waist/arms. [XM4D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07902) supplies optimality certificates for its formulated geometry problems; those certificates do not certify every physical property of the reconstructed scene.
