### Data annotation & validation

**Validate outcomes independently of the training loop.** PROVE uses 6,500 human-labelled rollouts and reports 96.83% agreement, versus 69.09% for native evaluation; agreement is not task success. WorldPhy supplies 300 tasks and 12,000 annotations around physical constraints. SealedLoop separates self-improvement from evaluation with a held-out judge. Together these support collecting explicit success criteria, failure labels and adjudicated examples alongside footage. [PROVE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33733) · [WorldPhy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34130) · [SealedLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33794)

**Interface quality can dominate model changes.** ActRosetta repairs action-interface mismatches and reports 42% lower visual prediction error, without establishing improved control. DTUBench shows severe degradation when manipulated objects change across 13 VLA configurations in LIBERO. These are evaluation findings, not evidence that a particular production fleet fails. [ActRosetta](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34739) · [DTUBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34538)
