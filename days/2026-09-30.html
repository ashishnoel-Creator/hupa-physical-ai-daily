    Data collection

    September 28: DexRoam links ego capture to mobile bimanual manipulation. A consumer VR headset plus head-mounted stereo camera captures continuous whole-body demonstrations without external trackers. Embodiment, action-semantic and temporal alignment map them to robot actions. Reported real-world average success rises from 29% to 56% with GR00T N1.7, and 32% to 57% with pi0.5; human-augmented training matches robot-only training using half the robot demonstrations. These are the authors’ task-specific experiments, not a universal replacement ratio for robot data. Preprint

    September 28, revised September 29: UVTA synchronises vision, touch and hand motion. Its tactile capture system supports co-training human and robot demonstrations with aligned action/tactile representations. Across five real-robot tasks, authors report 70% average success versus 29% for their strongest visual-tactile baseline. The abstract’s wording on human-demo totals differs from its per-task scaling statement, so no aggregate collection volume is inferred here. Preprint

    September 25, missed catch-up: VisTacAlign aligns the capture modalities themselves. Human motion is retargeted to a 17-DoF robot hand; stereo images are edited and reconstructed so human-derived point clouds share robot-view errors and visibility. A capacitive glove supplies aligned finger-force signals. Three real tasks test Lego assembly, strawberry picking and drill use; the abstract reports improvement without a single aggregate numerical gain. Preprint

    September 20, older catch-up: HEARTH adds measured temperature. Its 90 physical objects across 18 categories provide 145 captured states, calibrated RGB/thermal/3D assets and raw temperature measurements. In three LIBERO-derived tasks with 1,200 demonstrations, thermal observations raise temperature-dependent object-selection success from 35% to 75%. This is a simulation-oriented dataset study, not a real-workplace safety certification. Dataset paper

    Data annotation & validation

    September 28: MonoEgo makes invalid capture explicit. One 90-FPS global-shutter camera observes passive wrist markers and workstation anchors. Offline reconstruction retains map provenance and validity, leaving unsupported motion missing. The authors explicitly identify dynamic accuracy, deployment and downstream policy benefit as unresolved. The useful specification lesson is to preserve missing-data flags rather than silently interpolate trustworthy-looking trajectories. Paper

    September 27: NAVSIM label generation can erase useful distinctions. A study re-runs the released GTRS-Dense generator without its human-reference forgiveness overwrite. It finds changed supervision on 11,237 of 103,288 training scenes, about 10.9%. Improvements in lane-keeping discrimination coexist with lower adjacent-frame consistency in its fine-tuning experiment. This is a specific label-rule audit, not proof that every forgiveness rule is wrong. Paper

    September 27: VIDEAS converts demonstrations into explicit action preconditions and effects. It uses VLM extraction plus a text-based simulation check, including negative trajectories, to derive 34,000 samples from AgiBot-World. Its reported gains concern high-level action-semantic reasoning; that is distinct from measured robot execution reliability. Paper

    Research papers worth a look

    The seven collection/validation papers, GAE and DexWeave above are joined by eight further studies below, plus a reference back to HEARTH. Dates are original submissions, not discovery dates; results remain author-reported.

    Submitted Paper What it adds and what remains limited
    September 28 EMPIRIC Learns missing physics mechanisms as residual code on a physics engine. Five simulated domains and one physical wind/domino task test experiment-driven planning.
    September 28 When World Models Lie Calibrates a latent safety filter against observed prediction errors; simulation and hardware tests reduce failures. Its uncertainty-coverage guarantee is not a blanket safety guarantee.
    September 28 CF-WAM Varies the predicted future’s visual, semantic, geometric and interaction projections. Reports 82.50% RoboCasa-GR1 and 82.65% LIBERO-Plus success, plus up to 84% in its real experiments. Benchmarks differ from other papers here.
    September 28 P2P-T Object-centric pose priors support tool learning from human demonstrations without paired human/robot alignment data. The abstract’s 73% improvement is relative, not a 73-point increase or absolute success rate.
    September 27 CodeActionBench 25 manipulation tasks, nine configurations and 675 attempts. Success spans 2.7–73.3%; solving 22 tasks at least once is distinct from per-attempt reliability.
    September 25 Praxis whole-body manipulation One human demonstration per skill, with online visual/tactile correction across five tasks. This research title does not establish affiliation with the Praxis collection company.
    September 24, older catch-up Whole-hand force regulation Geometric contacts and proprioception support grasp maintenance/recovery on a 27-DoF system. Simulation comparisons omit gravity; avoid generalising their conditions.
    September 24, older catch-up Representation World Model Constructs latent paths and recovers actions through inverse dynamics, avoiding recursive rollout/search. Continuous-control results are not a production reliability claim.
    September 20, older catch-up HEARTH RGB/thermal/3D dataset discussed above; listed once in the library despite appearing in two sections.
