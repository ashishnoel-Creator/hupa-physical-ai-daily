### Data collection

**November 2 — Chef Robotics' training pipeline.** The company says its deployed robots have accumulated **150 million pick experiments**. Each useful training example connects measured pre-pick depth, the action, actual scale weight and post-pick depth. Its account describes content-addressed computation, exact data/code provenance and retryable processing; a rebuild uses more than ten times the previous training corpus. These are company-reported scale and infrastructure claims, not independent accuracy results. **Interpretation:** outcome measurements and reproducible processing can be part of the collection product, alongside images. [Technical article](https://www.chefrobotics.ai/post/building-a-world-model-for-food-manipulation-part-2-the-training-flywheel).

It claims human-only training and cross-embodiment policy transfer. Treat that as a company demonstration, not independently established generalization; no commissioned collection opening was verified. ### Data annotation & validation

**October 29 — offline scores are not enough.** A long-horizon dexterity study evaluates 281 policies across roughly 1,700 physical RJ45-connector trials. Only five of 67 tested offline proxies correlate within one policy family. The result is task-specific, but it supports retaining physical evaluation and separating representation changes from apparent dataset-size gains. [Study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40828).

**October 30 — explicit negative and recovery tests.** [MolmoSpaces CommonSense](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41377) stresses clutter, occlusion and object choice, including failures of model-based judges. [VLAs Do Not Know When Not to Act](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41609) finds that six evaluated checkpoints still act under empty instructions in a simulated benchmark. Neither establishes a field-wide failure rate. Both motivate annotating wrong-object actions, no-action conditions and recovery outcomes rather than scoring plausible-looking motion alone.

The broader annotation-company search did not yield another independently verified fresh release suitable for this edition. This is selective coverage, not a claim that those vendors were inactive.
