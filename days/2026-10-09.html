    Data collection

    Human-video scale comes with heterogeneous supervision. Being-M0.7, October 8, uses over 10,000 hours mixing video-only, motion-only and paired human data, followed by robot mid-training. VioLA, October 8, uses 140.6M frames, 93.2% human, and a shared body/hand action representation. These are author-reported training compositions, not open purchase requests; VioLA’s code/checkpoints are prospective.

    Contact and calibration matter. TACROSS, October 8, studies a $10.86 prototype glove with 285 sensing points. That is a component/prototype cost, not a commercially supplied kit price. Human contact events supplement robot demonstrations, which retain ground-truth actions. The promised 150+hour dataset is not verified released. YOCO calibrates operator/session glove bias. Together, these support treating operator and device calibration as collection metadata, not assuming all human streams are interchangeable.

    Repeat the same action as well as collecting new tasks. Same Action, Different Outcome, October 7, synchronizes 269 cloth rollouts with stereo, optical motion capture and robot logs; four calibrated simulators still miss real variability. This is evidence for repeated-trial coverage, not a general simulator ranking.

    Data annotation & validation

    DreamTrue, October 8: human-annotated video defects and counterfactual post-training address physical inconsistencies in world models. Reported human-assessed defect prevalence drops 48.12%→6.25%; do not read that as robot-policy failure rate. Paper

    ManiUnit, October 8: 137,899 segments, 21 skills and 417 subtasks from 50 BEHAVIOR-1K activities support evaluation from intermediate states. This makes skill diagnosis more specific; strong local skill scores still do not imply strong end-to-end completion. Paper

    Task Scrubbing, October 7, tests visual shortcuts such as background/camera cues. RoboAware, October 8, uses counterfactual branches from restored simulated states to assess executable capabilities. Both strengthen the case for distribution and failure audits alongside label correctness. No additional verified physical-data vendor tender emerged from annotation-company searches; generic agent products were excluded.

    Embedded AI systems

    OMNIVISION OG05D, October 5 catch-up. Official specifications describe a 5MP, 2.2µm global-shutter sensor with three-exposure HDR at 60 FPS. Linear modes reach 130 FPS with cryptographic operation or 146 FPS without it. Sampling is current; mass production is planned for Q4 2026. Sensor specifications do not establish a complete calibrated recording rig. Product page

    Compute results need their hardware context. WAMCache reduces prefill FLOPs 32–42% with a reported 2.5-point real-task success cost. LiteNWM reports 128× speedup against its stated baseline on RTX5090. Neither figure establishes Jetson latency or battery-powered collection performance.

    NavGPT-3, October 7, separates reasoning, acting and monitoring. Human-comparable RxR-CE scores are benchmark-specific; 0.5–1s reactions are not hard realtime control. These results show why demos, skill metrics and deployed task completion need separate reporting.

    World models & sim-to-real

    AWS Physical AI Toolchain, October 7 catch-up. Modular reference architectures and automation connect NVIDIA GR00T, Isaac Sim/Lab, Cosmos and OSMO with AWS training, storage and edge services. The workflow explicitly converts raw Zarr to LeRobot and passes robot assets/policies across URDF, ONNX and ROS2 stages. Its supplied 27 UR3 pick-and-place episodes are a worked example; users bring their own production data. Official technical walkthrough

    SimVLA, MiniWAM, Balanced Data Diet and DexOne2Many, all October 8, study synthetic training, compact future targets, adaptive reset curricula and one-video-to-simulation transfer. Their experiments justify closer examination, not a claim that physical collection is unnecessary. USDCraft also illustrates a QA opportunity: generated articulated simulation assets need physical and geometric checks.
